# Reuse, attribution, and rights boundaries

**Status:** No repository-wide `LICENSE` file was found at the 11 November 2026 reorganization checkpoint. **This document does not grant any new license or permission.**

Public readability is not permission to reproduce the text, algorithms, formalizations, or figures under an invented blanket license. Different materials may have different underlying rights and attribution obligations:

- Original authored mathematical manuscripts and code: the owner must choose an explicit license and approved terms for each rights class before reuse is represented as authorized.
- Imported mathematics: cite theorems and actual authors. In particular, Turturean's original Boolean-cube proof and its Lean components must not be represented as NIGHTFALL-originated.
- Upstream code and third-party datasets: preserve their specific licenses and provenance. An overall source license cannot supersede upstream restrictions.
- AI-assisted drafts, implementations, and source audits: credit substantial documented contributions accurately, without implying an AI instance is a human independent referee.
- Archived binary and historical files: do not publish a master source bundle without the separate disclosure and checksum review described in [publication scope](nightfall/PUBLICATION_SCOPE.md).

[CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) provides archive-level citation metadata, not permission to reuse. An eventual license choice requires an owner decision; this reorganization does **not** pick MIT, Apache, Creative Commons, or another license unilaterally.

A reviewer can quote or reference the repository within applicable law and venue policies, but should confirm permissions before redistributing substantial source or figures. Please report licensing/provenance errors as [source corrections](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/issues/new/choose).
