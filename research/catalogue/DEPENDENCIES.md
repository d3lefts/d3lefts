## Paper A/B/C are different promise classes, not competing discovery certificates

- **Paper A** gives exact recovery for bounded-degree finite-range observables by whole-coordinate reads. It does not require Paper B's approximate risk argument.
- **Paper B** supplies general bounded Hilbert prediction at a spectral tail plus tolerance, assuming the written finite-to-measurable lift and posterior potential; its kernel foundation is the finite scalar theorem, not the unproved exponent-two quadratic route.
- **Paper C** is a *model-indexed synthesis*: certificate geometry sharpens Boolean whole-coordinate selection; disjoint-output structure sharpens Paper B's rank accounting; the F16 cube-power sensing oracle changes what a query measures. Its fixed and adaptive finite phase codes have exact replay, but the 27-state adaptive Lean declaration is on a **closed, unmerged** source branch. The quadratic exponent-two rate remains conditional. These are distinct evidence tracks, not a newly certified fourth universal theorem.

## Three mathematical stop rules

1. The finite scalar influence theorem does **not** certify the arbitrary-measurable-product, Hilbert-rank or adaptive posterior chain. The weak `5/2` exponent and exact formal coefficient may be used only under the formal statement's hypotheses.
2. Neither the squared-variance degree-two influence theorem nor its `Theta(r/eta)` observation consequences are end-to-end formally or independently refereed. The stronger quadratic constant is **not** imported from the finite `5/2` Lean theorem.
3. The restricted cubic covered-block result in PR #335 depends on the quadratic analytic theorem; PR #381 reuses that core-removal mechanism and adds higher-degree scope, while its sharp top-Hoeffding-level projection is a separate claim. Count the mechanism once. The unrestricted cubic exponent-two conjecture remains open. Successful exact examples and refutations of stronger proof shortcuts are not counterexamples or proofs of the target.

## How to audit a dependency

For each edge record: source declaration/proof section, assumptions transferred, exact coefficient and scaling, transformation/equality used, known counterexamples, reviewer identity (if authorized), and a pinned source revision. A failure in an imported premise must demote downstream status until repaired. Do not infer peer review from workflow color; preserve non-author review as a separate field.
