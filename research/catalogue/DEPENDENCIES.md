## Three mathematical stop rules

1. The finite scalar influence theorem does **not** certify the arbitrary-measurable-product, Hilbert-rank or adaptive posterior chain. The weak `5/2` exponent and exact formal coefficient may be used only under the formal statement's hypotheses.
2. Neither the squared-variance degree-two influence theorem nor its `Theta(r/eta)` observation consequences are end-to-end formally or independently refereed. The stronger quadratic constant is **not** imported from the finite `5/2` Lean theorem.
3. The restricted cubic covered-block result depends on the proposed degree-two theorem. The unrestricted cubic exponent-two conjecture remains open. Successful exact examples and refutations of stronger proof shortcuts are not counterexamples or proofs of the target.

## How to audit a dependency

For each edge record: source declaration/proof section, assumptions transferred, exact coefficient and scaling, transformation/equality used, known counterexamples, reviewer identity (if authorized), and a pinned source revision. A failure in an imported premise must demote downstream status until repaired. Do not infer peer review from workflow color; preserve non-author review as a separate field.
