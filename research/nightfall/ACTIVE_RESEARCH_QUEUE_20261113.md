## Source and certification policy

1. **Mathematical truth before queue size.** A green Python CI check certifies precisely its finite checker cases; a green Lean CI check certifies only its exact printed declaration, axioms, and pinned imports; neither establishes worldwide originality or peer review.
2. **No clone merging.** For any repeated Lean file across branches, compare Git blob SHAs, original theorem statement, full dependencies, and active-toolchain replay. Extract only non-duplicated, verified additions into a clean PR; keep historical superseded PR URLs and review discussions.
3. **No unreviewed mass closure.** Leave other instances' branches and working drafts intact until their claims and unique content have been accounted for. An obsolete PR may be closed later with an explicit 'superseded by' and preservation path, never silent deletion.

Move to source-preserving operators rather than repeating the same counterexamples.
5. **Separate targets.** Q2 is an original-source finite-product *influence inequality*; #450 is a specific matched-oracle *sequential task-revelation complexity law*; #449 is a *conditional posterior observer*. Similar titles and shared vocabulary do not create theorem inheritance.
