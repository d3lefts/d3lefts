# Manuscript insertion — Task-Relevant Distinctions and the Engineering Cost of Justification

**Proposed placement:** immediately after the dissertation's Chapter 1 typed information-path definition, followed by a methods bridge in its systems-engineering/lifecycle-assurance chapter. **Research status:** editorial synthesis and one new-in-program exact elementary maintenance lemma, not a claimed world-original unifying mathematical theorem. Preserve the historical 70-page archive, source passports and independent claim statuses.

## Revised abstract paragraph

Complex systems can require exponentially many bits to describe yet only a small, task-specific set of distinctions to reach a valid decision. That observation does not by itself make knowledge cheap: the required distinctions must be available through the system's permitted observations, the policy must be computable at stated cost, and its output must be independently justified. This dissertation therefore studies a **typed information path** from admissible hidden systems through sensors and model oracles to a decision and a verifier, with separate invoices for state observations, model evaluations, arithmetic, certificate checking and revalidation after change. Exact finite permutation and additive-combinatorial certificates demonstrate when incomplete representations suffice for universal conclusions; an extremal-overlap witness exhibits numerical all-constraint verification and sparse exact recertification; product-space and posterior-influence theorems identify stochastic coordinate information but reveal a cost gap between existence and discovery; and quantum information geometry supplies a contrasting noncommutative information metric. The mathematical goal is not to assert that these objects obey one universal law, but to establish a new, oracle-matched **joint upper/lower bound** for affordable task-specific observation with maintained, independently checkable evidence in a structurally defined class of engineered systems.

## Technical statement of the motivating hypothesis

Let Theta be the feasible model/trajectory space. A task T and an information interface O jointly determine which distinctions among Theta matter and which can be exposed. In an exact deterministic version, two states x,y with T(x)!=T(y) must become distinguishable by a sufficient observation transcript; pairs with T(x)=T(y) need not be distinguished. For noisy, stochastic or quantum tasks this pairwise equivalence becomes a risk/distinguishability requirement, not mere equality of the observed record.

Write an information path as pi=(a1,y1,...,aq,yq,decision,certificate). Its costs are a resource vector, not a single unsupported scalar:

    C(pi) = (physical_reads, model_calls, simulation_samples,
             arithmetic_ops, verifier_work, revalidation_work).

The feasible Pareto frontier is determined by the task loss, false-claim tolerance, allowed oracle, chronology and authenticated model version. A certificate is accepted only by its named checker for its exact quantifiers. Under change Delta, previously checked lemmas may be reused only when all applicable source assumptions and dependent facts remain valid.

This viewpoint does not invent the notion of sufficiency, data processing, CEGAR, proof-carrying code, or incremental verification. Its potential original contribution lies in quantitative results under the SAME interface and with explicit lifecycle cost.

## A comparative experiment across established findings

**Experiment A: combinatorial universal truth from incomplete ordering.** The four-shuffle k=4 threshold is proved through 5040 standardized length-seven states and 302 candidate length-20 avoidance paths. The forcing certificate uses only locally known pairwise comparisons, never imputes unspecified distant order. Compare the number of queried distinctions and certificate size against brute full-permutation enumeration. The exact theorem was independently finite-replayed, but historical priority is not yet adjudicated. The systems lesson is **completion-robust certification**, not a generic sampling bound.

**Experiment B: necessary evidence rather than sufficient compression.** In the explicit Sidon 30-point construction the AP hypergraph has tau(H)=13 and tau(H-e)=12 for all its 28 edges. Therefore every edge is indispensable to the 13-cover statement **when the evidence interface consists only of a subset of these hyperedge constraints**. Indeed, if some edge e is absent, the known 12-cover of H-e also covers the available constraint subset. This gives a matching logical evidence-size lower bound of all 28 constraints under the edge-list contract. A source that exposes the underlying integers may infer unqueried AP edges, so that stronger interface is excluded. This is an exact contrast to Experiment A: some tasks have irreducible evidence even after representation compression.

**Experiment C: all-constraint numerical proof with local changes.** The Erdős minimum-overlap upper construction is certified by an integer-height source witness, 2N-1 signed-lag scores, and an exact grid-to-continuum bridge. Sparse source edits affect a signed-lag numerator through two linear terms and one quadratic term. This yields O(mN) exact arithmetic to update the **full** proof of an N-cell witness after m changed cells, and an explicit 2E peak-gap sufficient certificate for preserving the previously winning lag. Compare this to naive O(N²) direct score rebuilding and to an optimized NTT baseline O(N log N); the sparse method is advantageous only in the appropriate m/N regime and still requires authenticated old scores. The certified upper construction does not establish the unknown global minimum.

**Experiment D: stochastic prediction without hidden influence oracles.** For a known independent product source, ANOVA energies give exact conditional observation potentials; a lower bound on maximum influence can turn into a state-read complexity bound. But the degree-two heterogeneous exponent-two law remains author-analytic and externally unreviewed; the historically Lean-compiled all-degree transfer inherits Turturean. More importantly, actually computing which coordinate to read costs model observations and arithmetic. The dissertation must report a matched oracle cost, not just a conditional bound assuming a free influence selector.

**Experiment E: epistemic versus actionable uncertainty.** Given offline model evidence D, the total posterior-model coordinate influence decomposes as the influence of the posterior mean plus the posterior disagreement energy. A model whose posterior mean is zero may have positive model influence, so choosing sensors by the wrong statistic wastes measurements. An explicit two-independent-posterior cross estimator addresses the signal, but its estimation variance can remain positive when actionable influence is zero. Any expected observation-cost guarantee must charge this source of uncertainty.

**Experiment F: physical versus abstract observability.** In an independent-increment tree, correlated physical states may hide a simple independent-source representation. However, original vertex reads reveal relative path products and need not recover individual increments; nonadjacent endpoint reads on a two-edge path reduce Bayes risk from 3/8 to 9/40. An exact special-case binary-path dynamic program is therefore not a generic optimal-observation discovery; Krause–Guestrin studied optimal sensing on chains and related models long before this work. Compare all results under the original physical sensor interface.

**Experiment G: quantum geometry changes the information price.** For product-prepared qubit ensemble states, the SLD/Jordan inverse tensor comparison has an exact condition-number-dependent coefficient, with an author-derived ternary source that attains it. Under local visibility assumptions, conversion of local observation directions to global measurement gain can suffer an exponential degree penalty. The relevant object is not a Boolean coordinate-test partition; it is an operator metric. Only a new theorem that accounts for this metric can transfer the stochastic chapters into quantum measurement.

**Experiment H: bounded-change proof maintenance.** Under explicit authenticated tree-convex evidence dependencies, the change-stable safety programme identifies exact certificates and recheck costs. Deleting or altering one constraint can invalidate a proof even if the final yes/no decision remains unchanged; source authenticity and correct dependency declarations are prior obligations. Reuse only proof nodes whose assumptions are proven stable. Compare against FASE 2026 analysis-dependency slicing to avoid claiming the general incremental principle as new.

## Dissertation decision criterion

The signature theorem must do more than supply a new name for task-specific sufficiency. It must establish a family and oracle for which all of the following are proved: an algorithm to select and compute observations; a same-oracle lower bound; a bound for checking the ensuing claim; and a bound for maintaining that guarantee across authenticated system changes. Report the **resource Pareto boundary** and all required structural parameters. If no such theorem can be obtained, the portfolio supports multiple interesting papers and a research programme, but not a single claimed universal breakthrough.
