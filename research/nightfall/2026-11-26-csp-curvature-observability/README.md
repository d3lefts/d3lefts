## 1. Exact relative-area observation criterion
Let a_i be labeled eigenvalues (including distinct state labels at degenerate eigenvalues) in a finite common representation. The known transition support graph has edge i→j only when an admissible nonzero matrix element can be identified and its signed boost frequency resolved. By (4.12) the frequency, times kappa=8 pi G hbar, is a_j-a_i. Set B equal to the oriented incidence matrix. Data y=B a. Then ker B consists exactly of potentials constant on each connected component. A target c^T a is identifiable iff each component sum of c is zero. Proof: annihilate ker B; equivalently c=B^T u and c^T a=u^T y. With Euclidean deterministic additive noise ||e||<=epsilon, arbitrary a in R^N and exact feasible target, minimax error is epsilon ||(B^dagger)^T c||, by the two-point argument on a=±B^dagger e and minimum-norm estimator. Positivity alone does not eliminate the obstruction for absolute targets (shift all eigenvalues sufficiently positive). In a bounded prior model the minimax formula need not be sharp.
This reduces the need for an absolute controlled-unitary phase reference, not for signed transitions, sector labels, known preparation weights, matched frames, and common Hilbert/charge representation. Automorphisms alone are invariant under A→A+bI; with independent cut-specific shifts b(r), even metric scale can be arbitrary. No assumption that cross-cut coherence is physically available. Source §3.6 simultaneous boost cancellation is on matched opposite sides of the SAME corner, not a proof that different cuts share the same scalar offset.
If a common scalar offset is physically justified across a three-radius family and M(r)=b+q r²+c r⁴+O(r⁶), samples at r,2r,3r yield q_hat=(-13M(r)/24+2M(2r)/3-M(3r)/8)/r² and c_hat=(M(r)/24-M(2r)/15+M(3r)/40)/r⁴. Exact coefficient cancellation and noise bounds (4/3)epsilon/r² and (2/15)epsilon/r⁴ for per-sample errors <=epsilon. Remainder weights are absolute sums; q bias O(r⁴), c bias O(r²). Odd powers invalidate the stated orders unless removed or separately bounded. Not a source theorem that this common offset exists.

## 2. Directional Ricci spectral tomography
For a smooth 4D Lorentzian metric with signature (-+++), choose a unit timelike frame at p and k=(1,n), |n|=1. Let r be the affine parameter with that normalization. Before caustics use parallel screen Jacobi matrix J with J''=-T J, J(0)=0,J'(0)=I; trace T=Ric(k,k). Area density j=det J positive near the vertex. Classical expansion j/r²=1-r² Ric(k,k)/6+O(r³) uniformly on directions with sufficient regularity.
Let A_f(r)=integral f(n) j(r,n) dOmega. The candidate quantum interface is an admissible smeared area generator and phase-resolved expectation Gamma_f(eta,r)=omega(exp(i eta Ahat_f/kappa)). Sufficient correspondence is E[Ahat_f]-A_f=o(r⁴) in a controlled classical scaling limit (and readout error o(r⁴) in area units). Merely o(r²), sufficient for conformal scale, is not sufficient for curvature. Assume access to signed smooth weights, or differences of positive weights. Then the nine angular moments of F(n)=Ric(k,k) are obtained as lim 6/r⁴ * (r² integral f - E[Ahat_f]); divide by 4pi for normalized spherical averages. Define a=R00+tr(R_spatial)/3, b_i=R0i, C_ij=Rij-delta_ij tr(R_spatial)/3. F=a+2 b·n+n^T C n. Spherical averages give a=<F>, b_i=(3/2)<n_i F>, C_ij=(15/2)<(n_i n_j-delta_ij/3)F>. By symmetry and normalized moments E[n_i n_j]=delta_ij/3 and E[n_i n_j n_k n_l]=(delta_ij delta_kl+delta_ik delta_jl+delta_il delta_jk)/15. The kernel on all symmetric tensors is exactly span(g); the target has dimension nine and nine independent scalar linear moments suffice and are necessary for uniform exact linear recovery on this space. These are local frame-dependent coordinates of a covariant quotient. This is a characterization, not a derivation of gravitational field equations.
If all nine vanish at all points of a connected smooth region, Ric=Lambda(x)g; contracted Bianchi yields dLambda=0. With independent conserved matter stress tensor T, the analogous constraint on Ric-8pi G T gives Einstein equations with constant integration Lambda (trace adjustment by Bianchi). Vacuum source does not supply a quantum matter extension. ## 3. Vacuum Weyl test
Taylor in a parallel screen: J/r=I-r² T0/6-r³ T1/12+r⁴(T0²/120-T2/40)+O(r⁵), where T_i denotes i-th affine derivative at 0. In a Ricci-flat neighborhood tr T and its derivatives vanish. For two screen dimensions det(I+E)=1+tr E+det E, det T0=-tr(T0²)/2. Thus j/r²=1-r⁴ tr(T0²)/180+O(r⁵). For constant diag(w,-w), w>0, the exact factor is sin(sqrt(w)r)sinh(sqrt(w)r)/(wr²), giving -w²r⁴/90. Curvature squared response requires absolute area correspondence/readout o(r⁶), a stronger gate than Ricci. Scalar area cannot recover full polarization (T and -T have identical constant diagonal area responses); anisotropic screen observables are required. This is not a distinct prediction versus GR; it is a test of the proposed spectral interface.
