## Independent obstruction: reflection-blind prefix
**Lemma (all dimensions n≥3).** Let T:R^n→R^k be continuous and even, and suppose for a fixed unit vector u that T(x)=T(R_u x) for all x, where R_u x=x-2(u·x)u. For every continuous even scalar last reading f:R^n→R, the pair (T,f) is not injective modulo global sign. Indeed define D(v)=f(u+v)-f(u-v) on S(u⊥)≈S^(n-2). Reflection invariance and evenness give T(u+v)=T(u-v). D is continuous and D(-v)=-D(v). Since n≥3 the sphere is path-connected, some v has D(v)=0 (intermediate value along v to -v). The two signals u±v are not equal up to sign and have projective Euclidean separation min(||(u+v)-(u-v)||,||(u+v)+(u-v)||)=2. This holds for **arbitrary continuous final scalar**, stronger than a rank-one final detector.

For n=4, if the first five causal readings never couple x3 (i.e. all five outputs are invariant under x3 sign reversal), no sixth continuous reading can rescue universal recovery. This independently reproduces and generalizes the failure mechanism reported in #1047 S4. It does **not** rule out all rank-degenerate prefixes: a viable six-reading architecture must violate this reflection invariance before its final reading. The lemma is elementary and not asserted novel.
