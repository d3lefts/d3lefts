## Status hierarchy

- **[K] Known external result:** literature establishing background. Attribute it; never promote it to NIGHTFALL discovery.
- **[E] Exact elementary result / source derivation:** proof written with all quantifiers and independently falsifiable, but no independent referee claim.
- **[F] Finite test:** computational evidence only, with inputs/source and counts; cannot prove unrestricted theorem.
- **[L] Lean-scoped:** named declaration compiled at exact old/new toolchain/source receipt; scope bounded by printed theorem and imports. A historical receipt is not a current-pin replay.
- **[A] Author-side analytic candidate:** a written proof judged plausible internally; material missing audit or unresolved bridge.
- **[O] Open:** target, gap, conjecture or design idea. No result promotion.
- **[H] Human-independent mathematical review:** **none reported for the terminal central claims**.
- **[N] New original contribution:** **not attained merely by any entry above**. Requires dated prior-art and same-hypotheses comparison.

## Current independent facts vs proposed bridge

The most concrete facts are **not** a newly invented “law of knowing.” They are (i) exact typed examples whose different tasks have different query costs; (ii) standard orthogonal source representation machinery; and (iii) scoped kernel-checked building blocks for the quadratic program. These become a major contribution only if the missing arrows are proved or if an original lower/upper conversion theorem survives aggressive comparison to earlier fields.

## Research contract (pre-registration for next decisive cycle)

**Objective:** certify one genuine original quantitative conversion theorem for a fixed task and access signature, or produce a rigorous counterexample/obstruction that forces a narrower scope.

**Feasible set:** finite independent heterogeneous product alphabets (zero-weight atoms explicitly admitted), bounded scalar Hoeffding-degree-two \(f\), exact coordinate-refresh conditional expectations, separately priced model/state query oracles. Later measurable/Hilbert extensions are distinct gates.

**Strongest checked external references this session:** Packel–Traub 1987 on partial-information complexity; Torgersen's comparison-of-experiments/deficiency framework; Golovin–Krause 2011 on adaptive submodularity; Hébert–Woodford 2017 on sequential costly information; Fagin/Halpern epistemic work on logical omniscience. These are substantive neighbors, not novelty confirmation.

**Best construction:** exact addressed family C09–C11 (reusable, not historically new). **Best technical upper:** C06 analytical candidate, not terminal certified. **Remaining gap:** source-only matching/ownership-to-independent-neighbor moment and full mainline 4.36 same-pin terminal theorem, plus proof of non-reducibility to existing statistical experiment/active-learning bounds.

**Novelty gate:** exact theorem statement absent or materially strengthened relative to existing work under the same oracle, accuracy, task and hypotheses; a new definition, renamed quantity, or mixture of known theories is insufficient. **Significance gate:** quantitative improvement, sharp separating example not subsumed by known models, or a reusable theorem that composes several former separate access arguments.

**Audit:** independent adversarial reconstruction of quantifiers and energy identities **before** reading the candidate proof; exact checks of boundary/zero-atom cases; separate old-pin/new-pin source identity audit; literature counter-reductions; referee review beyond AI instances. No claim of independent human review until it occurs.

**Certificate/checker:** named final Lean declaration with all source hypotheses and only foundational axioms; exact source SHA, pinned compiler/Mathlib commit, CI run, theorem-type contract, independent checker run, full dependency graph and dated prior-art report. Existing \`scripts/lean_bridge.py\` is a compilation/receipt tool, not a global novelty checker.

**Budget and stop rule:** one finite Q2 terminal proof attempt plus two independent proof-attack routes; if repeated attempts require inserting desired influence/energy budgets as premises, stop polishing and change representation. In parallel, run the cheapest addressed-family counter-reductions and exact source graph checks. Do not submit or publish without human authorization.

## Mandatory demotion rules

1. A newly discovered source hypothesis or counterexample demotes all descendants until rechecked.
2. A successful old-pin source hash does not establish same-pin 4.36 recompilation.
3. A manuscript label (“proved”, “certified”, “new”) cannot outrun its most restrictive dependency.
4. Exact tests do not become theorems by increasing sample count.
5. Independent AI agreement does not count as human non-author refereeing.
6. Contradictory constants/versions remain archived with supersession reasons; never silently overwrite.
