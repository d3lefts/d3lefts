## Central thesis: two coupled systems, one evidence discipline

NIGHTFALL examined the minimum information, computation, and verification required for justified decisions. Simultaneously, its own human-directed, AI-assisted research workflow evolved into a complex, changing, interconnected *evidence production and maintenance system*. The research object and the research process are distinct but can be compared under common **typed** observation, task, provenance, and recertification interfaces.

**Physical/engineered level:** Authorized mission and authenticated configuration metadata jointly determine which component distinctions must be observed to certify safe action.  
**Research/metascientific level:** A human-approved mission, AI-generated candidate representations, independently checkable evidence, and versioned claim dependencies determine which scientific distinctions must be tested and certified.  
**Critical exclusion:** This conceptual cross-level correspondence does **not** mean the actual human and AI signals in NIGHTFALL have zero individual mutual information, nor does it establish a causal human–AI collaboration gain. Those are new empirical hypotheses, not consequences of the finite theorems.

The unified doctoral question is:

> Under which explicit information, authority, oracle, dependency, and change contracts can the joint availability of individually insufficient inputs reduce the *total lifecycle cost of justified action or justified scientific inference*, with matched lower bounds, reproducible certificates, and quantified failure under noise, stale versions, and interconnection?

## B. Who contributed which function

This is a functional and provenance description; it is not an empirical comparison of individual capacities.

**Human authority (Wright):** set and revised research missions; selected success and novelty thresholds; required exactness and hostile prior-art checks; rejected sub-threshold polishing and repeated obstruction loops; chose systems engineering as the thesis and graduate-application direction; retained authority over publication/submission, external claims, and consequential choices.

**AI construction and synthesis:** searched alternate representations and literatures; proposed algorithms, numerical constructions, proofs and counterexample searches; wrote code, draft manuscripts and Lean candidates; synthesized cross-domain invariant and transfer proposals; performed repeated source and proof-status audits. Outputs varied in quality and sometimes overstated completion, novelty, or certification.

**Tool-mediated evidence:** finite integer/rational checkers, independent reimplementations where available, Lean kernel checking of named declarations under pinned toolchains, CI, source/version hashes, and public prior art. These are *different* kinds of evidence and must not be merged into a single status flag.

**External challenge:** true independent scholarly review, physical engineering tests, and controlled human–AI performance experiments are mostly pending. Multiple AI agents do not automatically supply statistical, epistemic, or institutional independence.

This architecture is not the claim that the human knows the answer or the AI knows the answer. The stronger hypothesis is that separate sources of mission authority, constructive search, and reliable evidence can reduce the cost of *finding and certifying the right distinction*. That hypothesis remains to be tested with fair ablations.

## C. Two finite mathematical foundations — retain their types

**F1: row/column assurance complementarity.** A mission selects row S_t in an r-by-r component grid; authenticated scope selects column J_j; old certificates outside the change scope are valid. Exact, individually addressable component reads take place before any withheld metadata is revealed, and the zero-error all-safe verifier must reject any admissible one-fault unsafe world. Reads required with neither, mission only, scope only, and both metadata values are respectively r², r, r, 1. This is operational joint savings, **not pure positive cost synergy**: for V(S)=C(empty)-C(S), the interaction V(T,A)-V(T)-V(A)=-(r-1)².

**F2: pure modular routing.** Independent uniform T,A∈Z_r select K=T+A (mod r). Each alone is statistically uninformative about K, I(K;T)=I(K;A)=0, but I(K;T,A)=log₂r; exact-read costs with neither, either, and both are r,r,r,1. The operational savings interaction is +(r-1). XOR/Latin-square synergy is classical, not a claim of originality. Previously reported finite checks: 25 row/column contexts and 131 modular contexts plus forged-manifest counterexample; see the frozen v0.2 package and v0.3 contract. These are not independent Lean proofs.

**v0.3 analytic extensions:** (N1) With additive context noises U,V independent of uniform T,A, the joint posterior is controlled by W=U+V and I(K;T+U,A+V)=log₂r-H(W). Correlated canceling errors may preserve exact routing, while full support destroys zero-error panel reduction. (N2) For a finite dependency/invalidation graph, mission-relevant potentially stale nodes have conservative closure R=M∩C_G(Δ), or the union over uncertain authenticated contexts; |R| is an exact safe-path read cost only if independent single-node fault alternatives are admissible and reads are exact unit-cost. (N3) With a stable version window L that resets evidence on change, certification is feasible exactly when L≥|R| under N2's strict assumptions. The v0.3 exact-rational checker reports 18 noisy models, 174 contexts, 4,096 graph instances and 65,536 panels; no original-source Lean proof or engineering deployment has been established.

## D. The new meta-bridge: research claims as a changing assurance graph

Let G_E be a directed acyclic dependency graph with nodes for assumptions, source datasets, theorem declarations, checkers, imported facts, numerical artifacts, authorship/review claims and scientific conclusions. Edges record *which evidence or condition is necessary for which downstream claim*. A changed or contradicted node invalidates a closure C_{G_E}(Δ). For a human-authorized set M of claims presently needed, the candidate revalidation target is

R_E=M∩C_{G_E}(Δ).

This is an **application of versioned dependency-cone bookkeeping**, not a new theorem. The proposed transfer from F1 is sound as an exact *minimum* cost theorem only when each affected claim can independently be false in a feasible alternative and each verification action checks exactly one named claim. Real proof DAGs often violate both assumptions: lemmas can logically imply other lemmas; one compiler call can validate many claims; model-generated provenance may be correlated; a source-level error can invalidate an entire graph cut.

Research direction: replace crude cardinality |R_E| with a **source-matched information-separator / verification-cover cost** that explicitly models batched checkers, shared assumptions, trusted source attribution, overlapping proof requirements, conditional independence, and mutable version stamps.

This is the nonmetaphorical bridge: both physical subsystems and research claims are *certificate-bearing objects that lose validity under a precisely scoped change*. Neither is certified merely because a model claims to have checked it.

## E. Cost accounting: what the human–AI system actually buys

For a fixed task, source contract, version and admissible oracle family, define the total cost of justified outcome:

C_total = C_mission/context + C_trusted_reads + C_model_calls + C_search/computation + C_formal_or_exact_check + C_human_review + C_version_revalidation + C_failure/recovery.

Do not compare arms that give one architecture a free optimal sensor, privileged oracle, known relevant coordinate, extra posterior draws, or hidden expert prior. Record wall-clock, monetary and compute cost separately when not meaningfully convertible.

For controlled two-source ablations, define C(∅),C(H),C(A),C(H,A), under the *same underlying task and certification contract*. Operational cost interaction is

Γ_C = C(H)+C(A)-C(H,A)-C(∅).

A positive value reflects **superadditive savings**, not necessarily pure Shannon synergy. Pure informational complementarity additionally requires the explicitly specified *task variable* K to satisfy I(K;H)=I(K;A)=0 and I(K;H,A)>0. This property is proven in F2 for uniform modular signals; **there is no evidence establishing it for Wright's mission decisions and ChatGPT outputs**. In a real scientific workflow, the inputs are dependent, endogenous and changed by previous interactions. Conditioning on prior shared history and representation choices is essential.

## F. Why this is not automatic human–AI superiority

A preregistered systematic review (Vaccaro, Almaatouq and Malone, Nature Human Behaviour, 2024) covered 106 experiments / 370 effect sizes and found average *negative* human–AI synergy relative to the stronger individual baseline; the process design and task are major moderators. Berente and colleagues' 2025 information-systems literature already formalizes complementary team performance and information/capability asymmetries; human–AI complementarity in the abstract is prior art. Our research target must be narrower: source-authenticated *which-distinction-to-verify* routing, exact and noisy sensing-cost bounds, and amortized certificate maintenance in dynamic interconnected systems.

Therefore NIGHTFALL's case study may motivate an experiment, but must not be marketed as causal evidence of collaborative superintelligence, pure information synergy, or human–AI performance superiority.

## G. Falsifiable next experiment — prove or reject the meta-analogy

**Objective:** Measure whether source-matched early delivery of human-authorized mission context and authenticated proof/engineering change scope creates more-than-additive reductions in *total cost of validated conclusions*, not just raw number of AI outputs.

**Test bed:** Freeze 16–32 archived NIGHTFALL claim-update tasks before allocating experimental arms; cover Erdős certificate updates, finite-field reconstruction, Q2 source-to-Lean bridges, posterior-actionable influence, and digital-twin change impact. Freeze versions of tasks, gold-source data, tools, candidate checkers, and maximum cost. Include wrong and misleading manifests, stale source artifacts, source-to-proof gaps, and hidden common-cause errors.

**Four strictly matched information arms:** (0) neither additional early routing/context nor detailed signed change scope, (H) early mission-relevant prioritization only, (A) authenticated scoped change metadata only, (H+A) both. Every arm has the *same basic authorized task*, oracle family, data, time, source rights, and mandatory evidence standard; withholding an optional early context is not permission to proceed without authority. Reveal withheld metadata only after evidence purchase in tasks designed to match the F1/F2 acquisition contract.

**At least two additional baselines:** AI-led and human-led procedures with the same task, permitted sources and price of verification; and, where measurable, a uniform full-recheck policy. These are required for claims about human–AI complementarity relative to individuals. Use paired task seeds, randomized assignment, multiple repeated runs and evaluators blind to architecture/labels.

**Primary outcomes:** (i) total resource cost per independently validated conclusion, not token count or draft count; (ii) false-acceptance rate under adversarial faulty/forged metadata; (iii) version-stale acceptance rate; (iv) amortized recertification cost across updates; (v) correct abstention when evidence is insufficient. Also log counterexample rate, time-to-detection, revision count, and reviewer burden.

**Pre-registered prediction:** On carefully constructed F2-style tasks, the pure-synergy read-cost pattern follows exactly by theorem. On real archived research tasks, positive Γ_C is a conjecture. Potential discriminators include complementary task hints that actually change the feasible set, correlated errors that erase trusted separation, and source authentication costs that consume all apparent acquisition savings.

**Independent audit:** Different implementation and evaluator, exact-replay of selected tasks, frozen manifests, blind external expert critique; no author-side AI agent may certify its own final synergy claim.

**Stop rule:** If 16–32 frozen tasks show no robust positive Γ_C *after* context, verification and recovery costs, do not claim an empirical human–AI synergy. Publish a scoped negative result or reframe as a methodology; only refine the model after recording the failure and a new causal prediction. A matched lower bound and external review are required to promote a universal systems-engineering theorem.

## H. Dissertation architecture: this metacontext belongs in the body

**Part I — Structural Observability:** task-distinguishing evidence, F1/F2 and exact observational sufficiency.  
**Part II — Information Acquisition:** pure synergy, noisy posterior routing, KL-information-cover costs, oracle matching, prior-art boundary.  
**Part III — Lifecycle Assurance:** change cones, authenticated epochs, proof-carrying digital twins, noise, common-cause and stale-certificate attacks.  
**Part IV — NIGHTFALL as a research case study:** retrospective audit of our roles, methods, failed loops, independent checkers, repository records and response to critic; no proven claim of causal metascientific improvement.  
**Part V — Prospective verification experiment:** controlled complementarity ablations and transferable information-separator conjectures.

The title *The Convoluted Costs of Knowing* gains a second, disciplined interpretation: not only can a system require costly inference from distributed evidence, but the institution that **constructs and revises that inference procedure** also incurs information, computation, authorization and certificate-maintenance costs.
