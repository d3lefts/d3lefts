## Canonical five-stage journey (one reader-facing account)
1. **DEFINE** — Frontier freezes the exact question, baseline, quantifiers, significance target and cheapest falsifier.
2. **DESIGN** — Orchestrator optionally designs *separate* constructor/reproducer/adversary/formalizer lanes **before** the builder result.
3. **EXPLORE** — Discovery explores representations and preregistered operators. Failed attempts are preserved with scope and budget.
4. **CHECK** — Referee and domain-specific independent checkers replay exact arithmetic and adversarial controls. Distinguish same-code replay, independent-code replay and human mathematical review.
5. **RECORD & JUDGE** — Claim Lab retains local-first scoped receipts; Passport/Atlas carry evidence, dependencies, limits and a **separate approval** field. Vesper screens for R2; Devin approves R3 publication and R4 flagship.

Do not fuse Discovery's experiment core with Frontier's assignment validator, and do not fuse Referee's arithmetic verifier with the status ledger. Merge navigation, shared terminology and versioned handoff envelopes only. Observatory remains separately gated until valid paired evaluation and editorial authorization.

## Evidence and release gates
**R0 Assignment → R1 Candidate → R2 Independently checked manuscript → R3 Owner-approved original publication → R4 Separately approved flagship.**

For a paper packet: scope/quantifiers, method/proof, complete input bytes or explicit missing-input declaration, code and exact replay command, immutable source/manifest/hash, attack log, relevant prior art dated and under the same model, substantive negative controls, independent audit verdict, contributor credit, correction channel, publication approval. `Internal independent implementation` and `external human peer review` are separate labels.

For a computational scientific record, require validity, actual source data, exact score rules and same-day closest comparison. Sub-verifier-tolerance numerical differences are ties. Journal publication and benchmark acceptance are independent of the site's status system.

## Definition of success
**Engineering**: main cannot deploy an unchecked change; all approved public evidence URLs and hashes revalidate; mobile/desktop reader completes full Sidon replay with failures handled.  
**Science**: one source-backed R2 manuscript, with an independently attacked central claim and a documented dated novelty comparison; R3 requires Devin's explicit approval.  
**System evaluation**: run 12 genuine blinded, source-matched, provider-metered Gate C pairs and 12 independent grades only after budget and provider permission; report null/negative outcomes.  
**Learning**: each project logs reproducible wins/failures and at least one tested, cross-problem mechanism transfer.
