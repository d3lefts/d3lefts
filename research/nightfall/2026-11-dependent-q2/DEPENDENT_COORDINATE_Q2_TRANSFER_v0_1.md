## Objective, references, and decisive question

Determine which operators and finite-information approximation steps of the unconditional infinite-product degree-two influence theorem survive when the coordinate law is not independent, and identify a minimal nontrivial dependent-law extension. Retain reference inequality \\(\\operatorname{Var}_\\mu(f)^2\\le C_2B^2\\max_i I_i^\\mu(f)\\) with \\(C_2=(2(1+\\sqrt2)\\sqrt3+11901/6250)^2\\approx105.41623981998771\\), only under the exact product-law degree-two hypothesis established in the existing Q2 work.

The arguments here use classical conditional-expectation contraction, variational variance comparison, and block approximate tensorization. The combinations below should be treated as **provisional transfer corollaries**, not new discovery claims.

## Definitions

Let \\(\\nu\\) be a probability law on an arbitrary measurable product \\(\\prod_{i\\in I}S_i\\), with its product sigma algebra, not assumed independent. For real \\(f\\in L^2(\\nu)\\), let \\(Q_i^\\nu f=\\mathbb E_\\nu[f\\mid X_{I\\setminus\\{i\\}}]\\), \\(R_i^\\nu=1-Q_i^\\nu\\), and \\(I_i^\\nu(f)=\\|R_i^\\nu f\\|_{L^2(\\nu)}^2\\). For independent blocks \\(B\\in\\mathcal B\\), use \\(R_B=1-\\mathbb E[\\cdot\\mid X_{I\\setminus B}]\\) and block influence \\(I_B\\).

The block Q2 degree hypothesis is **triple-block residual vanishing** for every three distinct blocks (in the order required by the product-space theorem); it is **not** asserted equivalent to the triple-*site* residual condition under general dependence.

## Result A: unconditional finite-information approximation survives arbitrary dependence

Let \\(\\mathcal F\\) range over finite sigma algebras generated by finite measurable partitions of finitely many coordinates and \\(P_\\mathcal F=\\mathbb E_\\nu[\\cdot\\mid\\mathcal F]\\).

1. For every \\(f\\in L^2(\\nu)\\) and \\(\\varepsilon>0\\), some such \\(\\mathcal F\\) satisfies \\(\\|P_\\mathcal F f-f\\|_2<\\varepsilon\\). Proof: finite-coordinate measurable cylinder sets generate the product sigma algebra, and their simple-function algebra is dense in \\(L^2(\\nu)\\) for a finite measure. Choosing finitely many finite partitions capturing a cylinder-simple approximant gives \\(\\mathcal F\\).
2. If \\(|f|\\le B\\) almost everywhere, then \\(|P_\\mathcal Ff|\\le B\\) almost everywhere, by positivity and conditional expectation.
3. Uniformly across **all** sites \\(i\\), \\(\\sqrt{I_i^\\nu(P_\\mathcal F f)}\\le\\sqrt{I_i^\\nu(f)}+\\|P_\\mathcal Ff-f\\|_2\\), because each \\(R_i^\\nu\\) is an orthogonal projection of norm at most 1.

**Critical failure:** No general identity \\(R_iP_\\mathcal F=P_\\mathcal FR_i\\), no exact influence monotonicity and no automatic preservation of degree. These are independent-product properties and must not be silently imported.

## Result B: exact impossibility of a universal Q2 coefficient without controlling dependence

Take \\(X,Y\\in\\{-1,+1\\}\\) with \\(\\mathbb P(X=x,Y=y)=(1+\\rho xy)/4\\), where \\(0<\\rho<1\\). This has full support. With \\(f=X\\), \\(B=1\\):
- \\(\\operatorname{Var}(f)=1\\).
- \\(Q_Xf=\\rho Y\\), so \\(I_X(f)=1-\\rho^2\\).
- \\(Q_Yf=f\\), so \\(I_Y(f)=0\\).

Any \\(\\operatorname{Var}(f)^2\\le CB^2\\max I_i(f)\\) demands \\(C\\ge(1-\\rho^2)^{-1}\\). Extend the joint law by arbitrarily many independent sites: the function remains algebraic degree one and the triple-residual degree-two hypothesis holds because every three distinct sites include at least one site outside this correlated pair, whose residual annihilates any function of \\(X,Y\\). Thus no dependence-uniform C exists even with full support and infinite many sites.

Noncommutation witness: put \\(g=Y\\), \\(P=\\mathbb E[\\cdot\\mid X]\\). Then \\(R_Xg=0\\), but \\(Pg=\\rho X\\) and \\(I_X(Pg)=\\rho^2(1-\\rho^2)>0\\); at \\(\\rho=1/2\\) this is exactly \\(3/16\\). This disproves site influence monotonicity for finite-information projections under dependence.

## Result C: dependent-block transfer theorem (provisional analytic corollary)

Partition \\(I=\\bigsqcup_{b\\in J}B_b\\) into **finite** blocks, \\(|B_b|\\le k<\\infty\\). Assume the entire law factorizes **across** blocks as \\(\\nu=\\bigotimes_{b\\in J}\\nu_b\\), but allow arbitrary dependence inside any block.

Assume for every block \\(b\\) and \\(h\\in L^2(\\nu_b)\\) the single-block approximate tensorization inequality
\\[
\\operatorname{Var}_{\\nu_b}(h)\\le A_b\\sum_{i\\in B_b}\\mathbb E_{\\nu_b}\\bigl[\\operatorname{Var}(h\\mid X_{B_b\\setminus\\{i\\}})\\bigr],
\\qquad A_b\\le A<\\infty.
\\]
Let \\(f\\in L^2(\\nu)\\), \\(|f|\\le B\\) a.e., with **degree at most two in independent blocks**. Suppose \\(I_i^\\nu(f)\\le M\\) for every original site i. Then
\\[
\\boxed{\\operatorname{Var}_\\nu(f)^2\\le C_2 A k B^2M.}
\\]
Proof: regard each \\(B_b\\) as one categorical coordinate. The product Q2 theorem gives \\(\\operatorname{Var}_\\nu(f)^2\\le C_2 B^2\\max_b I_{B_b}^\\nu(f)\\). Condition on all *other blocks*, apply each block's approximate tensorization to the section of \\(f\\), and integrate, giving \\(I_{B_b}^\\nu(f)\\le A_b\\sum_{i\\in B_b}I_i^\\nu(f)\\le AkM\\). No positive atom floor is required.

This holds for arbitrary (including infinite and uncountable) **block-index** sets provided the reference product theorem covers those indices and the ambient sigma algebra is the block product sigma algebra. It is only as Lean-certified as the referenced product theorem; the reduction here has **not** been formalized.

**Nontrivial exact case.** For each independent correlated sign pair of strength \\(\\rho\\in(-1,1)\\), its optimal block variance-tensorization constant is \\(A=1/(1-|\\rho|)\\). In basis \\(1,X,Y,XY-\\rho\\), a centered \\(h=aX+bY+c(XY-\\rho)\\) satisfies
\\[
\\operatorname{Var}(h)=a^2+b^2+2\\rho ab+(1-\\rho^2)c^2;
\\quad I_X(h)+I_Y(h)=(1-\\rho^2)(a^2+b^2+2c^2).
\\]
The maximal ratio is \\(1/(1-|\\rho|)\\), attained by \\(h=X+\\operatorname{sgn}(\\rho)Y\\) when \\(\\rho\\ne0\\), and also by \\(X+Y\\) for \\(\\rho=0\\). Thus an independent sequence of non-independent pairs with \\(|\\rho|\\le r<1\\) enjoys the dimension-free site bound \\(C_2\\cdot2/(1-r)\\) for block-degree-two observables. At \\(r=1/2\\), the factor relative to product Q2 is 4. **This is a transfer, not a best-constant claim.**

## Result D: bounded density perturbation transfer (provisional analytic corollary)

Let \\(\\mu\\) be any independent product probability measure and \\(d\\nu=w\\,d\\mu\\) with \\(0<a\\le w\\le b<\\infty\\) almost everywhere. Suppose \\(|f|\\le B\\) and \\(f\\) has Q2 degree at most two **under the reference product law \\(\\mu\\)**. Then
\\[
\\boxed{\\operatorname{Var}_\\nu(f)^2\\le C_2\\frac{b^2}{a}B^2\\max_i I_i^\\nu(f).}
\\]
Proof: variational characterization of variance gives \\(\\operatorname{Var}_\\nu(f)\\le b\\operatorname{Var}_\\mu(f)\\). Variational characterization of each conditional residual gives \\(I_i^\\nu(f)\\ge aI_i^\\mu(f)\\). Apply product Q2 under \\(\\mu\\). **Do not replace the \\(\\mu\\)-degree assumption by a \\(\\nu\\)-degree assumption.**

This is a standard bounded-change-of-measure argument, useful as a second comparator but weaker than block reduction when global density ratios grow exponentially with the number of blocks.

## Prior art, significance, gaps, and validation gate

- The 2012 and 2025 dependent-Hoeffding papers already develop dependent ANOVA and oblique/hierarchically orthogonal decompositions; do not claim first dependent-ANOVA extension.
- Approximate tensorization and block spectral gaps are established in Glauber-dynamics literature; do not claim new AT or mixing theorems.
- Holley–Stroock bounded-perturbation comparisons are classical. No priority attached to Result D.
- The composite inequalities, counterexample and finite-label facts have **author-side algebraic justification**, exact finite spot checks, and a proposed independent checker; they do not yet have separate Lean kernels or outside mathematical review.
- **Open frontier:** non-factorizing weakly dependent fields (e.g. Gibbs distributions under Dobrushin/spectral-independence conditions) with degree described intrinsically under their actual joint law. Need a controlled replacement for the commutation/degree-preservation step; spectral gap or variance tensorization alone does not give it.

**Independent audit plan:** (i) adversarial finite-state checker tests residual formulas, finite-projection noncommutation, and pair tensorization Gram matrices; (ii) separately review the arbitrary-product cylinder-density proof; (iii) formalize variational comparisons and the conditional-block tower/section step in Lean; (iv) test counterexamples with three-site interactions and hidden common causes; (v) search specifically for degree-two dependent Q2 antecedents; (vi) only merge after a human review of provenance and transfer assumptions.

**Cheapest decisive next experiment:** use two non-independent blocks of two bits and a bounded block-degree-two \\(f\\), compute actual per-site and per-block influences exactly, and search for any ratio exceeding \\(C_2Ak\\). Search also for failure of degree preservation by finite-label projection under a three-block weakly dependent law. Stop after 1000 exact candidate tests without improved structural hypothesis and switch to commutator/oblique projection approaches.
