**Editorial checkpoint:** 2026-11-24

A sparsified Forrelation hard family has optimal fixed-error classical depth Θ(C_W), with degree two and bounded quantum-query cost.

## Scope and assumptions

Existential hard finite laws, fixed degree and error tolerances.

## Mechanism

Hard promise distribution, finite sampling and exact independent padding.
