## Independent proof attack B (designed to defeat, not affirm A)

1. **Matching cancellation:** build a finite dense graph with intersecting quadratic pair kernels; independently calculate both global parity-coupled expectations, verify exactly that all unmatched edges cancel and matched differences contribute half-rectangles. Attack zero-mass cells and selection of extrema.
2. **Spherical Jensen:** test the PSD reverse square-root inequality in rank one, singular rank-r, isotropic rank-r, and vanishing eigenvalue limits. Do not interchange maximum over coordinates with spherical expectation.
3. **Assigned increments:** verify each assigned variable depends on a distinct *neighbor* coordinate, and genuine Hilbert L² centering holds with arbitrary marginal masses. Prove the finite-family fourth moment with independently constructed covariance operators, not a supplied moment oracle.
4. **Posterior step:** challenge the exact p_{t+1}=p_t−m_t identity against the variance descent, which is false for pure interactions; the derivation uses total influence, not output variance.
5. **Completed product:** independently build a single countable support/section good set for all finite transcript-coordinate-index sequences. Arbitrary exceptional symbols are not suitable witnesses.
6. **Minimax and model equivalence:** compare the full adaptive hard cap with optimal conditional-mean Bayes risk; verify the fixed-queries lower example at arbitrary m and the norm-one rank lower. Do not conflate model with quantum query observations.

The new standard-library checker evaluates ANOVA covariance from a second representation, direct conditional expectations, actual assigned Hilbert fourth moments, exact rational root intervals, plus Bayesian lower examples; this is a *finite adversarial screen only*. Failures must be recorded with the smallest rational witness and source hashes. No unreviewed auto-upgrade to “original”.
