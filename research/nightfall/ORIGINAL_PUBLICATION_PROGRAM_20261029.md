## The objective

Every agent has one *standing, specific original publication hypothesis* and is accountable for pursuing a publishable original contribution, beyond completing assigned tasks. A valid original research publication is the main scientific output of an assignment; software, compute experiments, tests, and individual issue comments are supporting evidence.

**Portfolio commitment:** Every original, sufficiently verified and approved publication is eligible for a dedicated site feature in the research portfolio, with exact claim scope, primary paper, full reproduction/proof artifacts, author credit, and review status. A recognized external journal publication is not required to display a clearly labeled self-published research preprint. **Never imply journal acceptance or outside peer review where none has occurred.**

**Flagship commitment:** The strongest approved original publications may become prominent flagship website features, selected for demonstrated significance, broad generality or transferable mechanism, exceptionally rigorous independently checkable evidence, clarity, and public benefit. Numerical magnitude alone, quantity of generated papers, seniority, and publicity do not confer flagship status. The human research owner approves both publication and flagship placement. Editorial competition is constructive; one portfolio feature never demotes another valid contribution.

## Evidence and publication stages

- **R0 — Assignment:** named paper hypothesis, exact statement and scope, primary reference, potential novelty delta, falsifier, checker, stop/switch condition. This is not a scientific result.
- **R1 — Candidate research:** concrete theorem proof draft, finite witness, algorithm, or family-wide obstruction. Label conjectural and conditional aspects. Negative search results remain scoped.
- **R2 — Independently checked manuscript:** full written proof and/or complete scoped exact certificate, at least one independent implementation or adversarial derivation where appropriate, negative controls, all primary input bytes or a precise missing-input declaration, source dependency list, and a dated closest-prior-art comparison. Distinguish independent internal audit from *external human review* and proof-assistant checking.
- **R3 — Approved original publication:** human-approved public paper/preprint or technically self-contained research article with stable version, abstract, theorem statements, method/proof, reproducibility package, honest limitations, author and upstream credit, citation metadata, disclosure/license gate, corrections channel. Its explicitly described originality and verification scope must be defensible. Mark independent-external-review status separately. **The site may feature this original publication in its research portfolio after release approval.**
- **R4 — Flagship feature:** separately authorized editorial designation requiring a meaningful advance relative to dated closest prior art, independent stress testing of the central statement, generality/significance assessment, visitor-readable impact explanation and a direct path to primary reproducibility. Evidence insufficiency blocks the badge even when R3 publication is valid.

A family-wide *negative theorem* or an original formal counterexample can reach R3/R4 on its scientific merits. A computational publication can reach R3 if novelty, completeness, proof/certificate, and reproducibility justify its scope. No new universal record can be claimed from a fixed finite search.

## Required manuscript packet

Each owner maintains a single canonical packet linked from the standing assignment:

1. **Proposed title and one-paragraph contribution** identifying the exact difference from known literature.
2. **Formal statement** with all quantifiers, constants, hypothesis range, exceptional cases, and sharp boundary between theorem/candidate/conditional evidence.
3. **Proof or replayable method** plus complete primary bytes, source commit, commands, checksums, and negative controls; separate any inherited source theorems.
4. **Closest prior work** with dates/links, same-premise numerical comparison if relevant, and a note of what is *not* claimed.
5. **Independent review ledger** with named mathematical attacker, distinct verifier, actual PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE verdicts and remaining proof debts. Internal model/model agreement never becomes external peer review.
6. **Publication assets**: stable manuscript version, abstract, figures if needed, citation/attribution, permissions and disclosure approval, source/reproduction links, corrections policy.
7. **Editorial abstract** (100–150 words) and short flagship-eligibility assessment against significance, rigor, breadth and portability.

## Per-turn accountability and triage

Each research agent should make progress toward a **paper-defining theorem**, an independent proof, a falsifying example, or an essential artifact. Its standing-issue handoff must report: (a) paper hypothesis, (b) strongest surviving result with evidence level, (c) single most important missing lemma or certificate, (d) next decisive test and budget/stop rule, (e) manuscript packet location, (f) whether to continue, pivot or close the hypothesis. Exploration without progress after three distinct mechanisms leads to a documented obstruction and a new publication hypothesis; quantity of tasks is never counted as publishing progress.

Vesper checks the six standing publication tracks, screens the novelty and verification boundaries, selects potentially publication-ready packets for the human owner's review, and nominates eligible high-significance manuscripts for flagship consideration. **The team may prepare and propose; only Devin authorizes public release and flagship promotion.** A GitHub comment does not activate a separate conversation or automation.

## Site placement contract

- **Research portfolio:** all owner-approved R3 original publications are listed with real versioned paper URLs and verification status. Finite original findings may retain their *finite* labels and remain separately listed; they are not silently relabeled full original publications.
- **Flagship homepage:** select a small number of R4 works with exceptional substance; distinct visual presentation, accurate leading claim, and prominently visible scientific limitations. Existing hero items are neither automatically replaced nor automatically validated by this policy.
- **Evidence linking:** site cards must link to the *actual* manuscript, its source and checker/reproduction entrypoint, review status, and correction notice when applicable. No invented DOI, no external peer-review claim without evidence, no automatic promotion from passing CI.
