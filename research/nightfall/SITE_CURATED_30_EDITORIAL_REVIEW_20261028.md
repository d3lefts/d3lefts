## Editorial corrections across the 30 documents

- The **Werner atlas** is distinguished from separately generated 24/25-copy certificates and historical direct-margin 6–8 rows. The latter cannot be merged into the approved registry without matching upstream SDP source assumptions and independent checker lineage.
- The **four-copy nine-word and syndrome-gluing** papers are public **research digests** of original Library sources; no claim of complete original binary mirroring is made. The 4,209-mode, coupling and diagonal-rigidity proofs are restricted-witness or obstruction results; none proves unrestricted four-copy Werner undistillability.
- **Finite-field phase retrieval** separates its systematic decoder handoff from the conditional 2⁻⁹⁶ downstream span-tail computation. The span-count lemma, probability conventions and decoder completeness remain independent audit gates.
- The **nonabelian shadow-code** and **heterogeneous-erasure** manuscripts disclose established coding/optimization/complexity antecedents. Their useful synthesis requires a narrow originality review.
- **Factor networks, Gaussian pivoting, ETH, affine rigidity** and **periodic forest-gauge** retain explicit source scope and relevant negative results; no universal complexity or spectral breakthrough is announced.
- **SourceBound** remains a separate engineering evaluation project, with safety theory kept in assumption-qualified manuscripts.
- **Proof After Scarcity** preserves the observed adverse 8/20 versus 11/20 result, instead of a misleading positive headline.
- **Discovery Engine** distinguishes tested non-ML adaptive abstraction from a learned router or demonstrated cross-domain scientific gain.
