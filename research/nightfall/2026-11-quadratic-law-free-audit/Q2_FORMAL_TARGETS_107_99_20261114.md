## An exact obstruction to a c/5 proof by the old fourth-moment envelope

The c/4 argument uses F(x,d)=3-2*x^4+4*x*(1-x²)*d+(1-x²)*d², for 0<=x<=1,0<=d<=4. Its polynomial certificate (1-d/4)²*F<=3 is valid by a rational Bernstein decomposition. Replacing 1/4 with 1/5 is false.

At x=1/4 and d=3/4, exact rational arithmetic gives:

    F=1081/256
    3-(1-d/5)²*F=-5209/102400 < 0.

## A genuine, narrower c/5 theorem: zero odd term / pure Q2

There is a nontrivial **positive** result alongside the failed *general* c/5 fourth-moment envelope. When the singleton component vanishes, the odd third-moment cross term vanishes automatically. More generally, the same holds for symmetric independent-increment sums.

For a centered independent owner sum Z with second moment s and fourth moment q,
    q <= 3*s²+c²*s
and Holder s³ <= u²*q (u=E||Z||).
The exact identity (for t=sqrt(s))
    25*[3*t^4-(t-c/5)²*(3*t²+c²)]
    = c*[6*t³+(c-2*t)²*(6*t-c)]
shows (t-c/5)²(3*t²+c²)<=3t^4 for 0<=c<=5t. For c>=5t the desired conclusion is trivial. Consequently
    **sqrt(s) <= sqrt(3)*E||Z|| + c/5**
holds for every finite independent centered Hilbert sum under the stated bounded-increment fourth-moment premises. This statement does **not** assume symmetry of Z; h=0 suffices to eliminate the mixed term.

- fifth_of_zero_odd_term_moments (odd-term-free fourth plus Holder);
- fifth_for_pure_centered_sum (actual unshifted moment-bound shape).

For **pure Hoeffding degree two with zero singleton components** the source owner energy is e_i=s_i, so this c/5 argument can be connected to the existing source-owned independent Hilbert fourth-moment theorem. If the source section budget is the certified 5B, it targets the subclass coefficient (5sqrt(3)+8/5)² = 105.2728129...; with the stronger still-to-be-certified sharp section budget, it yields **99.262985... for this pure-degree-two subclass**.
