**NIGHTFALL v2, 17 November 2026 — stronger mathematical statement.** This supersedes the *pointwise-degree hypothesis* in [v1](FINITE_CELL_CONDITIONAL_AVERAGING_PROOF.md), but retains its finite-cell product-kernel/variance proof and verification boundaries. **Status: author-side complete analytic proof, not a transitive final Lean kernel certificate or independent nonauthor review.** The finite C105.416 theorem is an already kernel-certified ingredient, not newly re-proved.

## Theorem (actual law, null-set-invariant assumptions)

Let I be any set and \((X_i,\Sigma_i,\mu_i)\) any family of nonempty probability spaces. Let \(\mu=\bigotimes_{i\in I}\mu_i\) be the original product probability measure on \(X=\prod_i X_i\). Write \(H=L^2(\mu;\mathbb R)\). For every coordinate i, \(E_i:H\to H\) is **conditional expectation under \(\mu\) onto the σ-algebra generated by all coordinates except i**. Let \(D_i=1-E_i\), \(V(f)=\|f-\mathbb E_\mu f\|_2^2\) and \(I_i(f)=\|D_i f\|_2^2\).

For \(f\in H\), suppose:
1. **Essential bound**: \(|f|\le B\), \(\mu\)-a.e., for some \(B\ge0\).
2. **Genuine source degree ≤2:** \(D_iD_jD_k f=0\) **in \(H\)** for every triple of pairwise distinct indices i,j,k. This is the original product-law Hoeffding/Efron–Stein condition, not a pointwise predicate chosen on μ-null words.

Then for all \(M\ge0\) satisfying \(I_i(f)\le M\) for every i,
\[
 \boxed{V(f)^2\le C_2 B^2 M,\qquad
 C_2=\bigl(2(1+\sqrt2)\sqrt3+11901/6250\bigr)^2
 \approx105.41623981998771.}
\]
This does not require countable I, atom floors, finite alphabet types, a globally chosen representative of f, or a supplementary moment/owner/source-influence oracle. The empty-coordinate case has variance zero.

**Relation to source degree:** On a finite product, the commuting \(D_i\) kill all Hoeffding components with support cardinality ≥3 if and only if every distinct triple composition vanishes in \(L²\). For arbitrary I, finite-information conditional projections are dense in \(H\), and the same condition characterizes degree ≤2 of the observable, without presuming an infinite series converges pointwise.

## Proof

**A. Finite coordinate finite-cell density and actual averaging.** The finite Boolean algebra generated by one-coordinate measurable rectangle events generates the product σ-algebra. Its indicator linear span is dense in H. Every individual finite linear combination depends on finitely many one-coordinate Boolean tests. For a finite test-family Y supported on F⊂I finite, let \(P_Y=\mathbb E_\mu[\cdot\mid Y]\). For every ε>0 choose such Y and a Y-measurable simple h with \(\|f-h\|_2<ε\). Orthogonal best approximation implies \(\|f-P_Y f\|_2<ε\). This is the density operation already formally compiled in \`InfinitePiQ2FiniteCellDensity.lean\`; finite-list-of-tests extraction is mathematical finite Boolean algebra manipulation, not a new caller hypothesis.

**B. Explicit source conditional product and bounded finite table.** For each i∈F and finite label a∈A_i, set \(q_i(a)=\mu_i(Y_i=a)\). When \(q_i(a)>0\), define the actual conditional coordinate measure \(\nu_{i,a}=\mu_i(\cdot\cap\{Y_i=a\})/q_i(a)\). At i∉F use \(\nu_{i,a}=\mu_i\). For every finite label word y with positive mass \(q(y)=\prod_{i\in F}q_i(y_i)>0\), define \(\nu_y=\bigotimes_{i\in I}\nu_{i,y_i}\) and \(g(y)=\int f\,d\nu_y\). Each \(\nu_y\) is **the original product law conditioned on a positive finite-cell event**, obtained by finite cylinder π-system uniqueness. This is an explicit *finite* collection of conditional measures, so arbitrary spaces need no standard-Borel regular conditional kernel. The map \(g(Y)\) is a version of \(P_Y f\). Since \(|f|\le B\) μ-a.e. and \(\nu_y\ll\mu\) at every positive cell, \(|g(y)|\le B\) for **every positive label word**. For null or padding letters, choose one positive source label in each coordinate and extend g coordinatewise by the corresponding positive-label default; the resulting \(g^+\) has a global finite-table bound \(|g^+(y)|\le B\).

**C. The true original heat-bath projections commute with finite-cell projection.** For i∈F, condition on all other labels \(y_{-i}\). Both \(E_iP_Y f\) and \(P_Y E_i f\) are equal to the explicitly computable function
\[
 \sum_{a\in A_i:q_i(a)>0} q_i(a)
 \iint f(z_{-i},u_i)\,d\nu_{i,a}(u_i)\,d\nu_{-i,y_{-i}}(z_{-i}).
\]
The left composition first evaluates \(g(a,y_{-i})\) then averages coordinate i; the right first averages i with original μ_i then averages all other conditional coordinates. The identity \(\mu_i=\sum_a q_i(a)\nu_{i,a}\), finite Fubini, and product independence make them equal μ-a.e. For i∉F, \(P_Y f\) ignores i and tower/Fubini gives \(E_iP_Y f=P_Y f=P_Y E_i f\). Thus **as equality of original-source Hilbert operators**
\[
  E_iP_Y=P_YE_i,\qquad D_iP_Y=P_YD_i\quad(\forall i\in I).
\]

**D. Transfer degree and influence with no approximation loss.** Repeatedly applying the *actual* commutation shows, for pairwise distinct i,j,k,
\[
  D_iD_jD_k(P_Y f)=P_YD_iD_jD_k f=0\quad\text{in }H.
\]
Also \(\|D_iP_Yf\|_2=\|P_YD_if\|_2\le\|D_i f\|_2\); hence \(I_i(P_Y f)\le I_i(f)\le M\), uniformly with no dimension-dependent factor. The original label law is exactly \(q=\bigotimes_{i\in F}q_i\) (source-law transport source-compiled in #691), and the pullback of a finite table to the original product is an L² isometry. The original coordinate heat-bath of the table's lift equals the finite table heat-bath under q: this follows directly by integrating a single coordinate against μ_i and using its pushforward law q_i. Therefore, on the finite label product, \(D_i^qD_j^qD_k^q g=0\) in \(L²(q)\), and finite source variance/influences agree **exactly** with original variance/influence of \(P_Y f\).

**E. From L² triple residual to actual pointwise finite degree on positive source support.** Every finite label word with q(y)>0 is an atom of **strictly positive q-mass**, so an \(L²(q)\)-zero function is zero *at each such label word*. In particular \(D_i^qD_j^qD_k^q g=0\) pointwise on all supported words. For distinct coordinates i,j,k, the difference operator \(\Delta_i^{a,a'} h=h(x[i:=a])-h(x[i:=a'])\) annihilates any function independent of i. Hence
\[
 \Delta_i^{a,a'}\Delta_j^{b,b'}\Delta_k^{c,c'}g
 = \Delta_i^{a,a'}\Delta_j^{b,b'}\Delta_k^{c,c'}(D_i^qD_j^qD_k^q g)=0
\]
whenever all alternate labels and all unchanged coordinates are supported. The equality uses pairwise distinct coordinates and commutation of updates with integrations in different coordinates; it is an elementary finite sum identity. Thus **actual positive-source-supported pointwise degree ≤2** holds for g, even though f had no global pointwise degree representative. The coordinatewise positive-default extension \(g^+\) remains globally bounded and globally pointwise degree≤2. On zero-cell words f may be assigned arbitrarily without affecting any claim; they never enter original-source expectations.

**F. Native finite theorem and the original L² limit.** Encode the heterogeneous finite label alphabets into the common tagged finite alphabet from merged #692, with wrong tags of mass zero. Merged #693 establishes exact arbitrary-weight tagged/native expectations; source variance and each heat-bath influence follow by substituting the squared centered integrands and finite coordinate sums. Apply the already certified original finite C105.416 theorem #587 (or its source-supported finite adapter #687) to \(g^+\), obtaining
\[
    \mathrm{Var}_\mu(P_Y f)^2
      =\mathrm{Var}_{q}(g^+)^2\le C_2 B^2 M
\]
**for every finite coordinate partition Y**. Finally let \(\|f-P_Yf\|_2\to0\), using density from A. Mean and variance are continuous under L² convergence, so \(\mathrm{Var}_\mu(f)^2\le C_2B^2M\), with no additional hypothesis. □
