# Infinite original-product Q2 via finite-cell conditional averaging

**NIGHTFALL proof manuscript — 2026-11-17, v1.0.** This is a **complete paper-level mathematical proof** under the exact assumptions below; it is **not** a claim that the final infinite-product theorem has compiled in Lean, undergone a separate nonauthor mathematical review, or cleared originality/priority. The frozen finite C105.416 Lean terminal is imported as a proved finite statement, not re-proved here.

## Theorem and source convention

Let I be **any** index set (finite, countable or uncountable), and let (X_i, Σ_i, μ_i) be arbitrary probability spaces with nonempty underlying sets. Let μ=⊗_{i∈I} μ_i denote the ordinary product probability measure on X=∏_i X_i. Let f:X→ℝ be product-measurable, and suppose:

1. There is B≥0 such that |f(x)|≤B **at every source word x**.
2. For distinct i,j,k and every x and all six alternate coordinate values, the pointwise eight-term mixed third difference Δ_iΔ_jΔ_k f is zero.

Define the genuine *original-product* variance V=E_μ[(f−E_μ f)²] and heat-bath coordinate influence
\[
  I_i(f)=\|f-E_\mu[f\mid X_{I\setminus\{i\}}]\|_{L^2(\mu)}^2.
\]
Then, for **every** M≥0 such that I_i(f)≤M for all i,
\[
  \boxed{V^2\le C_{\mathrm{Q2}}B^2 M,\qquad
   C_{\mathrm{Q2}}=
   \bigl(2(1+\sqrt2)\sqrt3+11901/6250\bigr)^2
   \approx105.41623981998771.}
\]
If I=∅ the conclusion is immediate, since the source consists of one point and V=0. There is **no** positive-atom lower bound, finite-alphabet assumption, countability requirement, or caller-supplied finite-table inequality. The pointwise mixed-difference condition must not be silently weakened to an unspecified a.e. statement: its transfer through conditionally sampled coordinate values is the exact hypothesis used here.

The finite base theorem is the kernel-certified original-source [PR #587](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/587), and its stronger *on-positive-support-only* adapter is the merged [PR #687](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/687). They cover arbitrary heterogeneous source probabilities and zero-mass symbols **after** a finite heterogeneous-to-common-alphabet tagging reduction.

## Proof

### 1. Finite-coordinate finite-cell labels are L² dense

Let \(\mathcal A\) be the Boolean algebra generated by events \(x_i\in A_i\) for one source coordinate i and an arbitrary measurable A_i⊆X_i. This algebra generates exactly the ordinary product σ-algebra. Every **single event of** \(\mathcal A\) is a finite Boolean combination of finitely many such one-coordinate tests; in particular every finite linear combination of indicators of events in \(\mathcal A\) can be written as a function of a finite collection of finite coordinate-label partitions.

Because μ is finite, those indicator combinations are dense in L²(μ). The repository's independently kernel-compiled finite-cell algebra density and support modules are \`InfinitePiQ2FiniteCellDensity.lean\` (merged #678) and \`InfinitePiQ2FiniteCellSupport.lean\` (merged #688). The algebraic finite-list-of-tests extraction is explicit here: gather the finitely many cylinder generators in the Boolean expressions for all indicator sets, partition each of their finitely many coordinates by its finite membership vector, and take the resulting finite label map.

For such a map \(Y=(Y_i)_{i\in F}\), F⊂I finite, denote by \(P_Y f=E_\mu[f\mid Y]\) the actual-source L² conditional projection. Given ε>0, choose a finite-cell simple h with \(\|f-h\|_2<ε\), and choose Y with respect to which h is measurable. The best-approximation property of orthogonal projection gives
\[
  \|f-P_Y f\|_2\le\|f-h\|_2<ε.
\]
This is an *existence* argument for arbitrarily accurate genuine original-product finite-information conditional projections; no finite-approximant-exists predicate is left as a new theorem hypothesis.

### 2. Build the *actual-law* finite table with no null-cell division

Write each finite coordinate label alphabet as A_i (and a singleton alphabet for inactive coordinates if desired). Set \(q_i(a)=\mu_i\{Y_i=a\}\). These are precisely the pushforward original laws, normalized and nonnegative, possibly with zero probabilities. For each label y whose every q_i(y_i)>0, form the actual source conditional law
\[
  \nu_y=\bigotimes_{i\in I}\nu_{i,y_i},\quad
  \nu_{i,y_i}(D)=
  \frac{\mu_i(D\cap\{Y_i=y_i\})}{q_i(y_i)} \quad(i\in F),
  \qquad \nu_{i,y_i}=\mu_i \quad(i\notin F).
\]
This is a product of **explicitly defined coordinate measures**, not an assumed regular conditional distribution on arbitrary measurable spaces. Finite-cell conditioning under μ agrees with ν_y by the elementary cylinder π-system/product-measure uniqueness argument. Define the finite table \(g(y)=\int f\,d\nu_y\) **only on positive-probability label words**. Since the conditional law is a probability and |f|≤B, \(|g(y)|\le B\). The pullback g(Y) agrees μ-a.e. with \(P_Y f\). The exact original source pushforward law is kernel-certified in the current scoped source [PR #691](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/691), named \`sourceFiniteLabelLaw_exact\` and \`sourceFiniteLabelMap_measurePreserving\`.

For every coordinate i with at least one null label, choose a default positive label a_i*, possible because \(\sum_a q_i(a)=1\). Let r_i fix all positive labels and send every null label to a_i*. Extend the finite table **to every word** by \(g^+(y)=g((r_i y_i)_i)\). It is globally bounded by B, and equals g on genuine source support. This is exactly the null-cell retraction operator of merged #680/#684, without an artificial atom floor.

### 3. The finite table is globally pointwise degree two

Take any three distinct active finite coordinates i,j,k, arbitrary two finite labels at each, and fix all other labels to a *positive* source-support word. For the six labels that have positive coordinate mass, draw the six source coordinate values independently from the corresponding \(\nu_{i,a},\nu_{j,b},\nu_{k,c}\), and draw the common remaining coordinates once from their conditional product distribution. All eight source words assembled by choosing one of the two values at i,j,k share the same other coordinates. Their mixed third difference is **identically zero** by assumption 2, **for every realized six-tuple**. Integrating its finite eight-term expansion gives
\[
    \Delta_i\Delta_j\Delta_k g=0
\]
at all positive label combinations. Thus g has genuine pointwise degree ≤2 on its positive product support. All Fubini/interchange steps are valid because f is bounded, and only finitely many coordinate draws are specially conditioned. Passing through the **coordinatewise** default maps r_i preserves this identity globally, so \(g^+\) satisfies pointwise mixed-third-difference zero on *all* finite label words, including null labels.

This is not deduced from the mere L² density lemma: the explicit product conditional measure and shared-draw eight-term coupling are the structural degree-preservation proof.

### 4. Original source variance and each heat-bath influence transfer exactly

The finite product label law is literally \(q=\otimes_{i\in F}q_i\). Because \(P_Y f=g^+(Y)\) μ-a.e., measure preservation yields
\[
  \operatorname{Var}_q(g^+)=\operatorname{Var}_\mu(P_Y f).
\]
For every active coordinate i, independently refreshing the original X_i changes Y_i with law q_i, while all other labels remain fixed. Hence the **finite source** heat-bath influence equals the **original infinite source** heat-bath influence of its pullback:
\[
  I_i^q(g^+)=I_i^\mu(P_Y f).
\]
For inactive i, \(I_i^\mu(P_Y f)=0\). This can be checked either by direct conditional integration of the source/label products or by the paired independent resampling identity \(\tfrac12 E[(h(X)-h(X^{(i)}))^2]\) for h=g⁺∘Y; the latter has exact original-law label transport in #691, but identifying that energy with \`sourceOtherProjection\` is still an explicit Lean obligation.

Finally, the coordinatewise finite-label conditional projection \(P_Y\) commutes with the genuine original-source other-coordinate projection \(E_i h=E[h\mid X_{I\setminus\{i\}}]\). To see it, decompose Y into the label Y_i and the labels of all other coordinates. The original law is product, so averaging i and taking conditional expectations in any other coordinate's finite labels commute; conditioning on Y_i then averaging i is the same as averaging i directly. Thus
\[
    (1-E_i)P_Y f=P_Y(1-E_i)f.
\]
The orthogonal projection P_Y is an L² contraction, and consequently
\[
  \forall i,\qquad I_i^q(g^+)=I_i^\mu(P_Y f)
     =\|P_Y(1-E_i)f\|_2^2
     \le \|(1-E_i)f\|_2^2=I_i^\mu(f)\le M.
\]
**This exact contraction avoids any additive discretization error or dimension factor.** Its formal source-specific commutation is the remaining missing transitive Lean bridge. The generic Hilbert-adjoint mechanism has independently compiled in PR #694, but its source-specific \`hintertwine\` premise is not yet discharged by that module.

### 5. Invoke the *already proved* finite C105.416 theorem and pass to the limit

Finite alphabets A_i may differ between active coordinates. Embed them into one common **tagged** alphabet \(\{*\}\sqcup\bigsqcup_{i\in F}A_i\), giving each coordinate i zero probability on wrong-tag or * letters. The extra dummy symbol handles F=∅; if F≠∅ the existing merged \`InfinitePiQ2HeterogeneousAlphabet.lean\` already uses the tagged Sigma sum. Pull back g⁺ via the coordinatewise native-label decoder. Its degree and bound are unchanged, and original weighted sums on correctly tagged words equal their native sums while all wrong-tag words have exactly zero product weight. This transfers variance and every genuine heat-bath influence **without change**. The finite tagged-weighted expectation sum proof is in draft PR #693 and requires a strict successful Lean receipt before promotion.

Apply the already proved full original-source finite C105.416 terminal to this bounded degree-two finite table, using M as its exact influence cap. The result is, for **every** chosen finite label partition Y,
\[
  \operatorname{Var}_\mu(P_Y f)^2\le C_{\mathrm{Q2}}B^2M.
\]
By Step 1 there are arbitrarily accurate such projections. The identity \(\operatorname{Var}(h)=\|h-E h\|_2^2\) and L² convergence give
\(\operatorname{Var}_\mu(P_Y f)\to \operatorname{Var}_\mu(f)\). Let the approximation error tend to zero to obtain \(V^2\le C_{\mathrm{Q2}}B^2M\). QED under the stated product-space, pointwise degree and boundedness hypotheses.
