# Subcube parities force expensive local frames: counterexample to Conjecture E's frame converse
**NIGHTFALL / independent parallel review, 2026-11-19, v0.1.** Paper-level proof and exact finite checker; not Lean-certified, human-refereed, or priority-cleared. Does not imply a quantum speedup or a refutation of the broader task-conditioned structure program.

## Provenance and objective
Claude's source is TOWERS_20261119.md and LINEAR_DISTRIBUTIONS_20261119.md on branch structural-information-theory, consulted at exact branch head 179a28e5cd3ae1bd386c8897a30dfffe7821f301. TOWERS identifies the unbounded-depth subcube family with tiny bounded-sum subspace number, and asks for either a cheap simulation or proof that cheap frames fail. The argument below proves the **latter** for *every possible* frame, not just product/tower bases. It does not resolve whether an efficient simulation exists outside frames.

The local frame definition comes from Claude's Theorem B: a (c,c′,h)-frame consists of a set of vectors B in F₂^m such that (i) each original row r is a sum of at most c members of B; (ii) each b in B is a sum of at most c′ original rows; and (iii) no nonempty subset of at most h members of B has zero sum. Its Λ_T is the minimum cc′ with h=h(2cT, ε,δ). The exact threshold in the source is h(2cT)=2kφ max(4cT,D), kφ≥1, so h(2cT)>3c for T≥1 and any c≥1.

## The family
Fix L≥1. Index ambient coordinates by z in {0,1}^L, so m=2^L. Each ternary word a in {0,1,*}^L indexes the incidence vector r_a in F₂^m of the subcube {z: z_i=a_i whenever a_i is not *}. The original row family is R_L={r_a}, consisting of n=3^L distinct nonzero vectors and spanning F₂^m (singletons are included).

For any ternary word with a_i=*, denote by a^(0),a^(1) the same word with that * replaced by 0 or 1. Then

  r_a = r_{a^(0)} + r_{a^(1)}  in F₂^m.             (SPLIT)

These weight-three relations generate ALL dependencies among the indexed original rows: recursively split every free coordinate to express r_a as the sum of its singleton rows; singleton rows are a basis of dimension m, so the kernel has dimension 3^L−2^L and is generated by SPLIT. This kernel generation is included as a useful independent certificate, though only SPLIT is needed for the lower bound.

## Theorem H (frame obstruction; new derived claim to review)
For every (c,c′,h)-frame B of R_L with h≥3c, c,c′≥1,

    c ≥ (4/3)^L,     and so  cc′ ≥ (4/3)^L.

Consequently, for any fixed accuracy and failure parameters and T≥1, Claude's exact frame cost satisfies

    Λ_T(R_L) ≥ (4/3)^L = m^{log₂(4/3)}.

Meanwhile, for every r≥1, the largest dimension N_r(R_L) of a subspace contained in the sums of at most r original rows satisfies

    N_r(R_L) ≤ r log₂(3^L + 1) = O(r L).

Thus **the frame-converse Conjecture E, as formally stated in LINEAR_NECESSITY_20261119.md, is FALSE**: choose T=1, any fixed polynomial a(T) as the conjecture's sumset radius and k=ceil(a(1)log₂(3^L+1))=O(L). Its hypothesis N_{a(1)}≤k holds, but its conclusion Λ_1≤b(k,1) for a universal polynomial b is contradicted by Λ_1≥(4/3)^L. The obstruction holds even allowing arbitrary vectors B, arbitrary c′, and h-wise independence rather than global independence.

### Proof
For each original row r_a choose one representation as a sum of ≤c **distinct** frame vectors. Let A be the binary n-by-|B| incidence matrix of these chosen subsets, so each row A_a has Hamming weight ≤c. Let M_B be the |B|-by-m matrix of the frame vectors. Thus the n-by-m original incidence matrix R satisfies R=A M_B.

**Step 1: splitting identities become identities in the representation coefficients.** Fix SPLIT at a. Since r_a+r_{a^(0)}+r_{a^(1)}=0, the coefficient word d=A_a+A_{a^(0)}+A_{a^(1)} (mod 2) satisfies d M_B=0 and has Hamming weight ≤3c≤h. Condition (iii) of the frame prohibits any nonempty such dependency, so d=0. Therefore EVERY column of A is a ternary array f(a) satisfying

    f(a) = f(a^(0)) + f(a^(1))    (mod 2)             (LOCAL)

at every axis split.

**Step 2: nonzero LOCAL arrays have weight at least 2^L.** Prove by induction on L. For L=1, the triple (f(0),f(1),f(*))=(u,v,u+v) has weight either 0 or 2. For L>1, write the three length-(L−1) slices f_0, f_1, f_*; LOCAL gives f_*=f_0+f_1. At a coordinate b in {0,1,*}^{L−1}, the triple (f_0(b),f_1(b),f_*(b)) has weight 0 or 2, hence

    wt(f)=2 |supp(f_0) union supp(f_1)|.

If f is nonzero, at least one of f_0,f_1 is a nonzero LOCAL array, with support ≥2^{L−1} by induction. So wt(f)≥2^L. Equivalently, this is the minimum-distance-2^L property of the L-fold tensor product of the binary [3,2,2] single-parity-check code. The code property is standard; its use as a frame lower bound is the new proposed step.

**Step 3: rank forces at least m nonzero LOCAL columns.** The original rows R_L contain m independent singleton vectors, so rank(R)=m. Since R=A M_B, rank(A)≥m. Therefore A has at least m **nonzero columns**, each a nonzero LOCAL array of weight at least 2^L. Count 1s in A two ways:

    c 3^L ≥ sum_a wt(A_a) = sum_{b∈B} wt(A_column_b)
           ≥ m·2^L = 4^L.

This proves c≥(4/3)^L, and c′≥1 proves the frame-cost statement. QED.

**Step 4: short-sum bound.** Every sum of ≤r rows is a sum of r elements of R∪{0}. There are at most (3^L+1)^r such ordered tuples, hence |r·R|≤(3^L+1)^r. A k-dimensional subspace has 2^k members, implying k≤r log₂(3^L+1). QED.

No use of Sanders, PFR, FUR, Route 1, or a claimed numerical optimum occurs anywhere in Theorem H.

## Verification and limitations
Independent checker: dichotomy_tests/check_subcube_frame_obstruction.py. It verifies for L=1..4 that (a) original row rank is 2^L, (b) all splitting relations hold, (c) their span has dimension 3^L−2^L, (d) every nonzero LOCAL codeword has weight at least 2^L (exhaustive on all 2^{2^L} codewords for L≤4), (e) canonical independent basis attains cost c=m (upper bound) while theorem lower bound holds, and (f) a one-bit mutation of the full-cube row is detected by the splitting-relation audit.

**Status:** author-side all-L proof plus exact finite controls, not Lean kernel check or independent human mathematical review. A previously merged artifact claiming E true in a restricted class is NOT contradicted: the subcube row family is a growing-doubling unbounded-depth tower, not covered by small-doubling or bounded-depth hypotheses.

## Consequences for the wider programme
1. Retire **only** Conjecture E's specific **cheap high-independence frame converse**. Theorem B (frame sufficiency) remains sound as a conditional/paper result, and Theorem C (subspace produces advantage) remains unaffected.
2. Do NOT infer that quantum advantage exists for this subcube family. The theorem proves frame *inefficiency*, not classical simulation hardness. Both possibilities survive: (i) a non-frame simulator of depth poly(T,L), in which case a new simulation operator is required; or (ii) a quantum algorithm using poly(L) queries with a classical lower bound superpoly(L), in which case N_r is not a two-sided quantum advantage measure.
3. Existing Claude note TOWERS_20261119.md already anticipated that a cheap frame might fail; this theorem resolves that specific question by a general lower bound.
4. Next cheapest decisive experiment: test Fourier-analytic/pseudorandom simulation for this tensor family **without** forcing a local-frame factorization; alternatively seek a recursively composed quantum predicate. Keep the oracle model and classical access to original subcube parities unchanged. Avoid using Holevo alone to claim polynomial simulation.
5. Generalization operator: for a row family given by local relations whose coefficient-code C has minimum distance Δ and rank m, every (c,c′,h≥wc)-frame obeys c≥mΔ/n whenever all defining relations have weight at most w. This is a reusable obstruction for other geometrically defined tensor/LDPC row systems; it needs the relation code to be generated by weight-≤w checks and to have min distance Δ.
