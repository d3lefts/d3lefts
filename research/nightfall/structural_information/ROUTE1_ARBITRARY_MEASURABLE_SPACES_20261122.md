# Route 1 for arbitrary measurable coordinate spaces

**Paper-level lift.** The measure-theoretic lift below is not Lean-certified. Its finite dependencies are [#970](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/970) and the finite-output arbitrary-latent reduction in [#976](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/976).

Let \(n<\infty\), let \(E_i\) be arbitrary measurable spaces, and equip \(E=\prod_i E_i\) with the product sigma-algebra. Suppose the law of \(X\) admits a conditional product representation \(P_{W,X}(dw,dx)=\mu(dw)\prod_i\kappa_i(w,dx_i)\) with specified probability kernels and finite \(I(W;X)\), where mutual information is joint relative entropy. Write \(C\) for the infimum of these information costs. Let \(f:E\to[0,1]\) be measurable of interaction degree at most \(d\), i.e. a sum of measurable cylinder functions on at most \(d\) coordinates. For positive accuracy and failure parameters, there is a deterministic tree of depth \(\operatorname{poly}(d,1/\varepsilon,1/\delta)(1+C)\) with error more than \(\varepsilon\) on probability at most \(\delta\). Each read can be reduced to the bin of a fixed finite measurable partition of the queried coordinate. This is query complexity, not an effective construction for arbitrary spaces.

## 1. Product domination

Let \(\nu=P_X,\ \nu_i=P_{X_i},\ \lambda=\bigotimes_i\nu_i\). Data processing gives \(I(W;X_i)\le I(W;X)\), hence \(P_{W,X_i}\ll\mu\otimes\nu_i\). Choose a nonnegative jointly measurable Radon–Nikodym derivative \(r_i\), changing infinite values to zero on a product-null set. Its \(\nu_i\)-integral equals 1 for almost every \(w\), by testing against every latent event. Thus \(r_i(w,x_i)\nu_i(dx_i)\) defines a probability kernel after replacing exceptional rows with \(\nu_i\). For each **fixed** measurable \(A_i\), the replacement agrees with \(\kappa_i(w,A_i)\) for almost every \(w\), again by testing against every latent event. On each fixed rectangle only finitely many exceptions need be discarded. The original and replacement product kernels thus give the same joint law by the uniqueness theorem for measures on rectangles. By Tonelli the replacement joint law is dominated by \(\mu\otimes\lambda\), and \(\nu\ll\lambda\).

No countably generated assumption is needed. The supplied \(\kappa_i(w)\) itself need not be simultaneously absolutely continuous off one null set: for a countable–cocountable uncountable space with its 0/1 probability, \(W=X\) and \(\kappa(w)=\delta_w\) give joint independence on the product sigma-algebra but every \(\delta_w\) is singular.

## 2. Finite discretization preserving degree

The algebra of finite unions of measurable rectangles generates the product sigma-algebra; its simple functions are dense in \(L^1(\lambda)\) by a monotone class argument. Any such simple function \(u\) is constant on a common finite product partition. The conditional average \(g=E_\lambda[f\mid\text{partition}]\) satisfies \(\|g-f\|_1\le2\|u-f\|_1\). Absolute continuity therefore allows \(\Pr_\nu(|g-f|>a)\) to be made arbitrarily small at every fixed \(a>0\).

The cylinder summands may be taken bounded even if a supplied decomposition does not have this property: anchor at a single base point and take the ordinary inclusion–exclusion components of \(f\). The component on \(S\) is bounded by \(2^{|S|}\) and vanishes for \(|S|>d\), since every given summand then omits a coordinate in which the alternating sum cancels. Product independence implies the average of a bounded summand on a positive partition cell depends only on the bins of its own coordinates. Discard null marginal bins, sending them to arbitrary positive bins (a marginal-null modification). The finite bin product then consists of positive cells, so \(g\) is globally [0,1]-valued and of degree at most \(d\). A common alphabet, if required, is obtained by padding smaller alphabets with zero-mass duplicates and extending \(g\) via the coordinatewise retraction.

## 3. Same finite theorem and same dependence

Let \(Y\) be the bin-labelled vector. The kernels show conditional product structure and \(C_W(Y)\le I(W;Y)\le I(W;X)\), by #976 and data processing. For an alternative direct finite-output bridge, preserve the output marginal vector and conditional mean entropy by a finite convex combination of \((p,H(p))\) on the compact space of product probability vectors. The resulting finite latent has the same information cost.

Apply #970 to \(g\) on \(Y\). Reading a bin costs one original coordinate query. The final error event is contained in the union of \(\{|g-t|>\varepsilon-a\}\) and \(\{|f-g|>a\}\). More precisely, write the exact finite depth-\(m\) bound as \((A(d,\tau)+B(d,\tau)s)/m+\tau/\varepsilon^2\) for \(s>C_W(Y)\). The arbitrary-space statement inherits this same bound for every \(s>C\): choose a representation and charge with \(I(W;X)<s_0<s\); choose \(0<a<\varepsilon\) so replacing \(\varepsilon\) by \(\varepsilon-a\) uses less than half the slack \(B(d,\tau)(s-s_0)/m\), then choose the partition so its error uses the remainder. Constant targets need no queries.

## Scope and overlap

The proof does not assert that every measurable law admits regular conditional distributions, an effective procedure to compute partitions, or a tree uniform across targets. Existing NIGHTFALL infinite-product Q2 results use finite-cell averaging for product measures. The extra ingredient here is finite-information domination of correlated laws and discretization without increasing the Wyner charge; #976 by itself treats only finite output.

The finite-coordinate restriction matters for naïve infinite-dimensional degree definitions. Let \(W\) be a fair bit and, conditional on \(W\), take a countable IID sequence of Bernoulli parameters \(1/2-a\) or \(1/2+a\). Then \(I(W;X)=\log 2\) by the strong law. The tail observable that the limiting empirical frequency exceeds \(1/2\) equals \(W\) almost surely, yet every finite edit leaves it unchanged and it is zero almost surely under the product of unconditional marginals. A depth-\(q\) tree yields at most \(q\) fresh Bernoulli observations, even adaptively. The transcript KL between the two hypotheses is at most \(q\,D(\mathrm{Ber}(1/2+a)\|\mathrm{Ber}(1/2-a))=O(qa^2)\) for \(0<a\le1/4\), by the KL chain rule. Pinsker and binary testing then require \(\Omega(a^{-2})\) queries for any fixed error below \(1/2\) when estimating this observable to accuracy below \(1/2\). This refutes the extension with infinite-coordinate degree defined by finite-edit differences or degree under the product of unconditional marginals. It does not exclude an infinite-coordinate theorem under a suitably strong approximation hypothesis under the actual law.

The closest finite-product model in this search was Girish–Mittal–Nehoran–Raz, [Distributional Variants of the Aaronson–Ambainis Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35327), which treats biased products and slices; this comparison is not exhaustive priority clearance. The Radon–Nikodym, product-cell approximation, strong law, and testing bounds used here are classical.
