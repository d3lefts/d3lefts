## 1. Exact scope

Let V=F_2^m, N=2^m, X=[N]^V, Q_N be iid-uniform labels, and ν_N be the Simon law generated by a uniform nonzero shift s and an injective uniformly random labeling of the N/2 pairs {x,x+s}. Write H_k(Q_N) for the pure order-k Efron–Stein space and

Gamma_k(ν,Q)=sup{|E_νg−E_Qg|: g∈H_k(Q), ||g||_∞≤1}.

**Proposed Theorem A (symmetry-to-local transfer).** For every fixed k there exists B_k<∞ such that for every m and every probability law μ on [N]^V invariant under AGL(m,2) acting on input addresses and S_N acting simultaneously on output labels,

Gamma_k(μ,Q_N) ≤ B_k δ_k(μ,Q_N),

where δ_k=max_{S⊂V, |S|≤k} TV(μ_S,(Q_N)_S). The constant depends only on k, not on m,N or μ.

**Proposed Theorem S (Simon).** For every fixed k there exists C_k<∞ such that for every N=2^m,

Gamma_k(ν_N,Q_N) ≤ C_k/N.

Theorem A plus the collision coupling (Lemma 5) implies Theorem S. They assert no useful control for k growing with N. Risk transfer through #990 is still conditional on the separate correctness/audit of its level-wise risk certificate.

## 2. Paper proof of Theorem A (newly reconstructed and explicit)

We distinguish three levels: exact finite representation, exact finite interpolation, and a uniform-in-N argument.

**Lemma 1 (symmetry and stable orbit coordinates).** Averaging g over AGL(m,2)×S_N preserves H_k, ΔE and the sup bound. Assume m≥k−1 and N≥k. Each k-address orbit corresponds exactly to an isomorphism class of affine dependencies among k distinct points; there are finitely many types A indexed only by k. The set stabilizer induces a subgroup G_A≤S_k, also determined by the affine matroid.

Proof: subtract one designated point and examine its k−1 difference vectors. Any bijection between two finite affine configurations preserving binary affine relations extends to an invertible affine map on V (extend an isomorphism between finite-dimensional affine spans to V). The action on each k-set therefore depends only on its affine matroid and is stable for m≥k−1.

**Lemma 2 (pure-k equality-partition basis).** Let E_π(y_1,...,y_k) indicate equality of outputs within each block of a set partition π of [k], without conditions between blocks. Write P_k^N=∏_{i=1}^k(I−E_i), where E_i replaces coordinate i by an independent uniform [N] label. For N≥k, the projected functions P_k^N E_π over all partitions with no singleton blocks constitute a basis of S_N-invariant pure-k kernels.

Proof: equality patterns are all S_N-orbits of [N]^k, so the E_π are a basis of the S_N-invariant functions by triangular Möbius inversion when N≥k. Any E_π with a singleton block omits a coordinate and is killed by P_k^N. Conversely the pure-k invariant space has dimension Σ_{j=0}^k(-1)^{k-j} binom(k,j)Bell(j): write the natural permutation representation as 1⊕W and count fixed tensors in W^{⊗k} by inclusion-exclusion. This equals the number of partitions with no singleton blocks. Surjectivity of the projection and matching dimension proves independence. After G_A averaging, orbit sums of these partition kernels form an independent basis h_{A,λ} of the G_A-invariant pure-k space.

Choose U_{A,λ}^{m,N}(Y) to be the **average**, over all k-address subsets S in orbit A, of the transported h_{A,λ}(Y_S). Every doubly invariant g∈H_k(Q_N) has a unique expansion g=Σ_{A,λ} c_{A,λ}U_{A,λ}^{m,N}. There are D_k such basis functions, with D_k finite and independent of m,N in the stable range.

**Lemma 3 (finite affine calibration, nondegenerate diagonal).** Fix r=k−1 and c=k. For each deterministic c-color table φ:F_2^r→[c], sample a uniform affine map L:V→F_2^r and let the full-table probe be Y_x=embed(φ(L(x)))∈[N]. Define M_N(φ;A,λ)=E_L U_{A,λ}^{m,N}(Y). Define M_∞ by replacing P_k^N E_π within each h by the unprojected E_π. These matrices' entries are independent of m; M_∞ depends only on k and φ.

**Finite separation claim.** The D_k functions of φ, M_∞(φ;A,λ), are linearly independent.

Proof. Apply the exact k-variable Hoeffding projection under an independent uniform-[c] law on the finitely many color variables {φ(t)}_{t∈F_2^r}. Focus on the component supported on a *specific* k-point target set T⊂F_2^r. A term E_π(φ(L(S))) has zero projected component if L identifies any pair of the k source points: it then depends on fewer than k distinct color variables. Otherwise the projected component is P_k^c E_π, transported to T, and can occur only if L(S)=T. An affine map cannot increase affine rank. If source rank equals target rank, its restriction to the affine span is an affine bijection, forcing source and target affine-matroid types to coincide. Fix a nonzero hypothetical linear dependence among the M_∞ functions, select the *largest source rank* R for which any coefficient survives, and choose target T of rank R and the same affine type A as that coefficient. Lower-rank sources cannot contribute to the T projection, and higher-rank sources have zero coefficients by maximality. Same-rank sources of other types cannot contribute. For source type A, conditional on L(S)=T the induced bijections S→T range uniformly over the finite set of affine isomorphisms; this is a coset of G_A. Because h_{A,λ} is G_A-invariant, the resulting diagonal map equals a **strictly positive scalar** p_{A,T} times its pure-k kernel P_k^c E_π (or its G_A average). Indeed, there exists an injective affine map on the rank-R source span into F_2^r since r≥R, and every such map has positive probability under uniform affine L. The diagonal scalar is nonzero; the injectivity probability on a rank-R span is ∏_{j=0}^{R−1}(1−2^{j−r})>0. By Lemma 2 with c=k, the G_A-invariant pure-k kernels are linearly independent. Therefore every coefficient of type A and rank R vanishes, a contradiction. Descending in rank establishes independence.

Consequently, since the table space contains finitely many c^{2^r} colorings, select D_k colorings φ_1,...,φ_{D_k} yielding a rational invertible square evaluation matrix M_∞.

**Lemma 4 (uniform coefficient control).** Put L_k=||M_∞^{-1}||_∞, A_k=2^k−1, and take N≥N_0(k):=max(2^{k−1},k,ceil(2D_k A_k L_k)). For each projected no-singleton partition kernel, ||P_k^N E_π−E_π||∞≤A_k/N: expand Π_i(I−E_i), and for every nonempty resampling set, the conditional expectation of E_π is ≤1/N because a resampled variable must equal at least one other member of its non-singleton block. Hence ||M_N−M_∞||∞≤D_k A_k/N≤1/(2L_k). The Neumann-series bound yields ||M_N^{-1}||∞≤2L_k.

For ||g||∞≤1, all probe expectations |E_{L}g(embed(φ_j∘L))| are ≤1, so its orbit coefficients satisfy max_{A,λ}|c_{A,λ}|≤2L_k. Each kernel U_{A,λ} has norm ≤2^k, and its expectation gap under μ,Q_N is at most 2^{k+1}δ_k (TV duality on a k-coordinate law). Thus Γ_k≤2^{k+2}D_k L_k δ_k.

**Small-N repair.** When N<N_0(k), the crude Γ_k≤2 is *not* a bound proportional to arbitrarily small δ_k. Instead, any g∈H_k with ||g||∞≤1 has g=Σ_{|S|=k}g_S with ||g_S||∞≤2^k by the exact product projection formula. Hence Γ_k≤2^{k+1}binom(N,k)δ_k. There are finitely many such N, so enlarge B_k to max of that finite list and 2^{k+2}D_kL_k. This finishes the paper argument of Theorem A.

**Boundary cases.** k>N implies H_k={0} and Γ_k=0; k=1 follows from singleton marginals directly; N=1 and k=0 are trivial. The finite-template construction applies only in stable range and relies on μ possessing *both* specified symmetries. It is not a generic almost-k-wise independence theorem.

**Lemma 5 (Simon coupling).** For any k addresses, a uniform nonzero s connects an observed pair with probability ≤binom(k,2)/(N−1). Given no such connection, their outputs are distinct samples without replacement, which can be coupled with iid uniform labels with failure ≤binom(k,2)/N. Hence δ_k(ν_N,Q_N)≤binom(k,2)[1/(N−1)+1/N]. Combining with Theorem A gives the O_k(1/N) claim of Theorem S.

## 3. Finite audit strengthening at k=6

A new stand-alone checker `scripts/simon_k6_affine_structural_audit.py` enumerates the 41 no-singleton partitions of six labels, the affine relation patterns of four representative six-point affine matroids, all 720 coordinate permutations, the exact stabilizers and partition orbit actions. It prints:

| Affine type | Affine rank | Stabilizer order | No-singleton partition orbits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| six of eight cube vertices | 3 | 48 | 8 |
| one 4-circuit plus two points | 4 | 48 | 8 |
| one minimal 6-circuit | 4 | 720 | 4 |
| six affinely independent points | 5 | 720 | 4 |

Therefore the **stable doubly invariant pure degree-6 space has dimension D_6=24**. This is independent exact combinatorial arithmetic, not a full 24×24 calibration determinant nor a universal Lean proof.

Proof of completeness of these four types: the binary affine-relation code on six distinct points has dimension 5−rank, at most 2 (an affine plane has only four points), has only even-weight words, and cannot contain a weight-2 word. If its dimension is one, the nonzero relation has weight 4 or 6. If dimension is two, all three nonzero words must have weight 4 (a weight-6 relation plus weight-4 would sum to forbidden weight 2); the three complementary disjoint pairs determine a unique type. Dimension zero is affine independence.
