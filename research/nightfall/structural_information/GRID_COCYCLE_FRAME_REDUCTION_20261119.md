# Conjecture E: sparse-decoder / Lipschitz-potential reduction for overlapping graphic circuits

**2026-11-19; author-side exact finite linear algebra, not Lean-certified, externally reviewed, or novelty-cleared.** Companion to `KFREE_CIRCUIT_FRAME_THEOREM_20261119.md`. This is a *reduction*, not a solution of unrestricted Conjecture E.

## Graph/linear-code representation

Let `G=(V,E)` be a finite connected undirected graph. For each edge `e=uv`, take the binary row `r_e=1_u+1_v ∈ F₂^V`. The row span is exactly the even-parity subspace `W = {z∈F₂^V : Σ_v z_v=0}` of dimension `|V|−1`. Let `B=(b_j)_{j∈J}` be an actual `(c,c',h)` frame for these rows (so each b_j is a sum of ≤c' graph-edge rows, each edge-row is a sum of ≤c b_j, and any nonempty ≤h subset of B is linearly independent). The physical `b_j` are distinct nonzero vectors if h≥2.

Choose a **word** `a_e ∈F₂^J` for each original edge e with Hamming weight ≤c and `Σ_j a_e(j)b_j=r_e`. Choose a reverse edge-word `p_j∈F₂^E` of Hamming weight≤c' with `b_j=Σ_e p_j(e)r_e`. Define the linear decoder `D:F₂^J→W` by `D(e_j)=b_j`, so each D-column is the boundary of an edge chain of length≤c' and has physical Hamming weight≤2c'. The **minimum distance of ker D** exceeds h by the frame girth condition.

## Theorem H (face-cocycle rigidity and exact equivalence)

Assume the cycle space of G is generated by edge cycles of length at most `ℓ` and `h≥cℓ`. Then **every choice** of the forward words a_e satisfies

    Σ_{e∈C} a_e = 0    for every graph cycle C.                       (H1)

Indeed `D(Σ_{e∈C}a_e)=Σ_{e∈C}r_e=0`. If C is a generator of length≤ℓ, this kernel vector has Hamming weight≤cℓ≤h. The frame has no nonzero kernel word of weight≤h, so the vector must vanish. By linearity (H1) extends to the whole cycle space.

Fix a root o and define `φ_o=0`. For each v choose any path P(o,v) and put

    φ_v = Σ_{e∈P(o,v)} a_e ∈F₂^J.

(H1) makes this independent of path, and for every edge `uv∈E`,

    a_{uv}=φ_u+φ_v,          |φ_u+φ_v|_Hamming≤c,
    D φ_v = 1_o+1_v,        |supp D(e_j)|≤2c'.                     (H2)

Thus a high-girth frame yields a **sparse-column decoder plus an adjacent-Lipschitz section of the vertex differences**. This condition involves no global doubling parameter.

**Converse.** If there is a linear map `D:F₂^J→W` with each column a parity of at most c' original edge rows, `ker D` has distance>h, and potentials `φ_o=0, φ_v∈F₂^J` satisfy `Dφ_v=1_o+1_v` and `|φ_u+φ_v|≤c` for every edge uv, then the columns of D and the edge words `a_{uv}=φ_u+φ_v` are exactly a `(c,c',h)` frame. The converse needs no cycle-generation assumption.

**Reduction for square grids.** The cycle space of a rectangular square grid is generated by the 4-edge face boundaries, hence the theorem applies with `ℓ=4` whenever `h(2cT)≥4c`. Every such frame is equivalent to this sparse-decoder/Lipschitz-section certificate. It is therefore not enough to rule out low-stretch **tree** bases or rank-only frames: one must rule out **all** D with kernel distance>h and all word potentials φ, including redundant B and c'>1.

### Independent exact check and negative control

`dichotomy_tests/check_grid_cocycle_exact.py` uses actual edge-incidence XOR words for 2×2 through 5×5 grids. It checks an explicit BFS-tree basis, all face-cycle word cancellations, potential path-independence, boundary reconstruction `Dφ_v=1_o+1_v`, reverse source cost 1 and forward width c. A second control keeps **all** original edges as B: it has girth 4, so at h=3 it is legal with c=c'=1 but the face word sum is nonzero. This demonstrates the hypothesis `h≥4c` **cannot simply be deleted** from Theorem H.

## The unresolved theorem/counterexample dichotomy

To **prove E for grid-type cores**, build for every T a pair (D,φ) with `c c'≤poly(T)`, `dist(ker D)>h(2cT)` and adjacent potential jumps of weight≤c, uniformly over grid size. To **refute E**, prove a lower bound ruling out *all* such maps for grids of unbounded size. Neither has been established. The same condition applies to any connected graph whose cycle space has bounded-length generating cycles.

The remaining operator is **sparse chain contraction**, not a global small-doubling PFR estimate. This also gives an exact, finite SAT/SMT target for small cores; a finite-grid search that finds a feasible frame is a construction, while an UNSAT certificate for one finite grid does not alone establish a growing-family lower bound.
