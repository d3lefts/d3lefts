## Decision summary

**Highest-priority possible novel theorem:** Route 1, `poly(d,1/ε,1/δ)·(1+ln M)` classical **decision-tree query** depth under any finite mixture of M independent product laws, all finite alphabets and zero atoms. **No public source located with a theorem of this exact scope** in a targeted web/arXiv + scholarly-index search (see matched sources). **Provisional novelty only**: the proof remains author/AI paper-level, its finite star-coupling regressions do not constitute an end-to-end formal proof or non-author referee approval.

**Strongest separately promising theorem:** uniform **categorical degree-2** `Var_p(f)^2 ≤ C B^2 max_i Inf_i^p(f)` with absolute C≈105.416, arbitrary alphabets and atoms. No exact predecessor located. Original *binary* degree-two case already follows from classical Grothendieck machinery; do **not** claim it as new. The novelty threshold is the categorical, genuine-original-law, alphabet-independent form. Reported Lean kernel scope must be audited at named theorem and exact source.

**Most distinctive lower bound/corollary candidate:** Forrelation Gaussian-sign *product-mixture complexity* with explicit block-product, coordinate-product and TV-robust exponents. Existing information-theoretic **nonnegative-rank/common-information** framework is prior art. Do not attribute the initial exponential-mixture idea exclusively to NIGHTFALL; the repo credits a reviewer/collaborator and the Gaussian-sign construction to Aaronson–Ambainis. Full analytic source and assumptions remain separate from finite Lean components.

**Derivative/overlapping work:** arbitrary-product general-degree AA is a genuine scope extension, not the first proof of AA; strong Theorem P bounded-round transfer uses BDST and closely related source-encoding techniques already in GMNR; symmetric orbit row E2 and feature quotients are concise structural consequences of elementary LP averaging, row singleton constraints and Efron–Stein. Keep them as independently reviewable contributions, not first-in-field headline claims.

## Four corrections to earlier portfolio language

1. **GMNR is broader than “only one identical bias”.** Their Remark 3.5 permits `⊗_i Bernoulli(p_{n}^{(i)})` as long as each bias lies in the fixed interval `[a,1-a]`; their Section 3 already has a reversible `2mT` Boolean block compiler. NIGHTFALL's added scope is **arbitrary atom floors incl zero and categorical alphabets / strong bounded-round guarantee**, not the presence of independent biased input bits itself.
2. **Exact log-M in Route 1 is not an algorithm for *learning* an unknown mixture.** The classical tree is nonuniform in a **known** distribution and f, and its claim bounds **query depth**, not preprocessing, total running time, model training or sample complexity. Chen–Moitra, Gordon et al. mixture-learning results should be cited as distinct tasks.
3. **Forrelation is an application of known common-information / nonnegative-rank lower-bound operators.** Do not claim the general `log rank_+(P)≥I(F;G)` mechanism; novelty comparison must be theorem-by-theorem on the Gaussian-sign law, exact/approximate mixture model, and constants.
4. **Polynomial-in-degree variance exponent 2 remains open in Turturean's unrestricted setting.** Special symmetric, categorical degree-two and Boolean-block results are not unrestricted solutions. Ivanisvili's 2026 polynomial BH bound is relevant but does not obviously assert the exact unrestricted AA E2 claim.
