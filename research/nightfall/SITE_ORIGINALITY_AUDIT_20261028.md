## Hard publication rule

**Before adding a new finding to GitHub Pages:**

1. Identify the *specific proposition, integer witness, numerical record, or original controlled observation*, not simply a research theme.
2. Specify exactly what is mathematically/procedurally new relative to the closest pre-existing work, with dated links and a side-by-side comparison.
3. Supply an original primary artifact and separately executable verifier; record its output, hashes, and failure controls. Never call a model-generated proof kernel-checked without a proof assistant kernel run.
4. Show that the novelty claim survives targeted prior-art searches, allowing an explicit “priority unconfirmed” caveat for an original **artifact**, never for a general theorem claimed first.
5. Give the exact scope (finite, conditional, empirical), never transfer a stronger headline from a weaker test.
6. Keep uncertain work in the public **source archive** with honest status, outside the public **findings portfolio**. Link external foundational literature only as references/baselines, not as new NIGHTFALL findings.

A source-content change requires an editorial review of this whitelist. The versioned site checker guards the three approved research links and ensures removed unoriginal pages remain absent. This is a *curation safeguard*, not an automatic machine oracle for mathematical novelty.
