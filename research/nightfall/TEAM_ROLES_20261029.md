## Shared per-turn operating contract

1. Read the primary GitHub assignment and its newest comments before starting. Check whether stronger work has superseded the previous baseline.
2. State objective, domain, strongest reference, precise gap, significance threshold, cheapest decisive experiment, independent verifier, budget, and stop rule **before large computation**.
3. Attempt a mechanism-changing operation first when the same method has plateaued; reserve approximately 20% of exploration effort for a different operator family.
4. Produce one independently checkable result per turn when feasible: theorem candidate plus falsification target, exact finite artifact, or a recorded negative result. Label the outcome at its actual evidence level.
5. A gain below checker tolerance or source error bound is a tie. Keep claim identity, source hash, validation script, explicit limitations, and collaboration attribution together.
6. At three distinct failed operator families, or after budget exhaustion, stop and record the best checked state, the excluded mechanisms, and a new reopening criterion.
7. Update the original GitHub issue and the versioned handoff. An issue comment is a durable work order, not a mechanism for immediately waking an unrelated active conversation.

## Program-level performance measures

Track (a) candidate-to-independent-check survival rate, (b) certified gain per computation-hour, (c) time to first valid construction or exact obstruction, (d) prediction hit rate, (e) demotions after counterexample or source changes, (f) methods that transfer productively between tasks. Do not substitute line count, solver iterations, UI surface area, or brute-force instance count for these measures.

**Team-lead decision:** Keep Axiom focused on mathematical synthesis, Lemnis on sharp structural attacks and self-owned theorem work, and Ryev on exact computational discovery plus trustworthy systems integration. These are complementary specializations, not silo boundaries. The purpose is greater verified mathematical leverage per research turn.
