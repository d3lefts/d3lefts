## Claims and exact certification scopes

- **A: sharp static observation.** On an independent finite-group edge-increment tree, additive edge-relative |f|<=B admits q innovation observations (<=2q original node reads) with squared risk <=B²/(4q). Complete range/variance proof and standalone exact checker check_sharp.py. The variance-range inequality and independent edge generation are classical.
- **B: matching physical lower example.** A genuinely dependent Z3 star with 2q leaves has risk at least B²/(4q) after at most q leaf queries, even with the root supplied for free, including randomized and adaptive stopping with expected budget <=q. Direct conditional-variance proof and independently enumerated physical scenarios.
- **C: reconstruction-only cost frontier.** When the predictor uses only innovations for edges with both endpoints observed, exact minimum risk equals total independent-edge variance minus the maximum weight of induced selected edges. An O(nr²) weighted-tree knapsack recurrence and exhaustive verifier check_frontier.py establish the scope. This is NOT the unrestricted physical Bayes frontier.
- **D: source-ANOVA update.** Pinning independent innovation e transforms canonical product component F_A to F_A+F_(A union {e})(observed e). Exact risk and source-resampling influence equal sums of component energies. A source compiler and a separately structured original-spin enumeration checker test the statement.
- **E: bounded-path influence transfer.** A relative-pair target with each tree path <=D has independent innovation-degree <=D. Composing the pinned finite product influence theorem with the exact potential identity gives at most ceil(4 D^7 B³/(c*eta^(3/2))) innovation reads, each at most two physical site reads, and expected MSE<=eta. The inherited c*=sqrt(3/12500)/pi^6 is historically Lean-checked under #317/#325. The new composition is NOT newly kernel certified.
- **F: noncommutative path identity.** Ordered products of independent edge increments/inverses equal physical relative labels. Separate S3 checks cover noncommutative cases.
