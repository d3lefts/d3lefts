# External peer-review guide

**Research lead:** Devin J. Wright. Please cite the claim ID and the exact failing assumption whenever challenging a statement.

### Suggested assignment

**Referee A — abstraction / inference.** Verify (a) observation-fibre factorization and the restricted protocol decoder condition; (b) the maximal sound guard with the correct good/bad polarity; (c) two-world stochastic and interactive quantifiers; (d) the distinction between measured and universal collateral.

**Referee B — authorization / composition.** Check provenance-summarization requirements, history truncation, controlled/uncontrollable effects, counterfactual reach and closure ordering, graded-review equivalences and complexity classification against source literature. Seek counterexamples and validate the refuted frontier correction.

**Referee C — implementation / empirical methods.** Review SourceBound's generator/admission separation, independent binding, browser visibility, temporal reconciliation, source revocation, and the natural-web experiment's design. Rerun the bug-fixed unit suite and archive manifest after obtaining the frozen 116-file ZIP. Confirm that seen reviewer cases are regression-only.

### Evidentiary labels

- **Proved in manuscript:** mathematical statement under declared assumptions; external referee scrutiny pending.
- **Finite reproduced:** an exact checker or test suite ran over a limited population.
- **Archived reported:** a previous run was documented; its bytes and environment may require new reproduction.
- **Conditional:** some cited/attested premise remains external.
- **Proposed:** experiment not yet executed.
- **Retracted:** a known counterexample invalidates a statement.

### Specific adversarial review tasks

1. Construct a protocol class whose view suffices informationally but where the class lacks the induced decoder.
2. Recompute the two-world total-variation bound under a shared postprocessing rule.
3. Construct local observations whose joint view determines a label while no single local veto can.
4. Check closure of each reference configuration independently; test whether closing the union creates fictional counterfactual capabilities.
5. Attempt a source triple mutation where unchanged evidence misleadingly appears to support a changed entity or qualifier.
6. Test version replacement, identity-link invalidation, stale temporal claims and CSS-hidden material.
7. Audit paper novelty against prior security, control, automata and information-extraction literature.

### Submission

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/issues/new) with claim ID, reproduction steps, source citation, observed mismatch and proposed correction. Reviewer names and acknowledgments require reviewer consent. A response that merely finds no counterexample does not upgrade a claim to theorem or external verification.
