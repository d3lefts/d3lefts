## Priority corrections

1. **Restricted-class converse:** informational sufficiency alone does not guarantee a decoder exists inside a chosen protocol class. A two-world counterexample disproved the unqualified converse. The corrected boundary proof is archived.
2. **SourceBound truth scope:** byte digest and witness show source identity and a restricted assertion. They do not supply universal external fact-checking.
3. **Capability frontier:** a stated inequality for ρ(τ) was refuted using C={a,b,c}, with {a,b}→τ, budget m=1 and realized J={a,c}. The comparison remains excluded.
4. **CaMeL interpretation:** reconstructed source/reader inference omits causal data/control dependencies; admissions on reference calls do not show attacker-induced successful effects.
5. **Origin attestation:** publisher metadata and agent-authored descriptions remain adversarially controlled when appropriate. The provenance label is only as credible as its source and granularity.
6. **Originality:** the factor theorem, weighted set cover, classical automata and two-point bounds are pre-existing mathematics. A new proof arrangement is not a new general theorem.

**Current status:** peer-review candidate; finite checks and test fixtures rerun; natural-web independent evaluation, full implementation mirror and external referee signatures open.
