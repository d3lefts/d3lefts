# Dossier 36 — Intrinsic recovery of glued planar maps

**Editorial snapshot:** 2026-10-30 · **Evidence:** mathematical theorem candidate in unmerged PR · **Originality:** unreviewed.

## Statement under audit
[Axiom PR #203](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/203) proposes a uniform intrinsic-recovery theorem for glued normalized quadratic planar maps over F3^n, n≥3. The source asserts that heavy affine hyperplanes and selected codimension-two marked sections identify the two planar-map components up to exchange under the full ambient affine group. Its proof operator combines nondegenerate scalar polar forms, character orthogonality and incidence-intersection counting.

## What exists
The PR supplies a written universal argument, explicit counts and finite adverse cases. The 2026-10-29 [review queue](../../REVIEW_TRIAGE_20261029.md) prioritizes a **non-author** proof of the scalar-fiber inequality, a full quantifier check of all heavy sections, recovery under arbitrary ambient affine equivalences, negative control reproduction and a same-assumptions search.

## Limits
Finite projective/planar-map computations do not settle the n-uniform conclusion. The result does not improve global Sidon density, solve an Erdős problem, or establish first publication priority. All claims here describe the proposed draft, not an independently accepted theorem.
