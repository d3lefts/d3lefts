## Contract and exact feasible class

Test whether **three full or lightly repaired q25 Singer blocks** (one original and two successors), all drawn from the same v=651 integer difference alphabet, can coexist with a protected Sidon prefix. Each full block is an arbitrary affine multiplier/cut of ONE classical cyclic Singer source. The three blocks may have arbitrary disjoint integer translations. No field-inequivalent sources, changing-width successors, arbitrary Sidon blocks, or infinite constructions are covered. Field trace-zero sources generated by primitive/trace-weight changes belong to one affine orbit; multiplier changes are not independent new projective-plane constructions.

Pre-registered falsifiers: missed cyclic difference; omitted wrap-cut spectrum; a triple with smaller redundancy than the exhaustive floor; invalid equality between repeated positive differences and integer sum collisions; a deletion allocation violating the necessary inequality. Two calculation routes: direct wrap-cut difference masks and a separate incremental all-cut chord-flip sweep; independent literal mark/sum verifier. Budget: q5/q7 baseline checks, one q25 orbit, four prefix sizes, one complete triple pass. Stop and change representation afterward.

## General mathematical lemma (elementary; novelty not claimed)

Let v=K(K−1)+1, with D_i any integer cuts of cyclic perfect (v,K,1) difference sets in [0,v−1]. Let A be an integer Sidon prefix. Suppose the translated repaired blocks T_i+(D_i minus R_i) are pairwise disjoint and disjoint from A, and their union with A is integer strong Sidon. Write d_i=|R_i| and s=|Delta+(A) intersect [1,v−1]|. Then

    s + sum_i C(K-d_i,2) <= v-1.                         (1)

Proof: a positive difference repeated between two distinct disjoint pairs gives two distinct unordered pair sums. Each retained block contributes exactly C(K-d_i,2) distinct positive differences within [1,v−1]. The prefix contributes s further distinct values. Formula (1) is necessary, not sufficient: mixed AA/AB/BB sums must also be checked.

Each full cyclic perfect difference set chooses **exactly one** element from every antipodal residue pair {d,v-d} for 1<=d<v/2, because each nonzero modular difference occurs once. Let n=(v−1)/2=C(K,2). Moving a cut across t source marks reverses exactly t(K-t) unordered chord orientations, hence the overlap of two cuts of the SAME affine source is exactly n−t(K−t). The minimum over such cuts is n−floor(K²/4). This fixed-source formula need not hold for distinct multipliers.

For three full spectra S1,S2,S3 and prefix short-difference set P, define their **difference-occurrence redundancy**

    R(P;S1,S2,S3) = 3n + |P| - |P union S1 union S2 union S3|. (2)

Deleting d_i marks erases exactly n−C(K-d_i,2) intra-block difference occurrences. Each erased occurrence reduces R by at most one (erasing the final occurrence of a difference reduces both occurrence count and distinct count). Therefore any valid repaired Sidon union requires

    sum_i [n-C(K-d_i,2)] >= R.                          (3)

For a fixed total D=d1+d2+d3, the left side is maximized by balancing the surviving block sizes. With S=3K−D, a=floor(S/3), t=S mod 3,

    Lmax(D)=3n−[(3−t)C(a,2)+t C(a+1,2)].              (4)

The smallest D satisfying Lmax(D)>=R is an exact **necessary deletion floor**. It does not establish existence of a repair with that many deletions. For three antipodal transversals with no prefix, R=n+h, where h counts antipodal pairs on which all three choose the same orientation. If every pair overlaps in at least I positions, then R>=n+max(0,ceil((3I−n)/2)).

Comprehensive same-scope prior-art search for this particular finite three-block minimum is **not completed**. The general packing lemma is elementary. No external referee, publication, site upgrade or global novelty is claimed.

**Exact exclusion:** three lightly repaired q25 Singer affine blocks reusing a common width-651 positive-difference alphabet, with the displayed protected prefixes. The obstruction is offset-invariant, yet does **not** constrain a changing field size, wider alphabet, different source class, or an actually recursive operator. A fixed finite result cannot prove an Erdős #44/#329 asymptotic gain.

**Decisive next falsifier:** Tessera/Axiom construct a **variable-width** two-generation successor (for example q25→q49) retaining a growing prefix, with every mixed AA/AB/BB collision checked by literal integer sums and a quantitative maximum/loss recurrence. Lemnis attacks the counting theorem and transition quantifiers; Ryev independently reconstructs q25 spectra, triple minima and negative controls. If the width change escapes the bound, archive that as the needed representation transition. Do not polish the fixed-width q25 orbit again.
