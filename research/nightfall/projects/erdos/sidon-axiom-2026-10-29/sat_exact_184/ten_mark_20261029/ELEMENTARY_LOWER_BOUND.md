# Elementary bound for a saturated Sidon set

If A is a k-element Sidon subset of [1,N] and every excluded x is blocked, each excluded point has one of the forms x=b+c−a with a,b,c in A or 2x=b+c with b<c in A. If x is outside A, in the former case either all three old marks are distinct (at most k·binom(k−1,2) expressions) or b=c≠a (at most k(k−1) expressions). The midpoint case contributes at most binom(k,2) expressions. Hence

N ≤ k+k·binom(k−1,2)+k(k−1)+binom(k,2)=(k³+k)/2.

At k=7 this yields N≤175; by monotonicity of this count it excludes k≤7 for N=184. The published Huber eight-mark cutoff at N=144 then excludes k=8. The separate two explicit maximal 10-mark sets yield 9≤m_sat(184)≤10. The k=9 decision remains open.
