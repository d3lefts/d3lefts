# LEMNIS — Nonabelian universal deck-factor extension of the cyclic K3,3 cycle certificate

29 October 2026 · Parent issue #47 · Existing draft PR #57 · **INTERNAL, mathematical novelty and external review OPEN** · Research ownership Devin J. Wright.

## Quantified theorem

For **any finite group H generated by h,k** and any integer m≥1 with **147|m or m≥257**, form the regular K3,3 cover on vertex triples (u,x,g), u=0,...,5, x in Z/m, g in H. Edges between left 0,1,2 and right 3,4,5 use the ordered four nontrivial voltages

- (1,4): (1,e)
- (1,5): (5,e)
- (2,4): (17,h)
- (2,5): (64,k)

The other five base edges carry identity voltages. The cover is connected, simple, cubic and bipartite, has 6m|H| vertices, has **girth exactly 12**, has **no 16-cycle**, and has an explicit simple 32-cycle. The first power-of-two cycle length is 32.

## Proof

The regular-cover voltage group is Z/m × H. The chord (1,e) generates the cyclic factor, while the other two nontrivial H chords permit extraction of (0,h),(0,k); these generate all of H, establishing connectedness. The base is K3,3 so the lifted graph is simple, cubic and bipartite.

Every simple lifted cycle projects to a cyclically nonbacktracking base walk with identity deck voltage. Projection to the cyclic factor gives (1,5,17,64). The previously archived **complete base-walk certificate** excludes zero modular voltage for lengths 4,6,8,10,16 modulo 147. This excludes the same lengths whenever 147|m. Independently the complete integer-voltage spectrum at these lengths has no zero and absolute value ≤256; therefore exclusion also holds for every m≥257.

Two base words have total **integer cyclic voltage zero**:

- C12: 031405130415
- C32: 03140523041325140314031403140314

The C12 word uses only chords (1,4),(1,5), with identity H factors. The C32 word has H word k^(-1)k=e: the (2,4) chord never occurs and the remaining nontrivial H factors cancel, with all intervening factors equal to e. Thus **both words close in the full group H, without commutativity**. Their 12 and 32 lifted states are already pairwise distinct in the quotient cyclic graph modulo 147, so stay distinct in the full group for 147|m; their integer fiber ranges are [-5,1] and [-64,1], respectively, so stay distinct for m≥257. These are simple cycles; hence girth is 12 and the first power-of-two length is 32.

## Scope, priority, and stop

This is an **infinite family** over arbitrary two-generated deck groups, including nonabelian groups. It is **not** a counterexample to Erdős–Gyárfás #64 (it contains an explicit C32); it gives **no global bound** on arbitrary cubic graphs. The transfer is an elementary quotient-cover mechanism and is **not claimed as a worldwide original theorem**. It extends NIGHTFALL's cyclic 882-vertex base to the nonabelian group category; the result is structurally distinct but mathematically reducible to the prior cyclic quotient, so it falls **below the Original Finding significance gate** pending a genuinely new mechanism.

Historical comparison must consider classical voltage coverings and cubic girth-12/16-exclusion constructions. Relevant 2026 works include Daniel Garcia (arXiv:2609.04686) and Julius Tranquilli (arXiv:2608.02675). Dated prior-art check: Erdős #64 remains OPEN as of 29 October 2026.

Next operator: seek **non-split semidirect or nonregular** cover where positive-word closure is controlled by a nontrivial commutator identity; if it reduces to direct-product factor inflation, stop.
