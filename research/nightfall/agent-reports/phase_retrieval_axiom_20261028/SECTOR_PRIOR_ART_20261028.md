## Primary precedents checked today

1. Tejasvi Singh Tomar, *Undistillability of Werner states from three, four and five copies*, first posted 23 October 2026, https://www.mefyi.com/papers/werner-states-copies/ . Four-copy qutrit undistillability is proved for `alpha >= -0.4979`; the unrestricted endpoint `alpha=-1/2` remains open. His framework uses exact rational/moment certificates and the rank-two matrix criterion, but the displayed results do not provide this specific graded nine-word four-copy certificate.
2. Bharti, Gajjala, Haug, *Two-copy nondistillability of Werner states: sharp partial-trace inequalities and finite-copy extensions*, arXiv:2607.24479 (27 July 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24479 . Establishes sharp two-copy results and finite-copy structural extensions; warns that tensor-factor projections can increase matrix rank. Our grade-projection negative control respects this pitfall.
3. Song and Chen, *A partial-trace matrix inequality and Werner-state distillability*, arXiv:2607.23416 (26 July 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23416 . Provides related partial-trace rank-two inequalities and two-copy undistillability, distinct from the present four-copy graded restricted witness family.
4. Lluis Eriksson, *Bell and tripartite anchor sectors for finite-copy Werner witnesses*, working paper ARR-2026-16NB5DD5M18TTBET, 25 October 2026, https://airr.science/ . Establishes strictly positive restricted sectors built from Bell/GHZ anchors. Those are directly relevant to restricted-family priority comparisons; no exhaustive inclusion comparison with the new grade-resolved class has yet been performed.

**Disposition:** these checks substantiate the distinct *scope* of the present calculation, but they do not establish global originality. The mathematical operator-grading/Cauchy refinement is elementary and likely has general antecedents; claim only a new, independently replayable NIGHTFALL *certificate extension*, not a first-of-kind theorem. A proper priority screen must compare every restricted witness set and identify any equivalent projector decomposition or published grade-resolved bound.
