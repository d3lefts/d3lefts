# Odd-cycle obstruction for anchored sign-square codes: the p=23 capacity gap

**31 October 2026.** NIGHTFALL Constellation. Research direction: Devin J. Wright. Mathematical derivation and proof draft: GPT-6 (AI collaborator), building on Tessera PR #267 and Axiom PR #279. **Status: R1 internally proved theorem candidate, not independent human-reviewed, not global novelty-cleared.** This is *not* a new discovery about the elementary independence number of odd cycles; its application and sharp p=23 classification are the in-program contribution.

## Precise setting

For odd prime p and r≥1 let K=GF(p^r), write its additive group G=(F_p)^r, and use the single legal observation y=(a_0+∑_{j=1}^b a_j η_j)^2 with anchored relative signs η_j∈{−1,+1}. The coefficients are fixed once coordinate squares (hence magnitudes and support) have been read; the same row is applied to every sign vector. The target is to distinguish all 2^b outputs. The orbit alphabet has (p^r+1)/2 elements, but an affine sign cube has additional *central symmetry*.

## Lemma 1: quantitative overlap on odd additive cycles

For any A⊆G and any nonzero g∈G,

    |A ∩ (A+g)| ≥ |A| − ((p−1)/2)*p^(r−1).                  (1)

**Proof.** The permutation x↦x+g has p^(r−1) disjoint cycles, each of length p. For a single cycle, with n occupied vertices and e adjacent occupied directed edges, at most p−n occupied-to-empty directed edges exist, so e≥n−(p−n)=2n−p. Since e≥0, e≥max(0,2n−p)≥n−(p−1)/2. Sum over the cycles. QED.

Equivalently, if A and A+g intersect in at most one element, delete an endpoint of the possible unique adjacent edge. The remaining occupied vertices are independent on all p-cycles; at most (p−1)/2 per cycle, plus the one deleted point.

## Theorem 2: corrected capacity ceiling for centrally symmetric square codebooks

If C⊆K has distinct elements and is centrally symmetric around a∈K (C=2a−C), and the map z↦z² is injective on C, then

    |C| ≤ ((p−1)/2)*p^(r−1) + 1.                         (2)

Therefore, for the **one-square-query anchored sign model**, any b-bit code has

    b ≤ floor(log2( ((p−1)/2)*p^(r−1) + 1 )).            (3)

**Proof.** Symmetry gives −C=C−2a. Square injectivity implies C∩(−C)⊆{0}; in particular, its cardinality is at most one. If a=0 and |C|≥2, then −C=C, contradiction; otherwise g=−2a≠0 and Lemma 1 gives |C∩(−C)|≥|C|−((p−1)/2)*p^(r−1). Rearrange. A legal affine sign cube C={a_0+∑ a_jη_j} is centrally symmetric around a_0. If the b-bit squared map is injective, it has 2^b distinct pre-square values, so (3) follows. QED.

**Scope warning:** The theorem does not constrain arbitrary nonlinear codebooks, repeated/adaptive mixed queries, or the entire state-recovery problem beyond this anchored single-row sign block. It improves the ordinary raw output-alphabet upper bound **for centrally symmetric codebooks only**.

## Corollary 3: exact classification at p=23

At p=23,r=2 the new upper bound is

    |C| ≤ (22/2)*23 + 1 = 254 < 256 = 2^8.

Thus no 8-generator binary sign cube, of **any additive shape**, can be distinguished by one squared-linear query. The original proposed requirement of a *missing point* of T+T is categorically impossible here, but the stronger result also excludes a unique-diagonal-only sum.

The lower bound b≥7 is realized explicitly in G=F_23² by

    C = { (x,y) : 1≤x≤8, 0≤y≤15 }.

This is the affine binary cube generated by (1,0),(2,0),(4,0),(0,1),(0,2),(0,4),(0,8), translated by (1,0). Its 128 elements are distinct. No element of C is the negative of another because x+x'∈[2,16] never vanishes modulo 23. Under any additive F_23-basis identification with K, square values are all distinct because u²=v² iff u=±v. The actual mixed observation coefficients are a_j=W_j/2, a_0=c+∑ W_j/2.

Hence b(23,2)=7 exactly, in this frozen model.

**Representation multiplicity version:** For a dissociated 8-generator set W⊆G, T=Σ(W) has |T|=256 and central sum s=Σ_jW_j, so s−T=T. For every h∈G,

    r_{T+T}(h) = |T ∩ (T+(h−s))|.

If h=s this equals 256; otherwise (1) bounds it below by 3. Thus *every* residue is represented at least three times as an ordered two-term sum, so no hole and no singleton diagonal. This is a symbolic, unconditional obstruction for p23; the earlier 279,841-configuration 8×8-tile exhaustive search is superseded as evidence for the global claim.
