## A. Optimal online innovation compiler (task-relative separation)
Let S uniform F2^k, available labels R subset F2^k, query a returns a dot S. Let deterministic policy request labels adaptively from prior requested labels and replies and eventually stop. Maintain a basis of the span of requested labels and their replies. If request in span, compute reply by linearity; otherwise pay one actual original query, insert in basis. This yields exact transcript pathwise, paying rank of all labels on that path. Conditional on the paid transcript, S is uniform on the corresponding affine fiber: deterministic adaptive choices add no new condition. A new independent label is balanced on that fiber, hence its reply is a fresh fair bit. The innovation stream can be represented by independent fair tape up to the stopping time. For a uniform path cap q, if rank(R)>=q, after output is complete optionally pad by q-minus-paid independent original queries using a deterministic choice from R; the complete tape is deterministic from S, exactly uniform on F2^q and costs q total, including padding. If rank(R)<q use rank(R) instead. No free access to the padded tape is assumed. Without padding paid count is the path rank.

Optimality: any deterministic exact compiler with legal original queries, on its own leaf, has an affine secret fiber with direction orthogonal to its queried span. Its fixed output transcript specifies all requested labels and replies on that fiber. Each requested label must be constant on that affine fiber, hence in the compiler's queried span. Thus paid query count >= transcript rank on every input. The compiler above attains this pointwise. This is a standard linear-query principle applied to the NIGHTFALL adaptive transcript interface, not a new parity-query theorem.

Transfer: on the tensor source #1198, polynomial-depth task transcript has a polynomial-size innovation representation with zero Wyner for padded tape. Yet every full-source static representation satisfying #1228 joint approximate reconstruction and recovery has exponential br(1+C_W). The architecture obligations differ: online transcript encoder does NOT reconstruct all 3^L original coordinates. This is a strict representation-score versus task-transcript separation, not a general complexity lower bound for static algorithms. Paper V's one-query (and paper-level two-query) simulation results provide nontrivial polynomial-query task examples when their respective hypotheses hold. No claim of general online speedup over those optimal trees. Next: non-tautological task family where common online policy has provable polynomial cost before a simulator is supplied. Attack: check adaptive stopping, zero labels, duplicate queries, paid padding, pathwise alternative compiler, nonuniform secrets (fresh bits fail in general). Exact rank cases in checker.

## B. All-fixed-order primitive fiber localization, sharp at pure frequencies
For q>=2, let R(q,L) be all pure nonzero tensors from (F2^q minus 0)^L. Unique factor representation follows because F2 has no nontrivial scalar. For a nonzero f with a minimum-cardinality XOR representation B of size k<=m, let A_m(f) be all linearly independent sets A of at most m rows with XOR f. The minimum B is independent (otherwise delete a nonempty zero-sum subset).
Claim: |A_m(f)| <= sum_{j=0}^{min(L,m+k-2)} binom(L,j) sum_{u=1}^m binom(k(2^q-1)^j,u), with out-of-range binomials zero. Thus O_{q,m,k}(L^(m+k-2)) and O_{q,m}(L^(2m-2)). The count includes all primitive sizes up to m; ordered tuples add at most m!.
Proof: A symmetric difference B sums to zero. Greedily delete inclusion-minimal nonempty zero-sum sets; each is a linear matroid circuit over F2, so remainder still sums to zero and decomposition is disjoint. Every circuit meets both A and B, by independence of each. Lovitz–Petrov Corollary 21 (arbitrary field) says a size-s circuit varies (local span dimension >1) in at most s-2 tensor factors. Over F2, dimension one means equal nonzero factors. For each circuit anchor at a B row. The union J of varying sites has size <=|A symmetric difference B|-2c<=m+k-2 if at least one circuit. If A=B choose empty J. Every A row agrees with some B row outside J, including rows in the intersection. For fixed J there are at most k(2^q-1)^|J| candidate rows; count subsets and union-bound over J. End proof.
Sharp special case k=1: for pure f, choose m-1 distinct sites. Starting with f, split one current row at each chosen unused site by replacing factor v with two distinct nonzero a,b with a+b=v; possible q>=2. All previous rows have v at this untouched site. The replacement preserves independence: project the site modulo span(v), where a,b have equal nonzero image; a putative dependence must use both new rows or neither, reducing to an old dependence. All splits preserve the XOR; each chosen site has nonidentical factors thereafter because an eventual replacement at a different site preserves the factors in this site. Distinct chosen site sets therefore yield distinct irredundant decompositions. There are binom(L,m-1) choices, proving Theta_{q,m}(L^(m-1)) as L grows for fixed m, including m=1. Our generic exponent need not beat #1114's sharper m=4 bound for arbitrary f.
Decisive negative control: in q=2 with f=(1,...,1), for every tail v in {1,2,3}^(L-1) other than the tail of f, the four distinct rows {f,(1,v),(2,v),(3,v)} have XOR f. This yields 3^(L-1)-1 nonprimitive four-row supports. Thus one cannot use a polynomial all-support multiplicity bound at fixed order even for pure f. Quantum path cancellation is essential, not a technical detail. Next operator: classify zero-sum circuit blocks on 2T path labels, then prove contracted Hilbert norm bounds for their coherent contributions (cf. #1068's actual four-slot Möbius cancellation); primitive localization alone does not establish general all-T simulation or runtime. Attack: exact F2 circuit decomposition, local factor dimensions, anchor consistency, count exponent, independence preservation after split. Exact primitive comparisons in checker. External splitting theorem is the key dependency, not our original theorem.

## C. Exact task-relative risk transfer through tree leaf moments
Let f real square integrable under finite laws Q and nu on original inputs, and fix one legal deterministic query tree T with leaves l. Define p_l=Q(l), p'_l=nu(l), m_l=E_Q[f|l], m'_l=E_nu[f|l], V_Q=sum p_l Var_Q(f|l), V_nu likewise. At zero-mass leaves choose arbitrary conditional means; products with zero masses vanish. For predictor t with real leaf outputs t_l,
R_Q(t)=V_Q+sum p_l(t_l-m_l)^2, and similarly R_nu. This is conditional orthogonality.
For any kappa>=0 and allowed interval [0,1], uniform transfer R_nu(t)<=kappa R_Q(t) for EVERY bounded predictor on this fixed partition is equivalent to
V_nu-kappa V_Q + sum_l max_{0<=u<=1} [p'_l(u-m'_l)^2-kappa p_l(u-m_l)^2] <=0.
Each maximization requires only endpoints and, if strictly concave and inside interval, its stationary point. This follows because leaf outputs are independently selectable, so the supremum of the sum equals the sum of suprema. Handles zero source mass and singular laws. It is exact, not a loose density-ratio relaxation, and needs only O(number of leaves) moment inputs, though computing those moments can be expensive.
For unrestricted real leaf values, let A_l=kappa p_l-p'_l. Necessarily A_l>=0, and if A_l=0 then p'_l(m_l-m'_l)=0 (otherwise affine unbounded). Under those conditions the criterion becomes
kappa V_Q >= V_nu + sum_{A_l>0} [kappa p_l p'_l (m_l-m'_l)^2/A_l].
Proof: put u=m_l+v. Leaf difference =-A_l v^2+2p'_l(m_l-m'_l)v+p'_l(m_l-m'_l)^2; complete the square. At equality A_l=0 and slope zero the constant is zero (also if both weights zero). This gives exact necessary/sufficient unrestricted risk multiplier, equivalently Gram PSD of the residual subspace span{f, leaf indicators}. Can bisect kappa since valid multipliers form an upper ray. If Q risk can be zero while nu risk positive the multiplier is infinite; do not divide by zero.
Composition: a Q simulator whose tree partition admits the certificate yields nu MSE <=kappa times Q MSE, even if global Radon-Nikodym ratio does not exist. Can use sharper exact risk for that one predictor instead of uniform certificate. Example: shared (a,b) iid fair bits; nuisance z=0 under Q, z=1 under nu. Target f=b, tree reads a, then leaf masses, means, variances match; kappa=1 despite disjoint support and different degree-one nuisance marginal. Nuisance can be replaced by arbitrary correlated blocks independent of (a,b). This is more task-specific than #988 universal marginal conservation and #992 global spectral projection, but requires a known tree and task moments; it is not a non-tautological universal query-complexity characterization. Standard regression decomposition and Schur algebra credited. Next: bound the certificate from access-local couplings without full enumeration of leaves; compare against #1026. Attack: zero mass, zero variance, unbounded vs bounded leaf outputs, disjoint support, conditional mean shifts. Exact checker verifies scalar maximum in 684 cases.

## D. Robust causal consensus obstruction with approximate symmetry
Use exact #1149 Theorem 2 hypotheses: numerable principal G-bundle Z->B, candidate pair maps p_+,p_-, equivariant obstruction kappa_j(h,z) with representation W_j and invariant continuous virtual response eta_j(h,z), nonzero top SW class of associated direct-sum bundle. Assume normed output spaces and actual stage F_j defined on every relevant corrupted history. Replace equality condition at kappa_j=0 by
||F_j(h,p_+(z))-eta_j(h,z)||<=sigma_j and ||F_j(h,p_-(z))-eta_j(h,z)||<=sigma_j.
Then both physical candidates can be forced by adversarial stage noise of norm <=sigma_j to have identical corrupted transcript q(z). Proof: construct invariant virtual history q_j=(q_{j-1},eta_j(q_{j-1},z)) as in #1149; equivariant section has a simultaneous zero z. At stage j, if corrupted histories agree with q_{j-1}, choose noise eta_j(q_{j-1},z)-F_j(q_{j-1},p_pm(z)) for each candidate. Bounds make them admissible, induction forces exact q_j. No Lipschitz factor in history and no accumulation of stage deviations. If total squared noise budget is sigma^2, sum sigma_j^2<=sigma^2 suffices. With candidate pair quotient distance >=Delta, minimax metric error >=Delta/2 (also expected error for randomized decoder at same transcript), squared error >=Delta^2/4 by Jensen. This is deterministic sensing with adversarial per-input disturbances, not a stochastic or randomized sensing lower bound.
Explicit approximately odd/even nonlinear sensor case: globally continuous A_j(h,x), Phi_j(h,z) on all relevant histories; sup ||A_j(h,x)+A_j(h,-x)||/2 <=u_j; sup ||Phi_j(h,z)-Phi_j(h,-z)||/2 <=v_j; Phi_j Lipschitz in z with constant L_j. Use A_j^-=(A_j(h,x)-A_j(h,-x))/2 and Phi_j^+=(Phi_j(h,z)+Phi_j(h,-z))/2. Then norm difference between actual composite and ideal composite <=L_j u_j+v_j, by triangle. Ideal globally continuous odd/even protocol obeys #1149 Theorem 3 if stage dimensions partition into two bins each <=n-1. At its common virtual transcript actual outputs can be forced to ideal outputs with noise sigma_j>=L_j u_j+v_j. Candidate separation R on radius-R ball, error >=R/2. This is a robust structural threshold in symmetry defect/noise, not a claim that arbitrary approximate sensors remain noninjective without noise or that exact odd/even is necessary. Global corrupted-history domain cannot be dropped; attainable-only exact-history extension in #1149 alone is insufficient for actual perturbed protocol. Next: stochastic noise with pathwise uniform conditional closeness or controlled KL, not inferred from a single virtual transcript. Independent audit: exact group hypotheses, history legality, sign symmetrization and Lipschitz domain, adversarial quantifier order. No new independent numerical tests needed for this elementary transfer; no priority claim.

## E. Horizon-independent terminal tracking through geometric error allocation
Use #1208 Theorem A/B actual model: known law X on finite original coordinates, nested releases, globally [0,1]-valued degree<=d pullbacks of every state under every fixed admissible past control sequence; same innovations for true and approximate-control trajectories, scalar states in [0,1], deterministic history-selected tasks. Assume uniform source dichotomy private exponent p>1, energy constant a0>0, info drift coefficient b0>0. Let a=p-1 and private stage cost bound A tau^(-a) from #1208, where A depends only on d,a0,p. At tau=1/4 no phase/query is necessary. The theorem gives a deterministic uncapped causal policy with stage conditional MSE<=tau_j and expected total paid count <=sum_active A tau_j^(-a) + C_W(X)/(b0 min_active tau_j^3). No extra information charge per stage. Use valid conservative certified source constants if substituting for paper coefficients; whole causal composition remains paper-level.
Reference feedback and approximate feedback obey |z_{j+1}-s_{j+1}| <=rho |z_j-s_j| + c |hat{s}_j-z_j|, with same starting state, 0<=rho<1, c>=0, as in #1208. Desired target only terminal state H. Let weights w_H=1, w_j=c rho^(H-1-j) for j<H (0^0=1). Set theta=2a/(2a+1), S=sum_{w_j>0} w_j^theta, W=max_j w_j. Then S<=1+c^theta/(1-rho^theta), W<=max(1,c), independent of H. For requested terminal RMS epsilon>0, set e_j=min(1/2,(epsilon/S)w_j^(-1/(2a+1))) for positive weights and 1/2 for zero weights, tau_j=e_j^2. Stages with e_j=1/2 are skipped (posterior mean, no new query). All other thresholds fit #1208. By Minkowski and feedback recurrence,
||hat{s}_H-s_H||_2 <=sum_j w_j ||hat{s}_j-z_j||_2 <=sum_j w_j e_j<=epsilon.
This uses no independence of errors and no union bound over H. On active stages e_j=(epsilon/S)w_j^(-1/(2a+1)), so sum_active tau_j^(-a)<=(S/epsilon)^(2a) sum_j w_j^theta=S^(2a+1)epsilon^(-2a). Also min_active e_j>= (epsilon/S) W^(-1/(2a+1)). Therefore expected query count <=
Q*=A S^(2a+1) epsilon^(-2a) + [C_W(X)/b0] (S/epsilon)^6 W^(6/(2a+1)).
If no active stage count is zero. This bound has no explicit horizon factor (C_W(X) itself may depend on H). For quadratic p=2, private epsilon^-2; general p=5/2 private epsilon^-3; common term epsilon^-6 in both. Constants depend on d,rho,c and actual source theorem, not number of stages or original coordinate count. Earlier horizon-wide uniform tolerances paid private sum over stages; the terminal objective allows geometric allocation. If the global Wyner charge grows with H, it is still charged once, and no absolute horizon-independence claim is made about that source. For a deterministic cap m, couple policies until abort; P(abort)<=Q*/(m+1); terminal squared loss <=1 on abort, so MSE <=epsilon^2+Q*/(m+1). For terminal error threshold epsilon0 and failure delta, run uncapped parameters epsilon=epsilon0 sqrt(delta/2) and cap m+1>=2Q*/delta, then Markov on uncapped plus abort gives <=delta. No free observation of actual state. No runtime claim.
Sharp boundary: all-deadline accuracy cannot generally have a horizon-independent total query budget even at rho=0, C_W=0. Let s_j=X_j fresh independent unbiased bits, no feedback, reset system F(x,u,xi)=xi, degree 1. For epsilon0<1/2, any exact-to-tolerance all-time tracking with deterministic cap m has success <=min(1,2^(m-H)): every final leaf fixes at most m independent bits and its output sequence can be correct on at most one assignment of the remaining bits. Thus success >=1-delta implies m>=H+log2(1-delta). This separates terminal fading-memory cost from online sustained information acquisition. Next: replace global C_W by window/relevant conditional information, prove matching lower bounds in contractive nonreset systems. Attack: zero weights, rho=0, saturated tolerances, horizon-dependent C_W, actual feedback control paths, Markov cap and distinct terminal vs uniform tracking. Exact allocation test uses rho=1/8,c=1,p=2 and rational powers.
