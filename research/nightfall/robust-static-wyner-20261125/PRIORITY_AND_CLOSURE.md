## 1. Robust representation audit
The five-step proof in PROOF.md v0.2 is complete under its explicit joint reconstruction/recovery event, including finite alphabets and general Wyner auxiliary. The key property is isolation of each heavy-coordinate fiber, not isolation of the entire valid support. The low-coordinate mode is measurable by fixed tie-breaking, and independent of heavy coordinates conditional on W. List capacity comes from actual binary decision tree image sizes. List-KL data processing is classical, as in Sason (2019) and Sakai (2020). No inference from marginal reconstruction success alone; no dimension-free lower bound for arbitrary task-specific compression; distortion measured against code minimum distance. The user should not describe the theorem as solving classical query lower bounds. A useful extension already in v0.2 is arbitrary nonuniform source by replacing L/M with top-L mass. If encoders/decoders use a common independent random seed whose average joint success is >=1-eta, one may fix a seed with success >=1-eta when the joint (X,Z) law is seed-independent; if the latent law itself changes with the seed, the conclusion is only for the selected conditional law, not the original mixture. No blanket randomized-model claim.

## 2. New author-side theorem: noisy equivariant consensus through a common Gaussian reference
Use the precisely scoped #1149 Theorem 2 certificate: invariant virtual history, equivariant stage obstructions, nonzero top Stiefel–Whitney class and pair maps with metric quotient separation >=Delta>0. The topological argument supplies a fixed pair x_+,x_- and common ideal response vector u=(u_1,...,u_m) with virtual prefixes u_<j. Actual sensing stages F_j(h,x) defined on every corrupted Euclidean history and output dimension l_j, deterministic sensing policy. Assume actual mean deviations at the virtual history satisfy ||F_j(u_<j,x_±)-u_j||<=a_j. Assume for these two inputs that ||F_j(h,x_±)-F_j(u_<j,x_±)||<=g_j||h-u_<j|| for all corrupted histories h; Euclidean concatenated history norm. Independent additive Gaussian noise N(0,sigma_j^2 I_lj), sigma_j>0. Zero-dimension output stages can be omitted. This includes the symmetrization construction in FIVE_FRONTIERS.md D, with a_j=L_j u_j^{odd defect}+v_j^{even defect} (rename virtual response to u_j to avoid ambiguity).
Put V_<j=sum_{i<j} l_i sigma_i^2 and A=sum_j (a_j+g_j sqrt(V_<j))^2/sigma_j^2. For the true transcript laws P_+,P_-,
TV(P_+,P_-) <= sqrt(1-exp(-A)).
Hence every possibly randomized decoder has worst-case expected quotient metric error >= (Delta/2)(1-sqrt(1-exp(-A))), and worst-case expected squared quotient error >= (Delta^2/4)(1-sqrt(1-exp(-A))).
Proof: Let R be the independent product Gaussian law of fake responses Y_j=u_j+Z_j with the same noise covariances. Under R, E||H_<j-u_<j||^2=V_<j. Chain rule for reverse KL and equal-covariance Gaussian divergence yield
D(R||P_±)=sum_j E_R||u_j-F_j(H_<j,x_±)||^2/(2 sigma_j^2)<= A/2,
where Minkowski's L2 inequality proves each bound. Every law has positive density in its finite-dimensional Euclidean transcript space, since all conditional Gaussian densities are positive. Write r,p_+,p_- for densities. The Hellinger affinity beta=int sqrt(p_+p_-) equals E_R exp[-(log(r/p_+)+log(r/p_-))/2]. Jensen gives beta>=exp[-(D(R||P_+)+D(R||P_-))/2]>=exp(-A/2). Cauchy–Schwarz applied to (sqrt(p_+)-sqrt(p_-))(sqrt(p_+)+sqrt(p_-)) yields TV<=sqrt(1-beta^2). For any decoded output y, triangle gives loss_+(y)+loss_-(y)>=Delta and squared losses sum>=Delta^2/2. Integrate these inequalities with the common subdensity min(p_+,p_-) (mass 1-TV), averaging over decoder randomness conditionally on the transcript, then take the larger of the two risks. End proof. All KLs are nats. This is a stochastic lower bound, not an exact collision of the random observations, and does not require or imply any adversarial noise. At A=0 the two noisy laws coincide exactly, as expected. Nontrivial quantitative strength depends on small accumulated normalized feedback sensitivity; no claim of dimension-independent positive constant at large A. The pair is fixed before the random Gaussian draws; randomized sensing policy not covered. Source priority: adaptive KL/Le Cam method classical (Arias-Castro, Candès, Davenport arXiv:1111.4646); Gaussian amplitude-based phase-retrieval minimax literature exists; proposed contribution is choosing the hypothesis pair through causal topological consensus and using a common reference to avoid unstable propagation under either true law. No identical statement located. Non-author attack: absolute continuity, reverse chain direction, Jensen sign/factor, Euclidean variance, deterministic versus randomized sensing, quotient metric.

## 3. New author-side theorem: terminal tracking with only window-marginal Wyner charge
Use #1208 actual-law staged simulation and #1228 Section E geometric error allocation. State s_j in [0,1], known deterministic feedback pi_j, independent of source law except via state, with closed-loop map G_j(s,xi)=F_j(s,pi_j(s),xi) uniformly rho-Lipschitz in s, 0<=rho<1. (The earlier rho=L_s+L_u L_pi suffices.) Assume every trajectory with arbitrary fixed starting state in [0,1] and arbitrary fixed admissible control sequence has globally [0,1]-valued degree<=d pullbacks of its state to original coordinates of subsequent released innovations. Disjoint time blocks of original coordinates, known marginal laws; no free future queries. This additional fixed-start assumption is essential, not implied by degree bound merely on a restricted correlated support. If only full-horizon pullbacks are supplied, use #1228 E unchanged. Aim terminal RMS accuracy epsilon in (0,1), not all-time tracking or physically intervening on the original system.
Choose w>=1 with rho^w<=epsilon/2 (for rho=0 take w=1; for 0<rho<1 take max(1,ceil(log(epsilon/2)/log(rho)))). If H-1>=w, start at j0=H-w with a fixed state s_bar, e.g. 0; use no queries before j0. Define surrogate reference trajectory by the same closed-loop map on actual suffix innovations, from fixed s_bar. For every realization, induction gives |s_H-surrogate_s_H|<=rho^w|s_j0-s_bar|<=epsilon/2. The suffix simulator needs only the known marginal law X_win of innovation coordinates from j0+1 through H; all its predictions and query choices depend only on paid suffix histories, so its law under the full source is exactly its law under X_win. Apply #1228 E to this surrogate controlled system with terminal RMS budget epsilon/2, using the actual-law Wyner definition on X_win and fixed-start degree hypothesis; ignore original prefix completely. Minkowski gives full terminal RMS<=epsilon. If the horizon is shorter than w transitions (including H=1), use the original theorem on the full source including initial-state coordinates, no fictitious reset. Thus with same fixed contraction and degree constants,
expected paid queries <= A0 S^(2a+1)(2/epsilon)^(2a)+C_W(X_win)/b0*(2S/epsilon)^6 W^(6/(2a+1)),
where a=p-1>0, S<=1+c^(2a/(2a+1))/(1-rho^(2a/(2a+1))), W=max(1,c), and c is the feedback error coefficient from #1228 E. If full horizon shorter than w, use C_W(full source), still a short-window source. Deterministic cap m: use previous cap coupling with abort probability <=Q*/(m+1), terminal squared loss <=1, yielding MSE<=epsilon^2+Q*/(m+1). Constants use any valid source dichotomy from Paper VI; causal composition remains paper-level. This removes the previous implicit global history dependence through C_W(X). In particular one may have a divergent total source Wyner information but uniformly bounded window common information. Simple example: independent pairs of duplicate fair bits as successive innovation blocks, one bit of Wyner information per two-coordinate block; all H independent blocks have H bits by the exact affine-code support/free-coordinate identity #1095, while a w-block suffix has w bits. Can use affine contractive system s_{j+1}=alpha s_j+beta u_j+gamma mean(block) with nonnegative coefficients summing <=1 and a Lipschitz feedback such that rho<1; fixed-control pullback remains degree 1. Uniformly bounded per-block information gives O(log(1/epsilon)) window charge, regardless of H. This is illustrative, not a lower bound or claim of exact optimal dependence on epsilon.
Priority: Boyd–Chua (1985) fading memory and finite-memory approximation; state estimation under contraction classical. The proposed contribution is paid original-coordinate query complexity with actual marginal Wyner term, not geometric contraction or window truncation alone. No same-model theorem found in targeted search. Independent audit: short horizon, rho=0, suffix law and query legality, fixed-start degree, same-innovation coupling, actual vs surrogate controller, cap and tolerance. The all-time reset lower bound in #1228 remains valid. No new exact experiment needed for this elementary pathwise argument; no independent review or formalization.
