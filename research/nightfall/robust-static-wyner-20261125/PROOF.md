## Theorem candidate
Let C be a finite binary code of length N, cardinality M>=2, minimum Hamming distance D. X is uniform on C. Let Z have arbitrary joint law with X on a finite latent product alphabet. Let E encode Z to N bits, output j depending on a fixed set S_j of at most b latent coordinates. Let D_i(x) be a deterministic binary-query decision tree of depth at most r, defined on all binary inputs, and write R(x)=(D_i(x))_i. Assume positive integer b,r, integer 0<=2t<D, and
Pr[d_H(E(Z),X)<=t and R(X)=Z] >= 1-eta,
0<=eta<1/2. In particular exact latent recovery and TV approximation of the observed code are covered by suitable coupling; this is a joint success assumption, not a separate per-coordinate average.

Define s_i=|{j:i in S_j}|, H={i:s_i>=D-2t}, h=|H|; then h<=bN/(D-2t). Define L=min(M, product_{i in H} |D_i(C)|), with empty product 1. Since every binary depth-r tree has at most 2^r distinct outputs, L<=min(M,2^(rh)). Use bits throughout.

For every finite or general latent W making Z coordinates conditionally independent, there exists a W-measurable list of at most L codewords with success probability >=1-2eta in a coupling preserving (X,Z), W->Z->X. Thus:
I(W;Z) >= (1-2eta) log2(M/L) - 1.
For eta<=1/4 replace 1 by h_2(2eta). At eta=0 the entropy penalty is 0. Infimize over actual Wyner auxiliaries; nonnegativity can be combined with the bound. For any explicit K-component product mixture for Z,
K >= (1-2eta) M/L.
These statements are insensitive to the ambient latent alphabet size. The cardinality bound is not a time lower bound.

## Proof
1. V is the set of z for which some x in C satisfies R(x)=z and d_H(E(z),x)<=t. The x is unique since 2t<D; call it F(z). On the joint success event, X=F(Z). The probability Z outside V is at most eta.
2. Fix all high coordinates H. The fiber of V over them is isolated in the remaining categorical product: if two distinct valid tuples z,z' differ only at a low coordinate i, the encoder outputs differ in at most s_i positions. Triangle inequality gives d_H(F(z),F(z'))<=2t+s_i<D. Thus F(z)=F(z'), and R(F(z))=z=z', contradiction.
3. For a product law Q on finite low coordinates and any isolated set U, choose a joint coordinatewise mode a (first tied maximum). Then Q(U minus {a})<=Q(U complement). Proof from #1195 H1: sort each marginal p1>=...>=pq, decompose it into uniform initial prefixes with weights j(p_j-p_{j+1}), p_{q+1}=0. Product gives a mixture of uniform rectangles all containing a. A nonsingleton rectangle has at most half its mass in U because an axis fiber of length >=2 contains at most one U-point. Singleton rectangle contributes no mass to U minus {a}. Average. Empty low coordinate set is immediate.
4. Conditional on W=w, Z is product. Fix a high tuple and apply step 3 to its isolated low fiber. Integrate over high tuple (independence leaves the same low product and low mode a(w)); then integrate over W. Thus Pr[Z in V, Z_low != a(W)]<=Pr[Z outside V]<=eta. Modes are measurable by finite tie-breaking. Extend the original joint (X,Z) by sampling X from its original conditional law given Z and independent of W given Z; preserves both marginals, giving W->Z->X. The chance of joint success AND Z_low=a(W) is at least 1-2eta by union bound.
5. Define list A(w)={F(z):z in V, z_low=a(w)}. Distinct valid z with fixed low tuple can differ only in high values, each belonging to D_i(C). Hence |A(w)|<=L. Step 4 gives Pr[X in A(W)]>=1-2eta. Set e=Pr[X not in A(W)]. Entropy chain rule with error bit yields H(X|W)<=h_2(e)+(1-e)log2 L+e log2 M. Data processing and H(X)=log2 M imply I(W;Z)>=(1-e)log2(M/L)-h_2(e), giving the stated uniform weaker bound; for eta<=1/4 monotonicity of binary entropy on [0,1/2] gives sharpened penalty. This holds for any general auxiliary with conditional product kernels, without identifying W with a finite component count. For finite K lists, uniform X gives Pr[X in union_w A(w)]<=K L/M and at least 1-2eta, proving cardinality. End proof.

## Tensor corollary
From #1198/#1199, depth L0 tensor character code has N=3^L0, log2 M=k=2^L0, D=2^L0. Set t<=alpha D, 0<=alpha<1/2, s=br, c=1-2eta>0. Then log2 L<=min(k,s (3/2)^L0/(1-2alpha)), hence
C_W(Z)>=max(0,c (k - s (3/2)^L0/(1-2alpha))-1)
K>=c 2^[max(0,k - s (3/2)^L0/(1-2alpha))].
In particular for s polynomial in L0, C_W=Omega(2^L0) and K>=2^[(1-o(1))2^L0]. Write A=(1-2alpha)(4/3)^L0<=k. If s>=A/2, then s(1+C_W)>=A/2. Else s>=1 and 1+C_W>=c k/2, so s(1+C_W)>=c A/2. Thus for fixed alpha,eta the exact score obstruction persists exponentially in the relaxed model. All C_W in bits; multiplication by ln2 converts to nats, changing only constant factors. This is a representation-family obstruction, not an actual query complexity lower bound. Fractional distortion is relative to minimum distance, NOT ambient length (D/N decays exponentially). No arbitrary lossy or task-specific representation impossibility. At eta=1/2 the parity-code identity encoder with uniform product Z and maximal coupling to uniform parity attains joint success 1/2 and C_W=0; universal threshold cannot include that endpoint.

## v0.2 — full paper proof strengthening, 2026-11-25
The preceding five steps are a complete written proof of the general statement. We now sharpen Step 5 without an entropy slack. Let L* = min(M, product_{i in H}|D_i(C)|), and p=1-2eta. Write J for the joint law of (X,W) constructed in Step 4 and J0=P_X tensor P_W. The measurable list event B={(x,w):x in A(w)} has J(B)>=p and J0(B)<=L*/M. Binary relative-entropy data processing gives D(J||J0)>=D_Ber(J(B)||J0(B))>=D_Ber(p||L*/M) whenever p>L*/M: binary KL is increasing in its first argument and decreasing in its second when the former exceeds the latter, by direct differentiation, with boundary cases interpreted by continuity. Since W->Z->X, I(W;Z)>=I(W;X)=D(J||J0). Thus actual general-latent Wyner common information is at least D_Ber(p||L*/M) for p>L*/M, and at least zero otherwise. This improves the earlier entropy bound by the nonnegative term -(1-p)log2(1-L*/M), in the nontrivial KL regime. At p=1 the exact bound is log2(M/L*). For any finite K, K>=ceil(pM/L*). A coarser explicit L* upper bound is min(M,2^(r floor(bN/(D-2t)))). Measurability: finite coordinate alphabets, finite fixed tie order, and conditional probability kernels of finite Z; all mode and list choices are finite measurable operations. No assumption of a finite Wyner auxiliary. If one starts only from a TV approximation to the observed marginal, a suitable coupling must ALSO obey joint latent recovery; the TV statement alone is not sufficient.

Nonuniform extension: for arbitrary source X supported on C, let S_l be the sum of its l largest atom masses. The same construction gives I(Z;W)>=D_Ber(p||S_L*) if p>S_L*, and finite mixture must satisfy S_min(K L*,M)>=p. This uses the same product-reference event argument with J0(B)<=S_L*. No claim that source entropy alone controls the bound.
