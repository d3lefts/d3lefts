# NIGHTFALL Proof Passport v14 — Source-Compatible Incremental Factorization (SCIF)

**Date:** 14 November 2026 (US Eastern) · **Lead:** Devin J. Wright · **Construction:** AI-assisted · **Status:** scoped finite original-source Lean theorem; deployment economics and priority unresolved.

## Problem, representation, exact claim

When one coordinate law of a finite independent product changes from p_k to q_k, how much canonical decomposition, influence information and certificate material can be reused without rebuilding the full model? Preserve the *original source function* f; do not replace it with a synthetic interaction table.

The terminal theorem `Nightfall.SCIFCompleteOriginalSource.scif_complete_finite_original_source_terminal` (merged [PR #529](https://github.com/d3lefts/d3lefts/pull/529)) takes actual independent finite-product p and f, a common finite nonempty alphabet, normalized marginals including zero-mass atoms, and genuine original-law degree ≤2.

It derives (i) new-law degree ≤2; (ii) pointwise canonical pair/interaction-graph invariance; (iii) canonical-pair recentering; (iv) true source-influence marginal updates, both changed and unchanged coordinates; (v) changed-law variance/risk bound; and (vi) fixed-owner new-law amplitude and certificate cost bound with multiplier `1 + min(||q_k-p_k||_1,1)`, **conditional on an old-law source-derived owner amplitude envelope**.

## Mechanism, operator, certificate

Project source f onto original canonical Hoeffding singletons and pairs. Perform one-coordinate recentering under the replaced marginal and transfer the changed-law pair projections and influence directly by signed marginal integration. Apply clipped total-variation control to an unchanged owner assignment; avoid claiming a cost-optimal owner algorithm.

## Independent adversarial audit and limits

Attack deterministic/zero-mass atoms, an edited coordinate whose support changes, graph components with exact pointwise zero pairs, nonzero singleton shifts, and a fixed owner with high local degree. Distinguish a strict Lean kernel certificate from independent human review (none). No optimal cost certificate, no `O(deg(k)a²)` verified implementation, no heterogeneous alphabet *types*, infinite product, authenticated source state, cloud/plant effect-time enforcement, or worldwide priority result.

## Transfer and next decisive test

Connect this recentering kernel to source-valid semantic version fences: a law change should invalidate exactly the authenticated observation/certificate dependencies it touches. Build a source-matched recertification workload that counts instrumentation, reads, verification, lock contention, retries and stale-token denials against a full rebuild under identical safety obligations.
