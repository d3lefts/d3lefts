# NIGHTFALL | South Alabama MSSE readiness and thesis simulation
**Status:** Independent, AI-assisted pre-enrollment research and competency portfolio. **Not** University of South Alabama coursework, a transcript, academic credit, a GPA, an awarded master's degree, a committee assessment, or a defended thesis.
**Snapshot:** 2026-11-13. The current repository may evolve after this snapshot.

## Purpose
Organize the existing NIGHTFALL program into a legitimate, independently examinable **pre-master's readiness portfolio** tied to the University's published *thesis-option* Systems Engineering M.S. (MSSE). Use it to secure appropriate provisional admission, earn actual graduate grades after enrollment, and propose a supervised thesis. Do **not** describe a course here as taken at South Alabama or an automated test as faculty assessment.

**Official degree structure** (30 institutional credits, which this portfolio cannot award):
- **15 core:** SE 500 Engineering Probability & Statistics; SE 601 Systems Engineering Fundamentals; SE 602 Risk and Failure Analysis; SE 603 Integration, Test & Evaluation; SE 606 Systems Architecture.
- **3 research:** SE 609 Engineering Research Methods.
- **6 elective:** candidate SE 613 Decision Analysis and SE 614 Lifecycle Cost Analysis, subject to advising and actual offerings.
- **6 thesis:** SE 599 Thesis in Systems Engineering; requires responsible-conduct-of-research training and an oral defense.
Sources: [current university catalog](https://bulletin.southalabama.edu/programs-az/engineering/systems-engineering/systems-engineering-ms/systems-engineering-ms.pdf), [department course listing](https://www.southalabama.edu/colleges/engineering/se/curriculum.html). Confirm the entry term's bulletin with the program coordinator.

## One thesis, three components
1. **Structural Observability:** specify task-relevant distinctions and architectural separators; source-level quadratic-influence result remains conditional and under independent review.
2. **Information Acquisition:** enforce typed and priced oracles; prove exact finite observation-cost baselines and attempt a general matched upper/lower complexity frontier.
3. **Lifecycle Assurance:** specify source-to-claim certificates, authenticated version changes and the cost of safe revalidation.

## What may truthfully appear on a university application
- An independent **research portfolio**, with reproducible finite calculations and links to signed commit histories.
- Self-directed study *mapped to* SE courses; not coursework, grades, academic standing, or a GPA.
- A proposed supervised master's thesis and a clear record of unsettled mathematical claims.
- Reported GRE scores only if verified against the official score report and test-validity window.

## What must not be claimed
No simulated transcript, fabricated faculty evaluation, surrogate credit-hour completion, guaranteed admission, automatic Master's-to-PhD transition, verified world-first Q2 theorem, universal Lean certificate, accepted publication, or human referee review.

## Immediate real-world pathway
Request provisional MSSE admission (published minimum undergraduate GPA 2.5 with three recommendations), select the **thesis option**, confirm a faculty adviser and project scope, and aim to earn a genuinely strong institutional graduate GPA. The self-directed evidence matrix is preparation, not a replacement for enrolled performance.
