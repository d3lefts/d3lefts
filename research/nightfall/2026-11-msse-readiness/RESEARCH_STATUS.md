## Status terminology
- **Exact finite check**: enumerated tested cases, not a universal theorem.
- **Lean-scoped certificate**: only the particular compiled declaration, environment, inputs and axioms.
- **Conditional analytic**: conclusion relies on an unproved or externally provided mathematical premise and/or strong oracle.
- **Candidate**: plausible statement with unresolved correctness or novel scope.
- **Novelty unreviewed**: neither an originality claim nor evidence of first discovery.
