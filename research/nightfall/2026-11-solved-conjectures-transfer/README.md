## Load-bearing transfer: noncommuting posterior projection averages

Source: [agnostic VC proof, Section 2](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/d72a880f3f88bb8e38646ad8cb92789cae696a64/results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/PROOF.md), blob 5596685b851969db1800a62fff8963c5b46db5b3. Let q be any fixed finite law on N input coordinates; H=L2(q), or its finite-dimensional Hilbert-valued extension, P_J=conditional expectation onto X_J, K_m=average P_J over uniform m-subsets. Nested conditional sigma fields imply 0 <= K_m <= K_M <= I. The upstream overlap estimate is ||(I-K_M)^(1/2) K_m^(1/2)|| <= 1/sqrt(k) when k*m <= M <= N.

### Variance-sensitive deductions (not novelty-cleared)
For A=K_m and B=I-K_M:
1. A B A <= (A-A^2)/k.
2. B <= (I-A)^2+(A-A^2)/k.

Proof: choose k independent uniform m-subsets J_s, independently pad their union uniformly to size M, obtaining uniform V by permutation invariance. For any y in H set Z=(sum_s P_{J_s}y)/k. Each term lies in ran(P_V); E Z=Ay; E||Z-Ay||^2=(<y,Ay>-||Ay||^2)/k by independence and orthogonal projection. Best approximation to Ay by P_V yields (1), taking expectations. Best approximation to y gives (2), since E||Z-y||^2=||Ay-y||^2+E||Z-Ay||^2. No commutativity or product-law assumption. The first inequality implies the upstream bound on ran(A) by conjugation with the support pseudoinverse square root; the stronger bound is already implicit in the source variance calculation, so credit the source and do not count this as independent novelty.

Physical-query interpretation: drawing J_s and padding V is independent of the data. At most M actual distinct coordinates are observed, and P_V y is a permissible estimator only with known law/target and unrestricted computation. Conditional expectation is not an extra oracle. This is a nonadaptive result for each fixed law; it can be restarted at a positive-probability transcript using its posterior, but provides no posterior-uniform lower bound on gain, no adaptive chain rule for entropy, no low-degree leverage by itself. For uniform independent bits, parity y=(-1)^(sum X_i) has K_m y=0 for every m<N. Its risk remains 1, and I(y;X_J)=0 for every proper J even though all N bits reveal y. Thus a generic dimension-free target recovery or information decay inference is false. Even degree-one y=X_1 on fair bits has uniform m-subset residual (N-m)/N times its variance; targeted query choice matters.

### Nonuniform design and latent-information bridge (same-session role pass, not independent review)

The proof does not require uniform J_s or padding. For any data-independent distribution mu on admissible subsets of size at most m (including at a fixed transcript posterior), let A=E_mu P_J and let C=E P_U for the union U of k independent draws. Then |U|<=km and A(I-C)A <= (A-A^2)/k, I-C <= (I-A)^2+(A-A^2)/k by the identical sample-average variance argument. The selector may depend on past transcript, not on unread coordinates. This is a design-sensitive physical-query lemma, not a dimension-free selector existence theorem.

For a finite latent Theta and full input X under a fixed transcript posterior, use the vector y(X)=Pr(Theta=.|X). For any fixed U, P_U y=Pr(Theta=.|X_U). Therefore I(Theta;X | X_U)=E KL(y || P_U y). If all positive-prior latent categories have posterior predictive mass (P_U y)_theta >= a>0 a.s. for every allowed U, then log t <= t-1 implies KL(p||r)<=sum (p_theta-r_theta)^2/r_theta, hence

E_U I(Theta;X | X_U) <= a^(-1) <y,(I-C)y>
 <= a^(-1) [||(I-A)y||^2 + (<y,Ay>-||Ay||^2)/k].

Nats, finite latent alphabet, vector L2 norm with counting measure over latent categories. This is an explicit residual-mutual-information transfer with a physical query budget and an additional floor assumption; it does not improve the general Route 1 rate without a bound on bias and the floor, and a floor can degrade with latent support or adaptive conditioning. A positive prior alone does not imply the floor. For zero predictive mass the corresponding full posterior mass is zero a.s.; nevertheless no uniform inverse-floor bound follows without additional structure. This deduction uses standard conditional expectation and KL/chi-square inequality, not a novelty claim.

Exact noncommutativity check: in lexicographic 3-bit cube order, weights (1,5,4,5,1,6,6,1), [K_1,K_2]_(0,1)=-13201/1575288, using the archived checker matrices. Thus a simultaneous diagonalization proof would not be sound even for a tiny finite input law.
