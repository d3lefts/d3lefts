## Proof review by dependency
1. Exact feasibility: for x in ker B compare x and zero even for nonlinear transfer. Im F must lie in im C. From P F Q=F, CT_* B=F. For any exact T, T_*=C† C T B B†, compression by orthogonal projectors on V and U; rank and induced norm cannot increase. rank F ≤ rank T_* ≤ rank F. All degenerate cases including zero matrices handled.
2. Minimax lower: for every x in unit ball, orthogonal forbidden output gives one bound; for hidden x in unit ball, the errors at x and -x have sum ≥2||Fx||, also in expectation with common observation-conditioned random law. No continuity or measurability of deterministic g needed. Upper: in normalized block coordinates E*E ≤ I-D*D and GG* ≤ I-DD*. E annihilates ker Δ by quadratic form inequality. K=EΔ† is contractive by testing Δ†u on ran Δ; similarly L=Γ†G. DΔ=ΓD by rectangular SVD; the Julia operator maps forbidden-output ⊕ hidden-input to hidden-input ⊕ forbidden-output, with identity diagonal products and vanishing off-diagonal blocks. Thus residual [-KD*L,E;G,D] is contractive. Scale back. If t=0 all fixed blocks vanish, choose residual zero. Empty summands work without modification. Alternate check from Sou/Rantzer strict feasibility: for any s>t, obtain supported correction with residual norm <s; these corrections lie in a bounded finite-dimensional closed subspace, so take a convergent subsequence as s↓t. This uses established theorem, not a second independently authored proof.
3. Compression: in Frobenius inner product the allowed block is orthogonal to the other three. All rank≤k allowed blocks lift by C†MB†, so apply truncated SVD. Rank zero and rank exceeding matrix size use zero/unchanged truncation. The second-moment expectation identity is a trace identity; no claim of optimal nonlinear encoders.
4. Noise: exact feasibility implies FB†B=F and C T_*=FB†. Error exactly FB†e up to sign, so upper δ||FB†||. Two hypotheses ±B†e with opposite admissible noise yield same observation zero. Since FB† vanishes on (im B)⊥, maximizing e can lie in im B. If δ=0 choose zero error. If feasibility fails, scale hidden or forbidden-output witness with e=0 to get infinite worst-case loss. For unrestricted x only, δ≥0.
5. Continuation: induction on row-generation length gives W_h span of L M_w. Stagnation gives invariance forever; otherwise dimension increases by at least one, hence stabilization by N-rank L. Zero L separately trivial. Since W invariant, ker W invariant; conversely any invariant subspace inside ker L lies in ker W. R_s+R_t TB=0 equivalent to all words. Apply exact feasibility with F=R_s,C=-R_t. Generic delayed-detection example is for arbitrary joint linear update; does not assert sharp horizon separately for every prescribed block-diagonal source/recipient dimension pair.
6. Robustness: for orthonormal basis R, row closure gives R M_a=R M_a R*R; L=L R*R. For ordered products consistent with chronological evolution, repeated substitution gives H0 K_w residual. Induced norm submultiplicativity establishes the stated bound; for word length zero use empty product 1 even when γ=0. Uniform guarantee requires quotient stability, not full-space stability. Minimax is for signature norm, not claimed for worst actual future task output. PSD certificates equivalent to induced Euclidean norm bounds; with a rational nonorthonormal basis use a positive-definite metric and weighted PSD tests. All bounds apply only to declared fixed linear operators and domain.

No invalid theorem found in v0.1 at stated scope. Proof dependencies classical pseudoinverse/SVD, triangle inequality, PSD square root, finite-dimensional dimension growth, induced norm and Frobenius orthogonality. No proof scope weakened.
