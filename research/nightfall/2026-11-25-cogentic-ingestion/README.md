## NIGHTFALL research transfers (proposed, not automatic implications)

1. **Log-rank / conditional dependence:** highest mathematical priority. Source-reported monochromatic rectangle mechanism is relevant to structural observability, Q2 and Wyner, but matrix rank, communication bits, rectangle density and original-coordinate query cost are different resources. For deterministic communication transcript W, each transcript fiber is a rectangle; under product law, conditioning on a positive-probability fiber preserves product structure. Under correlated law, it need not. Counterexample: X=Y uniform on N labels, constant all-ones acceptance matrix of rank one, constant transcript W. Residual Wyner common information log N: conditional independence on diagonal support forces each product component to be concentrated on one shared label. This prevents an unjustified rank-only bound on source Wyner cost. Proposed test: use exact finite rational distributions, compare rectangle conditioning and original-coordinate query costs, distinguish source law from task matrix. No proof of the new external KL-stability theorem attempted.
2. **Graph reconstruction:** compare indistinguishability from unlabeled edge cards with our structural observation/phase retrieval fibers and graph certificates. Explicitly distinguish known labels vs unknown graph isomorphism, entire multiset vs individual observations, uniqueness vs efficient recovery. First test: tiny graph deck equality, relabeling and omitted multiplicities controls; does not test source m>=1000n theorem (small graphs cannot meet that threshold).
3. **Compression:** isolate selected sample plus side information, reconstruction cost and consistency domain; compare #1150 query-local recovery and #1182 worst-case sharpness without claiming VC compression implies adaptive oracle access. Countercheck a representation with cheap encoding but expensive recovery.
4. **Fictitious play:** source describes single-copy reuse with in-place reset rather than exponential recursive duplication. Relevant to representation-sensitive dynamical simulation and operator scaling. Benchmark state dimension, helper count and exact reset invariant, not elapsed convergence alone. Fixed tie rule and initialization are part of the problem. Do not infer general dynamics theorem.
5. **Online inverse optimization** (harness §3.1): source replaces log determinant potential by trace inverse-square-root with self-normalized rank-one metric updates, to obtain a horizon-independent budget. Candidate transfer to structural observation/active-query metrics; verify actual inequality with noncommuting matrices before using it. Harness reports efficient proper O(d) regret and cites later inefficient optimal O(sqrt(d)); not a new result of ours.
6. **Other papers:** maintain source watchlist for explicit success/error and resource contracts; no implementation of external number-theoretic algorithms in this checkpoint.

Primary new operator: **isolated fragment salvage**. A rejected proof may contain a correct reusable lemma, but neither acceptance nor rejection of the parent suffices to classify it. Each fragment carries source attempt ID/hash, explicit statement, dependency scope, falsifier, review result, checker receipt, reuse/invalidation constraints and author credit. Separate unreviewed candidates from promoted ledger. No cache reuse on strengthened claim, enlarged feasibility, changed assumptions, missing source or timeout. Inherit existing proof passport and fail-closed comparator requirements, not a new competing status ontology. Example from #1205: the certified-negative reuse premise is a reusable fragment even if the broader production-performance claim is rejected; controlled synthetic schedule savings are not an observed production gain. No new kernel proof or worker execution in this ingestion.

## Cheapest decisive experiment (preregistered design only)

Objective: isolate whether fragment salvage improves verified mathematical progress under a matched budget. Feasible set: preselected frozen archived proof attempts, equal base model, context/time/token limits, same verifier and independent review criteria. Baseline: full attempt record plus existing verified ledger. Variant: isolated fragment extraction/recheck plus same record. Freeze candidate lemma statements and counterexamples before seeing both arms. Include corrupted dependency, strengthened hypothesis, shared false lemma and revoked-source controls; zero tolerated invalid promotions. Randomize order and blind review of outputs; record all failures and costs, not just accepted lemmas. Primary metrics: independently accepted reusable lemmas per total metered inference cost, later independently accepted proof completions, and false promotions. Secondary: time, duplicate effort, context size and exact reuse dependencies. Small pilot: 12 matched pairs, no statistical generalization without power review; reserve 20% exploratory budget. Stop immediately on false promotion, missing provenance, or mismatch in frozen interfaces. Significance: improved validated reuse or cost versus matched comparator with no lost validity; no claim based solely on generated volume. Ryev can implement checker, Lemnis specify falsifiers, Vesper coordinate, Tessera test transfer; these are proposed roles, not dispatched tasks.
