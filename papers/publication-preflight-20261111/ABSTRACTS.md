## 1. Sharp quantum SLD tensorization

For faithful finite-dimensional average states with condition numbers kappa_i, we determine the exact coefficient C_d = 2^(1-d) product_i(1+kappa_i)/(1+product_i kappa_i) in an inverse symmetric-logarithmic-derivative (Jordan) tensor operator inequality. A locally prepared independent ternary-input qubit construction attains the bound in optimal scalar quantum Bayesian squared-error gain. Fixed positive local Gram visibility and bounded spectral condition numbers cannot, without extra structural assumptions, eliminate the additional exponential interaction-order loss. Conversely, Hermitian simple-tensor contrasts obey a factor-one SLD inequality, so binary-input Hoeffding components avoid this extra loss, though a^d may remain necessary. The general analytic argument has exact finite falsification checks but no independent specialist review or globally verified priority.

**Referee request:** Independently reconstruct the spectral operator minimization and Personick Bayes optimum, attack the Hermitian orientation/Jensen argument, and check for existing equivalent inequalities in noncommutative metric literature.

## 2. Copy-dependent qutrit NPT Werner undistillability

Let Q_k(t,C) be the partial-trace polynomial associated with the partial transpose of a k-fold qutrit Werner state, evaluated on arbitrary complex rank-at-most-two coefficient matrices C. Subject to the precise published sharp two-/three-copy endpoint inequalities and the proposed separable-spectator extension, our proof produces a copy-dependent protected parameter t_k=1/3+0.31040996/k for **every finite** k>=3. An exact rational odd factorial-series majorant leaves a strictly positive certified algebraic margin. This does NOT yield a fixed NPT state undistillable for all k. The imported source endpoints and spectator lifting require independent specialist proof review.

**Referee request:** Check the full arbitrary-rank-two published endpoint hypotheses, U tensor conjugate(U) spectator contractions, three-site response identity, odd-tail bound and parameter conventions against the most recent Werner literature.

## 3. Quadratic influence on arbitrary independent product laws
