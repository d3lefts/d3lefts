This is a researcher-authored pre-referee package, **not** external peer review, global novelty certification, or permission to submit. Positive CI tests are scoped to the checks they execute.

## A. Quantum SLD (first referee circulation candidate)

* Independently diagonalize the Jordan/SLD superoperator J_b(X)=(bX+Xb)/2 for nonuniform spectra, and minimize the exact inverse tensor ratio. Test mixed local spectra, maximally mixed factors, and Hermitian sharpness.
* Derive the optimal scalar quantum Bayesian squared-error gain directly from arbitrary POVM minimization. Check physical positivity, normalization and local SLD Gram in the ternary-qubit construction.
* Adversarially test the Hermitian simple-tensor factor-one inequality, especially mixed diagonal/offdiagonal eigen-index pairs and the orientation-averaged Jensen denominator.
* Compare exact condition-number formula and physical saturation with Personick (1971), Cao–Lu (2019), Hirche–Rouzé–Stilck França (2022), Tsang (2023), and other quantum monotone-metric/operator inequalities.
* Stop if a published equivalent operator inequality removes priority, or if any physical/gain normalization fails. Preserve narrower results rather than suppressing contrary evidence.
