## Reader rules

1. A green CI build has only the exact scope of its invoked workflow; it does not replace a referee.
2. A formal theorem's imported premises, source commit, and theorem declaration are inseparable from its certification.
3. Conditional results retain all source-premise gates; a failed proof strategy does not refute a conjecture.
4. Mathematical validity, reproducibility, novelty, significance, and publishing permission are **separate** dimensions.
5. This shelf does not modify the frozen historical 40-record research catalogue or 11 historical proof passports.

**Submission readiness:** None of these paper entries is represented as externally peer reviewed or worldwide priority-certified. The papers with incomplete primary source are expressly blocked from referee-ready submission until source custody is repaired.
