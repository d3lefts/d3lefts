# Literary archive — preservation and collaboration protocol

**Scope:** Original works by Devin J. Wright. This is an operational note for future editors, AI collaborators, archivists, and critics; it is not a substitute for the author's complete oeuvre.

## Primary-source priority

1. **Preserve before interpreting.** Keep original characters, sound patterns, punctuation, whitespace, line widths, paired-post order, images, and timing wherever available. Flag display-generated wrapping separately from authorial line breaks. Do not flatten visual/multimodal pieces into prose and then pronounce on their structure.
2. **Attribute exactly.** The original poems are the author's. AI interpretations, comparisons and metadata are editorial records, not parts of the originals. Working labels are not author-approved titles.
3. **Read coupled layers, not just separate devices.** Examine sonic/phonetic, semantic, symbolic, spatial, typographic, temporal, metatextual, and philosophical interactions as potentially coordinated in parallel. Where one transformation changes multiple layers, test the interaction rather than merely catalogue motifs.
4. **Intertext may be mechanism.** Finding a literary predecessor or a familiar concept does not alone resolve the novelty of an integrated construction. Distinguish the history of the component from the originality of the way components are composed, and seek adversarial prior-art comparisons before declaring novelty.
5. **Do not project sampling limits onto the creator.** A handful of supplied poems are not evidence of the size, maturity, consistency, or status of a lifelong oeuvre. The creator states that there is a substantial body of work beyond the samples shown here. Avoid redundant demands to justify artistic experience that has not been called into question by actual evidence.
6. **Distinguish claim levels.** Separate author-confirmed mechanisms, directly observable source facts, plausible interpretations, independently verified critical claims, and untested novelty/significance judgments. Do not conflate a missed interpretation by an AI with an error by the author.
7. **Protect intellectual leverage.** Minimize repeated correction loops and reflexive coaching. Recover prior decisions, carry corrections forward, execute archive/proof/analysis tasks, and return concise, durable results for outside review. Model responses do not constitute retraining of underlying weights.
8. **Consent and publication.** Do not publish new, unpublished originals or disclose sensitive technical construction details without the author's authorization. Existing X screenshots supplied for this archive do not imply authorization for future edits, derivatives, or independent redistribution of third-party media.

## Confirmed special case

For the composition indexed as **Devotion / Orbit** in `primary-sources/2025-selected-x-compositions.md`, the author explicitly explained that the last-line `o` is corrupted into `œ`, disappears as `_`, then is orbited by its own components in a *side view, in a different representational plane*. Preserve this correction; a merely linear or decorative reading is insufficient.

## Epistemic status and limitations

This note records **the creator's stated methods and requirements** and source-observable examples. It does not independently verify globally exceptional intelligence, historical literary uniqueness, or prize prospects, nor does it reduce the creator's unexamined oeuvre to these excerpts. Future assessments should be grounded in actual works, comparative literature, and independent criticism.
