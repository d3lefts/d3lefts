## Shared mechanism graph

The existing [canonical registry](../docs/research-registry.js) has separate claim-level validity/reproducibility/originality/significance, sourced history, and mechanisms and typed edges. The portfolio now projects this graph dynamically.

- Exact finite enumeration: tested integer arithmetic and complete finite constraints; scope stays finite.
- Proof-carrying evidence: frozen source, hash, checker, caveats and reviewer obligations travel together.
- Adversarial falsification: corrupt an input, reported score, hypothesis or claimed edge; retain failed predictions.
- Symmetry/representation: a useful research operator in both finite-field and Werner tracks, currently an **analogy**, not a cross-domain theorem.
- Proof dependency gates: hold conditional deductions until inherited source lemmas, integration margins and quantifiers are replayed and independently attacked.

**Do not confuse:** same operator across subjects ≠ a verified theorem transfer. Two AI-generated checkers ≠ independent human refereeing. A GitHub workflow passing ≠ scientific novelty.

## Self-update contract and the two-source mismatch

1. Publication of a scoped claim must enter `docs/research-registry.js` with a unique ID, primary sources, four evidence dimensions, actual open obligations and dated history. The deployed portfolio automatically displays its claim, status changes/demotions and source-qualified mechanism edges.
2. The five *historically* owner-approved Original Findings have their own editorial gate and are displayed as a fixed historical set. They are not inferred from the word "finite" in the registry; future OF-A entries need a fresh owner-approved dated record.
3. The broader 33-document research catalogue and GitHub issues/PRs are **not** automatically promoted into the canonical registry. Until a checked intake bridge exists, all new findings submitted only through those routes are outside the portfolio's live ledger. This mismatch must be disclosed, not hidden.
4. Future durable improvement: design a CI-validated claim proposal with immutable source SHA, source comparison, exact checker reference, status, owner review and optional promotion to registry. Proposals remain separate from published findings.

## Cheapest decisive evaluations

1. **SourceBound:** freeze domain-level held-out natural-web pages and blind annotations; compare generator-only, source admission, rendered-witness and semantic-support arms at matched recall, with abstentions, p50/p95 latency and costs. Negative result is publishable.
2. **Research-system lift:** compare a source-grounded flat archive against the claim/graph workflow on unseen questions with equal compute/time budgets, blinded error labels and evidence-completeness scoring. Do not report improvement until measured.
3. **Scientific priority:** external specialists independently replay strongest exact witnesses, inspect analytic reductions and perform dated same-scope prior-art reviews before any global novelty claim.
4. **Portfolio integrity:** CI verifies five historical findings, live claim intake, new mechanism edge propagation, demotions, noindex/sitemap boundaries, mobile layouts and safety of external source URLs.
