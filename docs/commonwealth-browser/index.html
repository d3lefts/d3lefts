    04 / PUBLIC EVIDENCE · NO PERSONAL DATA

    Records and source custody

    What constituents should eventually be able to inspect: original documents, precise motion questions, named public roll calls, valid-time boundaries, corrections and explicit evidence gaps. This preview does not fetch or certify any official source.

    READ-ONLY FIXTURES

    Open government, protected constituents

    Official public records may include named elected-official votes where legally public. Individual residents' questionnaire choices, private dates of birth and residential histories are not public records by default and are never exported from this demo.

    05 / STEWARD · INSTITUTIONAL ADMINISTRATION

    Authority gates, not shortcuts

    Administrators would reconcile legal sources, configure institution-approved rules, verify eligibility, maintain corrections and separately approve publication. Nothing in this local browser can perform those actions.
