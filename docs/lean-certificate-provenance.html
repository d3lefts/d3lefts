What quarantine means

Quarantine does not retract kernel compilation. It prevents a routine or inherited mathematical step from being presented as an original theorem or as evidence for a stronger, uncompiled conclusion. Every source and historical CI receipt stays available. Some specialized formalizations may be new code without proving a new mathematical fact; worldwide priority remains unverified for every row.

Separate, eligible passports: finite heterogeneous-product influence and concrete finite certificate transfer describe dependent program-specific results, not novel classical lemmas. Three-query F16 phase block is a concrete finite witness with a kernel-checked uniqueness certificate; no same-model global originality finding has been made.
