01 · Exact theorem statement

FINITE COMPUTATIONAL PROOF · k=4 ONLY

A permutation is a 4-shuffle if its positions can be partitioned into four disjoint increasing-index subsequences, all of the same size t ≥ 2 and all order-isomorphic. A factor is a consecutive block, considered up to relative order. Let N4 be the smallest N such that every permutation of N distinct entries contains a 4-shuffle factor.

N4 = 20

Stronger upper claim: every 20-entry permutation has a qualifying factor of length 8 or 12. The length-19 witness below avoids all eligible 8-, 12-, and 16-entry factors. The conclusion concerns consecutive factors—not arbitrary selections of a larger permutation—and does not resolve the conjecture for unrestricted k.

02 · Why every 20-permutation contains one

COMPLETE FINITE GRAPH / EXACT COVER
01 / INHERITEDClassify eight-entry patterns

Enumerate all 105 perfect pairings. A four-shuffle of four pairs exists exactly when either the inversion or noninversion graph admits a perfect matching.

02 / GRAPH5,040 states and 2,632 edges

States are standardized length-seven permutations. The edges are precisely the length-eight patterns that fail both pairing tests.

03 / COMPLETE SEARCH302 possible bad-eight paths

Acyclicity and longest-path computation enumerate every 13-edge walk corresponding to a length-20 permutation with no eight-entry shuffle.

04 / FORCED CERTIFICATEAll 302 contain a twelve-shuffle

Exact cover finds four triples with a common relative order within a consecutive 12-window; their comparisons are forced by local eight-windows.

The upper computation does not arbitrarily fill in unknown comparisons between distant positions. Each twelve-entry certificate uses only comparisons established by overlapping length-eight windows, so it applies to every permutation that realizes the corresponding path.

05 · Attribution, limits and next referee test

NOVELTY AND NON-AUTHOR REVIEW OPEN

Mathilde Bouvel posed the shuffle-consecutivity conjecture. David Bevan, Andrew Elvey Price and Stefan Felsner developed the factor-graph and perfect-matching approach, reporting an upper bound of 26 in the 2024 Oberwolfach workshop report. NIGHTFALL does not claim that established graph method as its own. The candidate improvement is the exact value 20.

The cheapest independent audit is to recompute the 2,632 admissible transitions and enumerate all 302 bad-eight paths without using the included checker, then verify forced triples under an independently written exact-cover implementation. The original 19-term witness should be checked separately. Search dated post-2024 publications for precedence before any novelty or submission claim.

Publication rule: a passing GitHub workflow certifies reproducibility of this finite computation, not external human refereeing, priority, or the full k-shuffle conjecture. The result remains in NULL FIELD · active research until those gates close.
