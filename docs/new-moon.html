THE NIGHT DESK / THREE RULES

A research program
measured by its checks.

Each investigation moves through a claim, a mechanism, a falsifiable test, and a precise limit. The aim is transferable understanding, not an impressive-looking number.

02 / ATTACK

Design an independent counterexample search or proof-dependency audit before claiming success.

03 / CERTIFY

What actually survives?

Separate numerical replay, formal proof, human review, and historical novelty.
