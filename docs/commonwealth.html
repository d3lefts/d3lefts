THE DIFFERENTIATOR / EVIDENCE AS INFRASTRUCTURE

Democratic Assurance Proof Passports

Rather than treating an AI summary or rule-checking dashboard as authority, the proposed legal layer ties every conclusion to exact document versions, effective dates, quoted premises, explicit exceptions, and inspectable derivation steps. A changed source triggers re-evaluation of the affected dependency chain.

RECORDS, REPRESENTATION & THE LIMITS OF INFERENCE

The historical intelligence layer

Commonwealth's back-end research already models source adapters, immutable local records, dated office tenure, issue-to-question matching, and exact named member positions. The current independent engine is fictional-only; the Living Voter mobile demo separately includes three dated congressional source examples.

01 / INGEST

Source-specific adapters

House, Senate, municipality and issue fixtures carry source identifiers and content digests.

02 / MATCH

Who served on the day?

Tenure and constituency matching use the historical vote date; a successor does not inherit a predecessor's vote.

03 / QUALIFY

How closely does it correspond?

EXACT_QUESTION, SUBSTANTIVE_REVIEWED, TOPIC_ONLY, UNREVIEWED, and no record have different meanings.

04 / DISCLOSE

Unknown is not “No”

Voice votes, absent records, procedural questions and member non-votes are kept distinct from explicit Nay votes.

NEW / INTERACTIVE, FICTIONAL SOURCE SNAPSHOT

The research lab shows tenure-at-vote matching, recorded versus voice votes, typed issue correspondence, private on-page preferences and a voluntary illustrative 20-question civic-archetype flow. All data is fictional and the archetype instrument is unvalidated.
