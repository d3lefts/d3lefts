01 / SOURCE-GROUNDED EXTRACTION & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH SAFETY

SourceBound

Conservative admission of machine-readable assertions from messy, changing web content.

Source/test release available
Real-web held-out evaluation open

The engineering problem

A generated subject–relation–object statement may refer to the wrong entity, use a stale web page or quietly change its qualifier. SourceBound separates candidate generation from admission: retain source digests, evidence spans, DOM locators, identity links, temporal qualifiers and version-aware checks before publishing an assertion.

What actually ran

The bug-fixed, known-fixture package reports 27 tests, 14,000 synthetic pages and 6,000 fixture-labeled retained assertions with zero extra fixture emissions. These are post-hoc regression and synthetic checks, not held-out natural-web precision, retrieval lift or production throughput.

Proposed triple→Source + version + span→Identity and consistency gates→Retain / quarantine

Research judgment: proof of source support is distinct from the truth of that source. The next decisive test is independent domain-held-out annotation with unsupported-assertion rate at matched recall, plus latency, cost and abstention reporting.

02 / STRUCTURED PROVENANCE & CAUSAL VERIFICATION

Proof After Scarcity

Separate bibliographic links, logical dependencies and verification priorities before drawing conclusions from a research graph.

Finite DAG algebra + source model
Empirical priority hypothesis unvalidated

The representation

A manuscript can cite another manuscript without logically depending on it. The dossier distinguishes a citation/reuse graph from a single-proof dependency DAG and explicitly treats shared sources and uncertain links as risks to inference. Its archived October snapshot lists 722 manuscripts, 597 bibliographic edges and 73 mutual-citation groups; these are not measured logical entailment edges.

The finite algebra

The v2 note gives an explicit finite path-sum identity for a proof DAG, an exact one-edge sensitivity formula, and conditional interval bounds under stated nonnegative edge weights. These are elementary finite facts, not novel mathematical priority or empirical evidence of better risk prediction. The earlier outcome-aware retrospective comparison is archived separately and supplies no prospective advantage claim.

Source records→Typed graph + dependencies→Priority hypothesis→Baseline / falsifier

Next test: prospective, frozen weak-link predictions with blinded assessment and separately published dependency graphs. The finite-DAG nilpotence property is established algebra; it does not by itself validate a real-world prioritization algorithm.
