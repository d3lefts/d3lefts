01 · Statement and hypotheses

WRITTEN ANALYTIC CANDIDATE; finite Lean core checked; infinitary bridge OPEN
Dμ(f) ≤ d⌊d² min{(q−1)², 2R/Δ}⌋ (candidate)

For a bounded independent-product function of interaction degree ≤d with a finite palette of q real values, range R and minimum gap Δ>0, the v1.1 analytic audit proposes almost-sure exact recovery after at most d·floor(d²·min{(q−1)²,2R/Δ}) whole-coordinate observations, independent of input dimension.

Premise 1
Independent coordinates, potentially infinitely many, with completed-product measurability and finite-output palette.
Premise 2
Hoeffding interaction degree ≤d, q finite, minimum palette gap Δ>0 and range R.
Premise 3
Correct statements are almost sure; no uniform pointwise recovery or efficient selection is claimed.
