01 / DEFINE THE CLAIM

What exactly
is established?

A Sidon subset has unique unordered pair sums, including sums of an element with itself. In a weak (4,5) set, each four-element selection determines at least five distinct positive pairwise differences. For this specific published 30-coordinate set, a 17-point Sidon subset exists; every 18-point selection fails.

The last step can be phrased as a finite hitting-set problem: the configuration contains 28 three-term arithmetic progressions. Removing only 12 points cannot hit them all; removing 13 can. This is a finite exact statement, with no worldwide extremal optimality claim.
