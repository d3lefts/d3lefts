PRINCIPAL MATHEMATICAL CLAIM / EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN

Quadratic product-space influence

Let X have an independent product distribution, F be real and of Hoeffding–Efron–Stein interaction degree at most two, |F| ≤ B, and V = Var(F) > 0. Set resampling influence Ii(F) = E[(F − EiF)2]. The supplied paper proposes:

maxi Ii(F) ≥ V2 / [K₈ B2], where K₈ = (10+2√2)2(1+√3)2 ≈ 1228.36.

Potential mathematical addition: degree-two, variance-squared scaling without an explicit dependence on marginal bias or alphabet size. The statement excludes dependence among inputs, uncontrolled output size, and arbitrary interaction degree.

Current certificate: an internally checked written proof is reported, but no independent external referee has certified it. The current v8 manuscript source with its scoped proof passport, the historical v6 source, and separate v7 source consolidation are publicly checked in; the 33-page v6.1 research attachment is not yet deposited in GitHub. The v8 update improves the earlier v7 coefficient, not the variance exponent. The full quadratic theorem is not end-to-end Lean-certified, and worldwide novelty remains unresolved.

COMPANION MANUSCRIPT / RATES TO AUDIT

Whole-coordinate observation

In the specified minimax model for unit-ball Hilbert outputs of covariance rank at most r and squared-error tolerance 0 < η ≤ 1/4, the manuscript proposes for degree two Θ(r/η) adaptive queries versus Θ(rm/η) nonadaptive queries when each coordinate alphabet has at most m symbols. It also proposes nonadaptive Θd(rmd−1/η) for fixed degree d.

Each query reveals a whole input coordinate. These are worst-case family bounds under precise conditions, not costs for every individual function. The analytic rank-r quadratic upper bound is ceil(2700(1+√3)²rB²/η) to squared risk at most the rank-r covariance tail plus η for known finite product laws. The proof uses a posterior total-influence decrement, not an automatic one-step variance decrement. No general-degree adaptive Θ(r/η) theorem follows; posterior selection, lower constructions, and rank lifting require independent scrutiny.

QUANTUM INFORMATION GEOMETRY / ANALYTIC REFEREE CANDIDATE

Exact operational quantum ANOVA

For a finite classical–quantum ensemble with product unconditional average state, the optimal squared-error Bayes gain of a single real target from a collective measurement of registers Q has the exact support expansion:

G*(Q) = Σ∅≠S⊆QγS,   γS ≥ 0.

Therefore the gain is monotone and supermodular without a global spectral-condition-number penalty. The v3 bridge also states a converse: for faithful finite-dimensional average B, universal supermodularity over all ensembles with average B characterizes product B. The sharp different inverse-SLD tensor comparison is Cd=21−d∏(1+κi)/(1+∏κi); ternary qubit encoders attain this factor, while binary simple Hermitian contrasts have a coefficient-one comparison.

Proof status: author-side analytic arguments and independent exact finite audits; not end-to-end Lean, not externally refereed and not globally novelty-cleared. The statements concern fixed-subset optimal single-target POVMs, not adaptive quantum query complexity or a shared optimal measurement across targets.

Consecutive four-shuffles: exact threshold 20

For k=4, every permutation of length 20 contains a consecutive factor partitionable into four subsequences of the same relative-order pattern; a displayed 19-permutation avoids all eligible factors. A complete exact finite proof checks 302 factor-graph paths and the 24 windows of the lower witness. SEPARATE PROOF LINE / FINITE CERTIFICATE TRANSFER

Two draws and transferred certificates

Conditioning independent draws on their unordered coordinate pairs induces fair orientations, allowing imported Boolean-cube certificate inequalities to transfer to heterogeneous product laws. A pinned finite-product influence theorem is kernel-compiled in Lean (PR #317, nineteen named declarations, pinned Actions run 42929857470): for positive variance V and bounded indicator-product degree d, 3V⁵ ≤ 12500(π²d²)⁶ Iⱼ(f)² for some j, on finite products with coordinate-dependent marginal tables, including zero-mass atoms.

David Turturean's Boolean-cube proof and antipodal machinery are inherited and explicitly credited. PR #317 proves the common-finite-alphabet case, while merged PR #325 kernel-checks the stronger coordinate-dependent finite alphabet result and finite Efron–Stein support-degree equivalence. No arbitrary measurable-product theorem is certified. It does not certify the stronger degree-two variance-squared formula above, an infinite-product lift, quantum-query consequences or the adaptive rates.
