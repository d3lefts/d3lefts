PRINCIPAL MATHEMATICAL CLAIM / EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN

Quadratic product-space influence

Let X have an independent product distribution, F be real and of Hoeffding–Efron–Stein interaction degree at most two, |F| ≤ B, and V = Var(F) > 0. Set resampling influence Ii(F) = E[(F − EiF)2]. The supplied paper proposes:

maxi Ii(F) ≥ V2 / [256(1+√3)2B2].

Potential mathematical addition: degree-two, variance-squared scaling without an explicit dependence on marginal bias or alphabet size. The statement excludes dependence among inputs, uncontrolled output size, and arbitrary interaction degree.

Current certificate: an internally checked written proof is reported, but no independent external referee has certified it. The linked 53-line public Markdown is an editorial synopsis, not a full proof. Worldwide novelty remains unresolved.

COMPANION MANUSCRIPT / RATES TO AUDIT

Whole-coordinate observation

In the specified minimax model for unit-ball Hilbert outputs of covariance rank at most r and squared-error tolerance 0 < η ≤ 1/4, the manuscript proposes for degree two Θ(r/η) adaptive queries versus Θ(rm/η) nonadaptive queries when each coordinate alphabet has at most m symbols. It also proposes nonadaptive Θd(rmd−1/η) for fixed degree d.

Each query reveals a whole input coordinate. These are worst-case family bounds under precise conditions, not costs for every individual function. Neither a general-degree adaptive Θ(r/η) theorem nor a rank-free result follows. Posterior selection, lower constructions, and rank lifting each need independent scrutiny.

NEW FINITE EXACT CERTIFICATE / HISTORICAL PRIORITY OPEN

Consecutive four-shuffles: exact threshold 20

For k=4, every permutation of length 20 contains a consecutive factor partitionable into four subsequences of the same relative-order pattern; a displayed 19-permutation avoids all eligible factors. A complete exact finite proof checks 302 factor-graph paths and the 24 windows of the lower witness. The graph method is inherited from Bouvel's 2024 workshop collaborators; external refereeing and comprehensive novelty review remain open. The general k-shuffle conjecture is not claimed solved.

SEPARATE PROOF LINE / FINITE CERTIFICATE TRANSFER

Two draws and transferred certificates

Conditioning two independent samples on their unordered coordinate pairs induces fair orientations, allowing an imported Boolean-cube certificate inequality to be applied to heterogeneous products. The companion reports a finite-product bound with V5/2/d6 scaling and scoped Lean statements.

David Turturean's Boolean-cube theorem and antipodal ingredients are prior art. A reported Lean receipt applies only to its exact declaration and assumptions; it does not certify the degree-two formula above, measurable-space extensions, quantum-query claims, or the adaptive rates.

SOURCE-ACCESS STOP RULE

A synopsis is not a proof release.

At the checked public checkpoint, the principal publication folder contains the synopsis, companion synopsis, and structural notes, but not the named main.tex, matching-core.tex, queries.tex, categorical.tex, rank appendix, source auditors, exact replay or SHA256SUMS. Their presence in a referenced ZIP filename does not make them publicly downloadable.

Before publication clearance: upload the original complete proof archive unmodified, validate hashes, compile the paper, rerun exact checkers, and obtain a non-author mathematical proof attack and scope-matched prior-art review. Until then, no complete proof PDF is offered and the theorem remains a manuscript-level claim.
