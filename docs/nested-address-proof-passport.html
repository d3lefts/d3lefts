01 · Statement and hypotheses

EXACT CONSTRUCTION; finite checked; general upper theorem separate
R*Q = 1/k − (a + g(b))/(r k²)

For one replica with s independent uniform m-ary addresses and K=m^s independent fair-sign leaves, the maximum explained variance from c fixed whole-coordinate queries is g(c)=0 at c=0; m^(c−1−2s) for 1≤c≤s+1; min{1,(c−s)/m^s} thereafter. For r outputs with k replicas each, L=s+m^s, Q'=min(Q,rkL), a=floor(Q'/L), b=Q' mod L, the exact optimal nonadaptive Bayes risk is 1/k−(a+g(b))/(rk²).

Premise 1
Inputs are s independent uniform m-ary address symbols plus K=m^s independent fair-sign leaf variables per replica.
Premise 2
Whole-coordinate queries are fixed in advance; squared-loss risk uses optimal conditional-mean prediction.
Premise 3
All r·k replicas are mutually independent and scaled by 1/(k√r).

02 · Mechanism and dependency chain

IMPORTS SEPARATED FROM NEW DEDUCTIONS
01 / PROOF OPERATORRepresentation

With h observed addresses and q leaf bits, exact explained variance is q/m^(2s−h).

02 / PROOF OPERATORInformation gain

The optimum per-replica gain g(c) has nondecreasing discrete differences; exchanging budget between partially observed replicas cannot reduce total gain when it concentrates queries.

03 / PROOF OPERATORComposition

An optimal fixed set completes a=floor(Q'/L) replicas, then partially observes at most one more. Adaptive reading can follow each address to the selected leaf at cost s+1 per replica.

Attribution: Classical conditional variance and Bayes estimators; NIGHTFALL merged five-closure audit. Claims are limited to the explicit address family.
