How much independence does Forrelation hide?

The Gaussian-sign Forrelation law requires exponentially many independent latent components. A sharper per-coordinate information argument improves the earlier aggregate-moment exponent, and the obstruction survives small errors in total variation.

Exact product-mixture representation
M ≥ exp(0.2192005 N)

Improved sharpest derived universal coefficient. Holds even when each latent component only separates the two N-bit blocks.

1% total-variation approximation
log M ≥ 0.2053376 N

Less an additive 0.1120031 nats. The obstruction is exponential for fixed sufficiently small approximation error.

Reconciled exponents

The earlier constant 2/π² = 0.2026423673 follows from a single Forrelation statistic and a quadratic relative-entropy bound. The stronger φ(2/π) = 0.2192005468 uses each coordinate's information witness and the exact binary-information function. Both are correct; the latter is stronger.

φ(t) = (1+t) log(1+t)/2 + (1−t) log(1−t)/2

What is proved, and what remains

The full analytic derivation uses the classical Gaussian sign-correlation identity, orthogonality, binary entropy convexity, and finite conditional-entropy continuity. The general latent-mixture exponential-moment operator passed a strict pinned Lean audit, but that does not constitute a Lean proof of the Gaussian-sign specialization or its sharpened entropy exponent.

Evidence classification: publicly released, non-peer-reviewed working preprint. The specific Gaussian theorem is paper-level, not end-to-end Lean certified; independent human review, full priority analysis, and final contributor attribution remain open. The law is unconditioned Gaussian-sign Forrelation, not every Forrelation promise-conditioned distribution.
