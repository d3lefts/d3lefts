01 · Statement and hypotheses

SCOPED LEAN COMPONENTS; joint adaptive theorem not kernel-compiled
MSEₜ ≤ d Var(g) exp(−t/(dK²)) (conditional)

For Boolean g on a finite full-support independent product, degree≤d and globally valid nonnegative fractional certificate rows of total cost at most K, the manuscript derives an overlap sampling law π_i∝sqrt(P_iQ_i) with E_{i~π}I_i(g)≥Var(g)/K². Exact posterior ANOVA drift then yields MSE after t whole-coordinate queries at most d Var(g) exp(−t/(dK²)), conditional on the complete product-state instantiation.

Premise 1
Finite independent full-support product, Boolean output, Hoeffding interaction degree ≤d≥1.
Premise 2
Every input x has a valid fractional row λ_i^x≥0 certifying every opposite-class pair and with Σ_iλ_i^x≤K.
Premise 3
One query reveals one whole coordinate, posterior update keeps unused coordinates independent; evaluation of π may be expensive.

04 · What this proof does not establish

CLAIM BOUNDARY
Boundary 1
No unconditional guarantee of alphabet-independent row cost K from this certificate theorem; the law-independent real degree-d exponent-two problem remains open.
Boundary 2
A full joint Lean proof of posterior row restriction, actual conditional Hoeffding energies, transition kernel and final MSE is absent.
Boundary 3
The sampling rule may require exponential computation; query count is not runtime, and this is not an all-measurable-product theorem.
