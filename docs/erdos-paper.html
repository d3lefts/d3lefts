01 / MATHEMATICAL OBJECT

One shape.
All real shifts.

Choose a function f on the interval [0,2] with heights between 0 and 1 and total area 1. Pair it with g = 1 − f on [0,2]. Both functions are zero outside the interval. Slide g across f and measure the overlap. The problem is to choose f so the largest overlap over all shifts is as small as possible.

Rf(s) = ∫ f(x) g(x − s) dxSUPREMUM OVER ALL REAL s · g = 1 − f ON [0,2], ZERO OUTSIDE

For N equal cells with rational heights Ai/D and ΣAi = ND/2, each event lag k has exactly the value R(2k/N) = 2 Σ Ai(D − Ai−k)/(ND²), summing over valid overlapping indices. Between consecutive event lags the overlap is affine, so its global maximum occurs at a grid event. Using s = −t gives the equivalent sign convention in the paper.

The exercise below makes both the correlation formula and the geometric affine interpolation testable in exact integers.
