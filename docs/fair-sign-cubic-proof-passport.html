01 · Statement and hypotheses

DRAFT PROOF PR; external referee, novelty and Lean gates OPEN
W ≤ 4 A √M (fair-sign ℓ₁-valued degree ≤ 2)

PR #354 supplies a written proof candidate: for an ℓ₁-valued degree-two function G of independent fair signs with uniform norm bound A, W=E||G−EG||₂² and M=max_i E||G−E_iG||₂², the bound W≤4A√M holds. This yields conditional rank-one cubic selector Var(f)≤5B√M for arbitrary finite leaf laws, and a fixed-rank posterior-stable subclass bound Var(f)≤(5+4√t) B√M.

Premise 1
Fair, independent ±1 controls; arbitrary independent finite-alphabet leaf variables are allowed in the selector corollaries.
Premise 2
G has Fourier degree ≤2 with values in ℓ₁^K and ||G(x)||₁≤A.
Premise 3
Posterior-closure corollary requires a fixed t-dimensional feature subspace for each leaf; no unbounded-rank generalization.
