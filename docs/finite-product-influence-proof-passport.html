01 · Statement and hypotheses

LEAN CHECKED • finite theorem; priority and external referee open
3 V⁵ ≤ 12500 (π²d²)⁶ Iⱼ(f)²

For bounded real f with product indicator degree at most d on a finite product, there is a coordinate j with 3·Var(f)^5 ≤ 12500·(π²d²)^6·I_j(f)^2. The original PR #317 used a common alphabet type, but merged PR #325 strengthens the finite result to a different finite alphabet for each coordinate, zero-mass atoms allowed. The theorem requires |f|≤1 only on positive-mass inputs.

Premise 1
Finite coordinate type and arbitrary coordinate-dependent finite alphabet types, with normalized marginal tables including zero masses (PR #325).
Premise 2
|f|≤1 on the positive-mass support, positive product variance V, finite Efron–Stein degree ≤d equivalent to indicator-monomial degree under the exact formal definitions.
Premise 3
Independent-resampling influence I_j = E[(f−E_j f)²], and the exact pinned statement rather than a stronger manuscript constant.
