The NIGHTFALL protocol

Each research task starts with a precisely scoped claim, its source digest, the target improvement and a falsification condition. A result moves forward only when its evidence supports the exact wording displayed.

Independent engines

Engine A — construction: search for representations, derive candidates, compute exact witnesses and document hypotheses. Engine B — adversarial reproduction: receive the frozen claim and original inputs without construction internals, independently implement the check, and try to falsify the argument. Separate models alone do not establish independence.

Claim ladder and gates

LabelMinimum gate
ConjecturePrecisely stated, falsifiable, unresolved proof
Numerical candidateInputs and numerical method documented; error/tolerance disclosed
Certified constructionExact witness, complete finite checker, conditions and reproducible output
TheoremComplete argument with every imported premise justified; external review recorded separately

The site's existing more granular labels (working, finite, conditional, empirical, software) remain authoritative; this ladder is explanatory, not a silent relabeling.

Portable provenance

Every certificate should travel with its source file, executable command, expected output, environment, and SHA-256 digest. Missing bytes remain visibly missing.

Stopping rules

Stop when exact reproduction fails, source assumptions cannot be discharged, novelty collapses under prior art, a numerical gain is below the acceptance threshold, or repeated search produces no structural improvement. Preserve negative results and correction history.

Primary contract: Autonomous verification protocol.

Negative results

Zerothesis: the measured gain was below the competition improvement threshold. Werner: the floating-point 25-copy t=0.34 candidate failed exact rational reconstruction. Competition and negative-result registry: other unsuccessful operators and provenance distinctions.
