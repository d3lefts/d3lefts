01 · Bias-independent quadratic influence

PAPER PROOF / INTERNAL CHECK · EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN
For independent coordinates, real Hoeffding degree ≤ 2, |F| ≤ B and V = Var(F) > 0, the source proposes maxᵢ Iᵢ(F) ≥ V² / [256(1+√3)² B²].
Assumptions
Independent product laws, bounded real output, interaction degree at most two; influence is the mean squared resampling difference E[(F−EᵢF)²].
Mechanism
Pair-interaction matching and mixed differences isolate disjoint contributions without dropping off-matching interactions.
Available certificate
Editorial Markdown manuscript plus source package referenced by filename; written proofs and internal computational checking, not a Lean compilation of this full theorem.
Limitation / decisive audit
Reproduce the full matching-core LaTeX, attack off-matching estimates independently, then perform dated priority comparison. No general cubic result follows.

02 · Adaptive versus fixed queries

PAPER PROOF / INTERNAL CHECK · EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN
For the specified unit-ball, rank ≤ r minimax prediction regime, adaptive Θ(r/η) versus fixed Θ(rm/η) whole-coordinate queries at degree two and alphabet cap m.
Assumptions
Squared-error Bayes risk, 0 < η ≤ 1/4, bounded output covariance rank, independent inputs, queries observing entire coordinate symbols.
Mechanism
Posterior conditional variance descent for the upper strategy; replicated address variables and packed output directions for lower families.
Available certificate
Supplied queries manuscript and internal checks described in the editorial principal paper. Not an externally refereed or complete kernel-certified policy.
Limitation / decisive audit
Independently inspect stopping time, strategy quantifiers, rank dependence and lower construction. These are minimax rates, not costs for every individual function.

04 · Cubic matching and rare-gate obstruction

EXPLICIT MATHEMATICAL NOTE · NO EXTERNAL REVIEW
For independent fair signs Bⱼ and U ∼ Bernoulli(p), Z = (U−p) Σⱼ Bⱼ makes a proposed overlapping-increment fourth-moment bound fail as p → 0. It does not refute the cubic influence conjecture.
Assumptions
Finite product input, canonical degree-two increments for the counterexample; bounded Hoeffding degree for the accompanying matching and oscillation lemmas.
Mechanism
A shared rare coordinate gates many interactions. It defeats an unconditioned L4/L2-style operator while leaving the main influence question unresolved.
Available certificate
The checked-in note gives exact fourth-moment formulas and elementary proofs of its hypermatching and oscillation lemmas.
Limitation / decisive audit
Replicate the moment algebra independently, then test a codegree-aware conditional inequality on star and sunflower families. No general degree-three proof or novelty claim.
