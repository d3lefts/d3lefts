01 · Bias-independent quadratic influence

PAPER PROOF / INTERNAL CHECK · EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN
For independent coordinates, real Hoeffding degree ≤ 2, |F| ≤ B and V = Var(F) > 0, the source proposes maxᵢ Iᵢ(F) ≥ V² / [256(1+√3)² B²].
Assumptions
Independent product laws, bounded real output, interaction degree at most two; influence is the mean squared resampling difference E[(F−EᵢF)²].
Mechanism
Pair-interaction matching and mixed differences isolate disjoint contributions without dropping off-matching interactions.
Available certificate
Editorial Markdown manuscript plus source package referenced by filename; written proofs and internal computational checking, not a Lean compilation of this full theorem.

05 · Log-free Kaczmarz best-iterate projection bound

WRITTEN PROOF + EXACT REPLAY · ORIGINALITY / EXTERNAL REVIEW OPEN
For any consistent real linear system, maximal normalized-residual row projections give a successful prefix iterate in at most ⌈n/ε²⌉−1 projections for the original residual criterion. This is an existential projection count, not a fixed-time randomized guarantee.
Assumptions
Consistent system Ax*=b; exact orthogonal row projections; nonzero rows; selection by maximum normalized squared residual; a best prefix iterate can be chosen.
Mechanism
Projection Pythagoras, maximum of weighted row errors ≥ their weighted mean, and telescoping squared error. The existing greedy selection rule is classical, not a newly invented algorithm.
Available certificate
Complete written argument and standard-library exact-rational checker: 240 test systems, 2,880 greedy updates, zero failed checks. Source and assumptions published in a dedicated proof passport.
Limitation / decisive audit
Independent mathematical proof review and dated earlier-literature priority search; no claim of a new global algorithm, fixed-final-iterate high probability, or solved-problem publication status.

06 · Exact consecutive four-shuffle threshold

FINITE EXACT PROOF · EXTERNAL REFEREE AND PRIORITY OPEN
Every length-20 permutation has a consecutive four-shuffle factor of length 8 or 12, while a 19-entry permutation avoids all admissible factors. Thus the finite computer-assisted claim is N₄ = 20.
Assumptions
Four disjoint equal-length subsequences of the same relative-order pattern, each of size at least two; factor means a consecutive block. The result is for k=4 only.
Mechanism
The pre-existing perfect-matching factor graph has 5,040 states and 2,632 bad-eight edges. All 302 length-20 avoidance paths force a length-12 common-triple partition.
Available certificate
A public standalone exact Python checker replays the 302 upper certificates and 24 factors of the explicit 19-term lower witness. The original manuscript/source archive has verified hashes, but its PDF/ZIP binaries are not GitHub assets.
Limitation / decisive audit
Rebuild the factor graph and forced local-comparison exact covers independently; check post-2024 precedent and obtain a non-author referee. The general k-shuffle conjecture is open.
