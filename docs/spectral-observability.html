01 / BIGGEST UPSIDEdLINEAR SUPPORT
V5 PAPER PROOF · NOVELTY UNCHECKED

All fixed degrees, when interaction supports are linear.

The v5 synthesis proposes an alphabet- and bias-independent squared-influence bound at every fixed interaction degree d, and Θd(r/η) adaptive spectral prediction. Its special assumption is explicit: distinct nonsingleton Hoeffding supports meet in at most one coordinate. This is not an unrestricted cubic result. The linear-support proof is neither Lean-checked nor independently refereed.

In bounded m-ary settings, the nonadaptive rate grows to Θ(rm/η). The law-independent degree-two influence mechanism is matching plus a fourth-moment estimate, separate from the imported general-degree certificate theorem.

02 / THE REUSABLE MECHANISM

From certificates
to observations.

Transport. Two independent product draws reduce conditional orientations to fair Boolean signs, making a pinned antipodal certificate inequality usable on finite heterogeneous products.

Overlap. Opposite output classes cannot have substantial inverse-certificate mass while hiding all shared resampling influence. The resulting overlap profile defines a concrete probability distribution over coordinates.

Posterior descent. Once a coordinate is observed, its influence drops out of the expected remaining total-influence potential. The one-step Boolean finite-product inequality is Lean-certified; a whole-policy Hilbert prediction theorem requires additional spectral, measurable-selection and recursive-state arguments.
