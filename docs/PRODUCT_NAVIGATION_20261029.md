# Product architecture — three destinations

**Homepage → the most important results.** Present scoped finite witnesses, flagship research campaigns, negative results and open proof boundaries. The Golden Hour and New Moon treatments offer different editorial entrypoints without changing the facts, sources, scientific status or destinations.

**Constellation → how mechanisms connect.** A curated, typed graph distinguishes documented finite checks, shared methods, unresolved premises and untested analogies. Graph proximity never establishes mathematical transfer. Selecting a claim exposes its Proof Passport, primary source and a targeted Independent Review link.

**Independent Review → how to challenge claims.** Each task identifies an attack surface, source or archive, checker and reporting route. PASS, FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE verdicts cover only the audited scope. Worldwide novelty, proof completeness and independent human review require separate evidence.

## Scope and maintenance

The full research library, living papers, experimental workbenches and deeper guided process remain available. Their existence should never compete with the three top-level destinations.

The three landing pages expose their navigation in static HTML, including accessible current-page state without JavaScript. The shared `site.js` displays the same destinations on specialist pages. Local workbench procedures remain deeper tasks rather than falsely presented as stages of proof completion.

Run `node scripts/test_product_destinations.cjs` and `python3 scripts/check_site.py` on every website PR. Test in a real browser across mobile widths, both skies and reduced motion; the offline test is a navigation contract, not an independent visual or mathematical audit.
