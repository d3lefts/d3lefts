01 / DEGREE-TWO INFLUENCE · MANUSCRIPT PROOF

A variance-squared signal.
No bias penalty.

For a real-valued function F with Hoeffding/Efron–Stein interaction degree at most two, |F| ≤ B and V = Var(F) > 0, the principal paper proposes an explicit influence guarantee independent of coordinate count, alphabet sizes and marginal probabilities. Here Iᵢ(F) = E[(F − EᵢF)²], with Eᵢ averaging only coordinate i.

Each query reveals one complete input coordinate. Bayes risk is E||F−E[F|transcript]||². The rates concern worst-case families of unit-ball outputs at tolerance 0 < η ≤ 1/4, covariance rank at most r, and where indicated alphabet size at most m.

04 / CUBIC FRONTIER · PROOF ATTACK

The matching lemma survives.
A moment shortcut fails.

The newest research note proves an elementary matching budget: for disjoint top-order interaction supports S of size d, ∑S ||hS||∞ ≤ 2dB. It also gives the section-oscillation estimate ∑i osci(x) ≤ 4d²B. These are paper proofs, not independent priority or Lean receipts.

A separate exact counterexample invalidates a tempting proof step for degree three. With A = U−p for U ∼ Bernoulli(p) and independent fair signs Bj, put Z=A∑Bj. Then E[Z²]=np(1−p), but the ratio E[Z⁴]/(E[Z²]+E[Z²]²) becomes arbitrarily large as p→0 and n=⌈p−2⌉. A shared rare input can gate many overlapping interactions.

New exact pure-cubic stress test: for a shared rare Bernoulli core and 16 dependent orthogonal increments, the quadratic independent-increment moment bound fails by the exact ratio 179722/6993; a family with p=m−2 grows asymptotically as 3m. This disproves the transplanted proof step, not the cubic influence conjecture.

v6.1 conditioned repair: if the increments become centered and independent after revealing the shared core, their fourth moment is bounded by c²s + 3s² + 3Var(Q), where Q is conditional total second-moment energy and s=E Q. No general degree-three influence charge for Var(Q) has been proved. Inspect the assumption and open mechanism ↗.
