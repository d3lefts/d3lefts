02 / HUMAN–AI COLLABORATION

Who does what.

Devin sets the problem, the research scope, the publication threshold, and the final human sign-off. AI collaborators can propose representations, run experiments, seek counterexamples, and prepare reproducible artifacts. Their agreement is never treated as outside expert verification.
