02 / HUMAN–AI COLLABORATION

Who makes the decisions?

Devin sets research priorities and decides what may be published. AI collaborators propose mathematical constructions, write tests, investigate counterexamples, and document their results. An agent checking another agent's work is still an internal check, not external peer review.

Current research assignments: Axiom — constructive mathematical architecture; Lemnis — structural theorems and adversarial analysis; Ryev — exact computational discovery and evidence systems; Tessara — continuum theory and scale-transition mathematics. Read the scientific role charter ↗

03 / INDEPENDENT EVALUATION

What reviewers should challenge.

  • The exact statement, definitions, and assumptions
  • Source material, witness files, and checker independence
  • Known prior work and limits of novelty claims
  • Counterexamples, failed attempts, and corrected wording
  • Publication state and independently completed review gates
