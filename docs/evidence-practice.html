03 / WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE CLAIM?

Evidence has failure conditions.

Safety

A paired evaluation that fails to separate from seed variance, or materially harms legitimate task completion, becomes a negative result. A proposed control cannot be called an effective defense before those runs.

External math

Matching an exact witness confirms its finite arithmetic. A discrepancy triggers frozen-input reconciliation, negative controls, and author notification. Success is not automatically a world-first finding.

Lean

The passing finite-mod-3 kernel check establishes only those two finite lemmas. An actual decoder theorem requires a faithful full statement and a separate accepted kernel certificate with no hidden axioms.

Calibration

Without a complete historical candidate population and preregistered prediction ledger, survival and success rates remain unavailable. Begin prospective measurement rather than invent retrospective precision.
