04 / HOW CLAIMS ADVANCE

The verification contract.

The research process is explicit, evidence-dependent, and designed to survive changes in models, collaborators, and techniques.

01 — CONSTRUCT

Change the representation.

Look for algebraic structure, symmetry, a better parameterization, or a new active constraint before spending more compute.

02 — FALSIFY

Write the attack first.

Separate candidate construction from its checker. Audit arithmetic, numerical margins, missing hypotheses, and hidden source dependencies.

03 — CERTIFY

Make evidence portable.

Keep inputs, exact checks, hashes, proof obligations, failure cases, and credit beside each claim. Preserve a complete revision trail.

A convincing argument is one that another researcher can try to disprove using the same evidence, without relying on our interpretation.
