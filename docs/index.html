02 / PROOF ATTACK · CUBIC FRONTIER

A bound is not a proof.
Attack the step.

The degree-two matching argument motivates a stronger degree-three influence theorem, but the naive fourth-moment extension fails. A rare Bernoulli coordinate can gate many overlapping interactions and make the proposed moment ratio unbounded. This is an explicit counterexample to a proof technique, not to the desired theorem.

Next decisive test: develop a conditional-core or codegree-sensitive inequality that survives the rare-gate example, then independently audit its assumptions before claiming an all-degree result.
