RESEARCH FRONTIER / 10 NOVEMBER 2026 · DEGREE TWO TO DEGREE THREE

Low interaction.
High observability.

Adaptive Observability of Low-Interaction Product Systems. The new principal manuscript gives a degree-two, bias-independent variance-squared influence bound on arbitrary finite product alphabets: maxᵢ Iᵢ(F) ≥ Var(F)² / [256(1+√3)² ||F||∞²]. For rank-r Hilbert outputs it proposes a sharp minimax separation: adaptive Θ(r/η) versus nonadaptive Θ(rm/η) whole-coordinate queries for degree two and alphabet size at most m.

The linked companion develops the two-draw certificate-to-influence mechanism and reports Lean-checked finite-product results with their own scope. The latest cubic note establishes an elementary hypermatching budget and a concrete rare-coordinate counterexample to the naive fourth-moment extension. The general degree-three variance-squared theorem remains open.

Evidence status: quadratic and query-rate claims have manuscript proofs and internal checks but no external human referee or end-to-end Lean certification. The site's five separately approved finite/bounded findings are unchanged. The publication drafts are not cleared as globally novel results.
