THE STRUCTURE / THREE SEPARATE CLAIMS

A reason to act is not the same as permission to act.

01 / AUTHORITY

Who may act?

Jurisdiction, applicable rules, effectivity, the empowered institution and the scope of its competence. A proposed interpretation must name its source and limits.

Legal authority: separately established, never inferred from a checksum
02 / EVIDENCE

What supports the claim?

Exact cited text, source version, provenance, factual premises, exceptions, competing interpretations and missing records—without smoothing over disagreement.

Authenticity and interpretation are distinct questions
03 / ASSURANCE

What was checked?

The review scope, checker, independent challenge, source freshness, known gaps and change-triggered rechecks. A successful test never grants legal power.

Machine checks do not replace institutional review

STATUS VOCABULARY / DO NOT CONFUSE EVIDENCE WITH AUTHORITY

Four distinct evidence conditions.

Verified Evidence

Only the specifically identified source, provenance, integrity and reviewed scope are supported. It never means the proposed action is legal or authorized.

Conceptual status · not assigned to this demo
Unverified

A source or premise has not met the required authentication, review, or freshness conditions. No conclusion is silently promoted.

Requires additional checking
Contested

Material claims or interpretations conflict; preserve both the dispute and its supporting evidence until adjudicated.

Disagreement is not failure to read
Illustrative

Entirely fictional content for teaching and design review. It cannot establish real-world source validity, jurisdiction or consent.
