PEDAGOGY & SCOPE

The course behind the course

Why rigorous, independently auditable research skills belong in the systems engineering curriculum.

Why this exists

There is an enormous difference between knowing systems-engineering terms and being able to deliver a research result that survives another person's attempt to reproduce, falsify, revise, and reuse it. Many of the needed habits are taught by apprenticeship: stating exact scope, carrying assumptions across models, pricing observations, tracking decision authority, recognizing unidentifiable worlds, designing independent audits, and knowing when evidence stops applying after a change.

This course teaches those habits directly. It aims at doctoral-level research practice, while remaining useful to professional engineers and research-intensive master's students.

What this course is not

It is not an accredited program, a replacement for graduate coursework, or a claim that 14 sessions amount to a doctorate. It is not affiliated with MIT, INCOSE, NASA, or any other cited organization. It provides a short, practical lab sequence to complement foundational mathematics, research supervision, original discovery, and scholarship.

Teaching design

Each session follows a repeated sequence: principle → operational method → example → exercise → adversarial check → doctoral extension → acceptance artifact. The repetition is intentional. The goal is to move from passive recognition to the ability to construct and defend a bounded claim.

AURORA gives the course one common, small, inspectable world: all exercises can point to the same source code, trace schema, assumptions, and revision history. It is useful precisely because it is not a realistic system—unmodeled effects become easy to identify and teach.

How to contribute

Identify a specific error, reproducible counterexample, missing assumption, improved experiment, or independently checked result. Proposed changes should say what the current material claims, why the claim fails or is incomplete, and which lesson and artifact need revision. Preserve correction history; do not erase failed results.
