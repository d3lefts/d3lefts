THE IDEA

The invisible curriculum.

Standards describe what engineers should do. Graduate coursework teaches models and methods. Research is where those abstractions collide with incomplete evidence, organizational authority, shifting assumptions, and hostile scrutiny.

This independent course trains the connective discipline: how to move from a consequential decision to a bounded claim, from a model to an experiment, and from evidence to a defensible research contribution.

01 / REPRESENT

State it exactly.

Define the world, units, hypotheses, baseline, and falsifier before solving.

02 / CHALLENGE

Attack your own result.

Use paired tests, counterexamples, independent checks, and changes in assumptions.

03 / PRESERVE

Make truth travel.

Keep the source, proof or evidence, version, limitations, and next experiment together.

EXECUTABLE FIELD METHODS

Make the reasoning runnable.

Paired policy comparisons, exhaustive finite-state checks, SHA-256 evidence passports, and change-impact analysis. Four linked practicums with explicit failure tests.

THE STANDARD

Not just a result.
A result that survives.

A defensible research result should be explicit enough to reconstruct, narrow enough to falsify, and packaged so its limits survive revision and reuse.

The course is a graduate research supplement, not an accredited doctoral degree. It assumes mathematical depth and disciplinary supervision outside these pages.
