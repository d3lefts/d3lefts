SESSION 05 / 14 — MODEL

Models, abstractions & digital threads

Connect analytic, executable, and operational models without false equivalence.

90 min · lab 2hLECTURE + LAB + ADVERSARIAL CHECKNO PREREQUISITE INSTALLS

The operating principle

A model is a deliberate loss of detail. The question is not whether it is “accurate”; the question is which decision survives the loss. Every model should have a model card for systems: purpose, state space, structural assumptions, numerical method, calibration data, validity region, and known failure cases.

Use three levels of representation when possible: an abstract proof model, an executable digital model, and a field or experimental observation model. The arrows between levels are assumptions to verify, not automatic equivalences.

Model hierarchy

  • Abstract: a binary event, three sensors, common-mode failure and local failure probabilities.
  • Executable: labs/aurora.py, with a seeded pseudorandom episode generator and deterministic decision policy.
  • Operational: an actual sensing network, not provided by this course. It includes drift, maintenance, unmodeled faults, human response, and nonstationary conditions.

An empirical success at level two cannot certify level three without external validation. A theorem about level one does not imply its assumptions are met at level two.

Lab — create and break a model card

  1. Read the simulator and write its variables, probability distributions, and conditional independences.
  2. Draw a compact influence diagram for true event, common outage, channel availability, channel reading, alarm, and observed cost.
  3. Identify two assumptions whose violation could change the preferred alarm policy.
  4. Change one parameter at a time; explain where this local sensitivity study misses interactions.
  5. Define a model-version identifier that includes source revision, parameters, seed, and output schema.

Acceptance artifact: 05-model-card.md with a bounded applicability statement and two disconfirming scenarios.

Adversarial check

Have one critic infer the simulator's probabilistic model from outputs alone, then compare against the source. Missing information reveals observability limits. Ask whether the chosen metric hides severe failures in rare subpopulations.

Doctoral extension

Specify a refinement relation between abstract and executable states. For a proposed theorem, list the premises required for that relation to preserve the result. Then present either a proof sketch or a counterexample.
