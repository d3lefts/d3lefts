## The operating principle

Experiments should distinguish explanations, not merely produce a favorable metric. A good design specifies the counterfactual, assignment mechanism, estimand, sample unit, measurement process, and stopping rule **before data inspection**.

Simulation is an intervention on a *model*, not automatically an intervention on a real system. Reproducible random numbers make a computational result replayable; they do not make its assumptions true.

## A decisive experiment, minimally

- **Claim:** quorum reduces false alarms relative to any-positive at a specified event rate and failure model.
- **Alternative:** quorum has little gain, or has an unacceptable miss-rate tradeoff.
- **Intervention:** hold generated episodes fixed; change only the policy.
- **Metrics:** miss fraction conditional on an event, false-alarm fraction conditional on no event, operability, and cost.
- **Decision rule:** compare policies on identical synthetic worlds; report uncertainty for estimated event rates and avoid turning “no significant difference” into equivalence.

`labs/aurora.py` supports seeded traces. For a *paired* comparison, make two runs with the same seed, trial count, and sensor configuration but different decision policies. The generator consumes randomness independently of the decision rule, so episodes align by row. Verify this from the trace, not by assumption.

## Lab — preregister and execute

1. Write a prediction and stop rule before looking at results.
2. Run:

```bash
python3 labs/aurora.py --policy any --seed 2026 --trials 5000 --out labs/any.csv
python3 labs/aurora.py --policy quorum --seed 2026 --trials 5000 --out labs/quorum.csv
python3 labs/audit.py labs/any.csv
python3 labs/audit.py labs/quorum.csv
```

3. Match the rows by trial and demonstrate that latent events and channel readings are the same across both traces.
4. Compute paired disagreements and condition on event state; explain why an unpaired significance test would waste information.
5. Repeat after raising the common-mode failure probability. State what changed and what did not.

**Acceptance artifact:** `06-experiment.md` with preregistration, commands, paired summary, counter-hypothesis, and decision.

## Adversarial check

Criticize the stopping rule, multiplicity, hidden data exclusions, outcome leakage, seed selection, and the difference between repeated simulation and independent replication. An exact replay is not an external replication.

## Doctoral extension

Give a finite-sample confidence method or a non-asymptotic bound under explicitly stated sampling assumptions. Distinguish coverage guarantees from empirical intervals, and note whether repeated trials share latent random structure.
