01 / PROPOSED MATHEMATICAL CONTRIBUTIONS

A transferable certificate engine.
Proof still under review.

Arbitrary product-space influence. For a bounded real function |f| ≤ 1 of interaction degree d ≥ 1, with V = Var(f) > 0, the manuscript proposes maxᵢ Iᵢ(f) ≥ cprod V5/2 / d6, with cprod ≈ 0.00120125, independent of coordinate count, alphabets and marginal biases. The influence convention is Iᵢ(f) = E[Var(f | X−i)].

GENERAL INFLUENCE BOUND / PROPOSED maxi Ii(f) ≥ cprod · Var(f)5/2 / d6 Unverified general theorem · cprod ≈ 0.00120125069 · all independent coordinate laws in the proposed scope

Localized fractional certificates. For Boolean g, the Certificate Engine draft proposes V₁V₀ ≤ ∑ⱼ √(Iⱼ(g) PⱼQⱼ), where V₁,V₀ are inverse-certificate-cost masses and P,Q are loads on the two output classes. This strengthens the global maximum-influence statement by identifying coordinates where both certificate families place mass. It is a proposed theorem, not yet a Lean theorem.

LOCALIZED TWO-SIDED INEQUALITY / PROPOSED V1 V0 ≤ ∑j √(Ij(g) Pj Qj) This is the unverified premise of the separately kernel-checked overlap consequence.

Consequences under study. The integrated draft also treats competing-symbol Chebyshev certificates, arbitrary index sets, finite-palette adaptive recovery, and classical whole-symbol prediction of quantum acceptance probabilities. These claims remain dependent on upstream proof obligations.

01B / ARCHIVED MANUSCRIPT · CANDIDATE

Certificate Engine v2.
Readable. Reproducible. Under review.

The 18-page Fractional Certificates, Influence, and Observation on Product Spaces manuscript was merged as research pull request 291. The PDF, LaTeX source, four Python checks, and status ledger are available together. Its stated results are written proofs and computational checks; the new theorem statements have not received independent human review, Lean certification, or dated priority clearance.

Proposed level-crossing result (Theorem 1.7): for bounded, interaction-degree-d functions under product laws, the manuscript gives a mean-crossing probability lower bound of Var(f)2 / (4π4d4). These (2, 4) exponents apply to crossings, not to the unresolved general coordinate-influence bound. The missing jump-size argument is stated explicitly.

Proposed observation results (Theorem 1.8): a conditional-influence-greedy predictor and an adaptive/nonadaptive separation using fair-bit pointer functions. The source checks include exact finite examples; they are not proofs of the general claims. The unprojected localization question and zero-weight formalization also remain open.

Scope: candidate research archived for reproducibility, not an approved original finding or a journal publication. The seven Lean-certified declarations described below are narrower than the new manuscript's main results.

03 / REMAINING MATHEMATICAL GATES

The missing bridge.
Clearly named.

Highest priority: formalize Turturean's weighted antipodal premise transferred through the two-draw conditioning, including the reciprocal denominators and fixed weights. Then prove the complete abstract weight-removal lemma, including its Cauchy–Schwarz reduction and all zero-load cases, and derive the nonconditional localized two-sided inequality.

The general influence result additionally requires the competing-symbol Chebyshev row construction, finite-partition and infinite-product reductions, and the real-function inequalities. Adaptive and quantum-query conclusions have their own proof dependencies. Certificate Engine v2 also isolates the missing jump-size lemma for the full (2, 4) influence problem and the open unprojected-localization question. A separate adversarial mathematical and dated priority review is required before a publication-clearance claim.
