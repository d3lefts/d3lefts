BUILT AROUND A CLEAR TRUST BOUNDARY

Proof is not
permission.

Commonwealth’s research direction asks whether changing source law can trigger selective, privacy-preserving recertification of decisions. A proof object only supports claims under its assumptions. Governmental legitimacy, legal interpretation, institutional authority, voter eligibility, publication rights and enforcement must be established separately.

Source integrityHash and version checks do not prove an issuer or legal interpretation is authentic.
Human authorityNo AI legislation, official ballot or binding effect without institution-specific authorization.
Private by defaultNo covert profiling, sensitive preference resale, political microtargeting or unconsented reuse.
Challengeable evidenceIndependent checkers, visible gaps, uncertainty, change custody and explicit release gates.
