# Commonwealth OS v1.1 — Democratic Assurance Addendum
**Design proposal, 2026-11-15. Source baseline:** main 17571cf14e49a76c599b0e7f98a769699b1314d9.

## Summary
Preserve the existing BLUEPRINT_v1_0.md, implementation plan, source-ingestion freeze, pilot PRDs, versioned laws, role/tenant isolation, mobile-first app, and independently reviewed public publishing. Change the **first production pilot** to binding votes conducted in-app by a legally consenting and authorized university organization or student council. Development continues in DEMO until legal, security, accessibility and election approvals pass.

## Rollout
The first institutional pilot follows the previously agreed order: University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH) student government -> other Huntsville universities -> Huntsville City Schools public high-school student councils -> participating Huntsville HOAs -> Huntsville City Council (the final phase of the first pilot). The school stage requires district authorization, youth-specific privacy and consent safeguards, and excludes adult federal political profiling. The first *binding organizational vote* is a subsequent conditional release goal for a specifically authorized university organization, not an unconditional promise of binding voting during prepilot. HOA votes must satisfy each association's law and governing documents; the municipal stage is advisory unless separately lawfully authorized. City-level citizen direct ballots are outside the first pilot absent separately established legal authority and independent assurance. No stage is an existing institutional partnership. A pilot does not create public-election authority.

## Architecture
1. **Client**: mobile-first accessible representative and member interfaces; AI-off choice; source text, revisions, discussion, explicit human vote confirmation.
2. **Civic intelligence**: source-cited optional AI analysis; private, consent-scoped preferences; no ballot access, official signing keys or consequential effect credentials.
3. **Democratic procedure**: immutable proposal and rule versions; authenticated eligibility; dedicated independently reviewed balloting adapter; secret member ballots, recorded roll calls, and advisory expressions are different modes.
4. **DAP / authority**: proof manifest binding exact motion and election to competent jurisdiction, effective law, issuer signatures, contest status and change-stable evidence.
5. **Effect sink**: independently reviewed sole channel for supported effects; current epoch, stay, action bytes, budget and idempotency checked atomically. Unmediated paths are explicitly outside certification.

## The four research requirements
Informed human agency (A) and effective political equality (E) require preregistered human studies and participation/access audits. Legal authorization (L) requires competent institution-specific legal review. Operational assurance (S) requires independently verified election and scoped effect checks. These are four simultaneous requirements, not interchangeable scores. Passing a JSON validator or source-cited AI output does not prove A, E or L.

## First vertical slice
Fictional proposal -> amendment -> text/rule freeze -> DEMO vote -> independently verified tally -> human attestation -> DAP -> simulated effect. Negative cases: wrong tenant, text swap, changed rule, false quorum, duplicate credential/cast, replay, revoked legal authority, effective stay, overspend, simultaneous commit, correlated public metadata, direct effect API bypass. After external reviews and explicit authority, activate binding mode for only that institution.

## Release gates
G0 authoritative procedure profile and fictional test fixtures. G1 tenant/identity isolation, immutable sources/events. G2 legislative dashboard and optional AI. G3 isolated election adapter and independent tally. G4 DAP + effect guard and adversarial replay. G5 written institutional authorization, independent election/privacy/security and accessibility assessment, named election/legal officers, appeals and fallback, ethics review where required. G6 HOA membership/consent/proxy configuration. G7 lawful municipality rolls and public-record workflows.

Do not import Huntsville records yet, claim city/university endorsement, automatically cast votes from survey profiles, invent public election cryptography, or claim real security certification from finite tests. Full detailed architecture, schemas, synthetic checker and v0.3 proof audit are packaged as a separate versioned conversation deliverable; this file is the repository integration delta.
