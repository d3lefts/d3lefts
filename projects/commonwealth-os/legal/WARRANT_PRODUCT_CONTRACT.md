# Commonwealth Warrant — Public legal assurance product contract (v0.1)

**Naming and purpose.** The **Commonwealth Warrant** (public interface: **The Warrant**) is a proposed *record of justification*, not a judicial warrant, search warrant, regulatory permit, official opinion, legal advice, institutional delegation, or authority to take action. It neither creates nor implies legal authority. Counsel and each institution must approve terminology and behavior before any operational use.

**Three strictly separated layers**

1. **Authority:** exact jurisdiction; institution/office/competence; source and current effective time of an applicable rule; limitations, exceptions, disputed interpretation, and required approvals.
2. **Evidence:** exact source, source version/effective interval, custodian, integrity/authenticity verification, dated factual premises, counterevidence, disputes, missing records, provenance and retention.
3. **Assurance:** scope, independent reviewer, verifier/version, passing and failing checks, unresolved assumptions, change dependencies and independent replay results. A passing test is not legal authorization.

**Evidence conditions (not legal decisions):**

- **VERIFIED_EVIDENCE:** source provenance, integrity and the claimed check scope have been independently verified. It does not prove substantive legal applicability, factual completeness or an authorized action; no public prototype assigns this status.
- **UNVERIFIED:** required source custody/freshness/checks missing, even when a text matches.
- **CONTESTED:** a material factual or interpretive dispute with competing positions and traceable evidence; do not erase it by averaging.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE:** wholly fictional example only, no factual or legal effect. This is the sole status of the public R-12 demo.

**Fail-closed policy.** Missing jurisdiction, absent authenticated source, conflicting applicable rules, changes since verification, missing institutional approvals or an expired reviewer scope block a claim of authorization. Future production records must carry the source version and scope; a change invalidates affected dependencies pending recertification. A checksum does not authenticate an origin; human review is not a machine proof; a kernel check does not prove an actual law.

**Terminology separation.** *Technical Proof Passport* remains the NIGHTFALL-origin machine-readable evidence/replay/check architecture. *Democratic Assurance Passport* is the proposed institutional governance-process record (legitimacy, representative approval, ballot provenance and publication). *The Warrant* is the Commonwealth public legal/evidence view. None inherit one another's authority or verification scope.

**Current implementation.** `docs/commonwealth-warrant.html`, `docs/commonwealth-warrant.css` and `docs/commonwealth-warrant.mjs` implement a fictional browser-only explainer. A simulated source change illustrates `RECHECK_REQUIRED` but runs no legal revalidation. The technical code and separately authored replay under `projects/commonwealth-os/legal/` are independent fictional research artifacts, *not* invoked by the explainer. No real sources, people, legal claims, telemetry, storage, network calls, legal authorization or live identity are introduced.

**Operational gate.** Real deployment requires named counsel and human authority, approved scope, validated provenance, conflict-resolution procedures, independent audit, privacy and accessibility review, institutional authorization and a source-version change policy. There is no current production legal Warrant or judicial authority.
