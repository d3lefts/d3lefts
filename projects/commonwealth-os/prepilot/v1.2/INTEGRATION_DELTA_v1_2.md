# Integration delta — v1.2 over main v1.0 and PR #609 v1.1

- Retain representative-first Chamber application, institutional governance rules and later binding organizational pilot gates. A **new, public-facing prepilot Living Voter** is the primary concept demonstration before institutional implementation. It does not claim authority to conduct a real election.
- The prepilot progression is **survey → issue briefing → user simulation → personal dashboard/history → exact official legislative roll-call comparison**. Dashboard is also available immediately after survey, then issues expand as choices are made.
- Prepilot issue scope is *any matter supported by an authenticated source and a reviewed civic question*; do not promise total legal coverage. Provide a source-request queue and honest unavailable states.
- User saved simulated historical choices are private and nonbinding. Any later official member ballot is logically and physically distinct from these choices; certified secret-ballot content remains secret.
- Official records are **member votes on precise House/Senate roll-call questions**. Do not infer per-member votes on voice votes, or collapse amendment, cloture and passage votes. A topic match is not proof of issue-level agreement.
- Rep choice demonstration requires participant-selected jurisdiction; example fixtures cover Alabama's Fifth Congressional District. Nothing is inferred from location signals or the account.
- Configurable surveys: preserve 24+12 institutional instrument and 1+40+30+25 adult living-voter budget, neither asserted as legislatively exhaustive; prepilot uses short illustrative sample.
- No public citizen political profile, no party-affinity scoring or politician rankings, no auto-ballot from survey, no unauthorized federal political profiling of minors.
- The DAP attaches to sources, procedural applicability, record mappings and simulations with *typed evidence status*; it never certifies a user's private preference as a legal referendum or that an issue summary alone establishes how Congress voted.

## Work to implement after the offline demo
