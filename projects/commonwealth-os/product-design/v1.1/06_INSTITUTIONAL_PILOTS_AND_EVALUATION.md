# Institution-specific pilots and rigorous evaluation — v1.1

The canonical civic-pilot order is **1 UAH student government → 2 other Huntsville universities → 3 Huntsville City Schools public high-school student councils → 4 participating Huntsville HOAs → 5 Huntsville City Council last**. The existence of synthetic union, student or municipality test data is not institutional authorization and does not amend this adoption sequence.

## Three questionnaire families, five institutional adoption stages

| Family | Who may participate | Product question limit and provenance | Political/authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| University/student government | Eligible students under the participating SGA's actual rules | 24 research-draft questions (8 shared governance, 16 campus-specific), up to 12 sourced adaptive + optional priorities prompt; 37 max | Advice unless SGA explicitly authorizes a defined voting procedure; university policies that SGA cannot enact labeled as recommendations |
| HOA | Members and residents distinct under exact association governing documents | 24 HOA research seeds (8+16), up to 12 adaptive + priorities | Resident opinions are not automatically unit/member ballots; proxies and weights depend on bylaws |
| Municipality/Huntsville example | Adult participants only where authorized and consented; separate public read-only browsing for anyone | 24 municipal research seeds (8+16), up to 12 adaptive + priorities | Public input advisory until municipal law and institution separately authorize an action; do not fabricate actual Huntsville law |
| Adult cross-jurisdiction Living Voter (parallel research stream) | Voluntary adults with properly scoped source data | Up to 96: 1 priorities + 40 local + 30 federal + 25 adaptive | Informational representation of preferences; never an election ballot or exhaustive law position theorem |

Use response values Support / Do not support / No preference / Need more information / Not applicable / Skip as appropriate to pilot; map No preference, missing, skipped and insufficient information to separate stored states. Retest wording neutrality before claims of empirically neutral questions. Fictional seed items are not certified current legal requirements.

Minors do **not** complete adult federal political profiling as part of the school pilot. Data collection, retention, institutional approvals and educational purpose must be reviewed in advance; not inferred from the existence of a demo app.

## Historical roll-call workstream

The civic-history feature can be demonstrated with synthetic House, Senate and municipal source adapters while all real-data intake stays disabled. Test that historical officeholders, district changes, date boundaries, moving users, multimember seats, missing named records and non-substantive motions are displayed correctly. A city-council document ingestion phase is **not required** to complete the fictional technical verification.

The research contribution is factual provenance and transparent citizen access to official actions—not predictive targeting or politician grades. Do not claim an exact citizen-vs-official comparison when the issue-to-motion correspondence lacks reviewed equivalence.

## Institutional approvals and stages

Every target institution must provide a written charter/bylaw/rulebook version and responsible owner, approved functions, authority/eligibility source, privacy and accessibility review, incident response path, records retention, evaluation consent, publication/redaction policy, and rollback/revocation procedure. A public city meeting note is useful data but not institutional permission to use the OS for official procedure.

Proposed sequence *within* an approved pilot: shadow historical replay → staff-supervised parallel recording → separately authorized limited action (if lawfully possible) → independent audit and participant study → correction/replay → next-stage decision. No timetable or institutional endorsement is presumed.

## Pre-registered metrics and denominators

| Research question | Primary outcome | Failure/abstention representation |
|---|---|---|
| Historical join correctness | Fraction of held-out **source-named** member records correctly assigned to applicable term/seat/time, with exact denominator | Mark unknown location/term as UNKNOWN; wrong officeholder is a critical failure |
| Completeness / custody | Period- and chamber-specific received/verified record count relative to an independently inventoried official source register | No source inventory → NO COMPLETENESS CLAIM; differentiate voice votes and missing positions |
| Issue-to-motion correspondence | Exact/reviewed correspondence precision and ambiguity abstention vs blinded human reference set | Cloture/amendment mistaken for final substantive stance is critical |
| Questionnaire neutrality | Upper confidence bounds on equivalent-wording distribution shift and comprehension across groups | Mere nonsignificant p-value does not certify equivalence |
| Coverage/fidelity | Exact-version directly confirmed provisions and calibrated reviewed mappings / predeclared verified corpus | Unknown, conflict, source stale and skipped visible |
| Representative workflow integrity | Replayed expected state and reject rate for unauthorized actions / predeclared injected failure set | Any false authorized/binding transition blocks pilot promotion |
| Privacy / accessibility | Identifiable preference/DOB leakage tests, role abuse, no-smartphone/assistive-tech completion | Any material leak or inaccessible required action blocks release |

**Independent criticism:** preregister the held-out or adversarial case before seeing the construction's outputs; use a separately implemented parser/join checker for historical boundaries and same-version legal sources. Exact finite synthetic enumeration only certifies tested finite cases. Source authenticity, political scientific validity, institution-specific law and actual completeness require separate external work.

## Promotion gates

G0 design accepted → G1 synthetic API and UI contract complete on one main-head build → G2 independent synthetic checker / accessibility / threat-model evidence → G3 institution grants limited advisory pilot + consent review → G4 trustworthy sourced real-record migration, staged, audited and reversible → G5 any official institutional voting separately authorized. Do not confuse G3 participation with electoral authorization.

The **cheapest decisive next experiment** is an end-to-end synthetic historical-residence/tenure/rollcall run across the integrated app on current main with an independent expected-output oracle. This exposes a currently documented integration gap at much lower cost than a real municipal import.
