# Huntsville first-pilot staged roadmap (2026–2030)

**Commonwealth OS / Product Design v1.0 — 15 November 2026**  
**Status:** Proposal / not institutionally authorized  
**Scope:** Huntsville institutional pilot; mobile-first  

## Fixed sequence — one evolving pilot

**1. UAH → 2. Other Huntsville universities → 3. Huntsville City Schools public high schools → 4. Huntsville HOAs → 5. Huntsville City Council.** These stages are sequential adoption gates, not independent site deployments; however, each institution retains independent legal/charter authority. No partner has committed to participation.

| Phase (target) | Institution | Initial authority boundary | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 design / 2027 operation | UAH SGA | University charter/bylaws; only those procedures formally approved | Rulebook verification, representative workflow, authorized motions/receipts and external audit |
| 2027–2028 | Alabama A&M and Oakwood (candidates) | Institution-specific student governance | Rule portability, tenant separation, comparable adjudication and accessibility |
| 2028 | Huntsville City Schools high schools (district approval) | Student council and educational governance only | Child privacy review, accessibility, age-appropriate usability and verified permissions |
| 2029 | Huntsville HOAs (partners TBD) | Association covenants/bylaws and Alabama entity law | Membership/quorum/proxy/weights test, legal review, actual decision evidence |
| 2029–2030 | Huntsville City Council | City law and council rules; resident views initially advisory | Clerk source integration, independently audited authority chain and authorized municipal approval |

## UAH initial research instrument

Do not force the public federal legal questionnaire into an SGA representative workspace. At UAH, validate legislative practice using student government bylaws and an institution-specific proposal/ballot flow. The private adult Living Voter local→federal instrument is a **parallel design stream**, not a precondition for executing student votes.

## Partners and approval dossier

For each stage collect: accountable sponsor; exact governing document versions; role and eligibility list source; permitted action matrix; institution data protection agreement; protocol for consent and IRB determination when research is involved; clerk/secretary review; technical/security review; independent observer; remediation, termination and export provisions.

## City legal boundary

State law authorizes municipalities to legislate through prescribed council processes; do not infer that an app may legally replace elected council members or hold binding general-population votes. Council documents, meeting records and legal counsel are required before any official decision-mode switch. An advisory participation program and lawful legislative voting are different legal uses.

## Pilot methodology

Each stage runs (1) shadow mode for at least a defined sample of historical decisions; (2) supervised side-by-side recording with official procedure as authority; (3) opt-in limited live mode only where authorized; (4) holdout and audit; (5) postmortem before next expansion. Counts and durations are proposed in the preregistered evaluation, not preapproved.

## Failure and rollback

Any contested election-integrity result, unexplained authorization bypass, privacy leak, minority-exclusion pattern or failed audit causes the affected capability to revert to advisory/read-only until resolution. Do not graduate to next institution simply because usage grows.

## Suggested artifacts at each transition

- Approved charter or board resolution;
- institution-specific role and legal-rule annex;
- independent proof-passport replay report;
- privacy/accessibility record;
- incident/corrective-action ledger;
- transferability matrix showing changes required by next institution.
