## Verification policy

Consulted 15 November 2026. URLs below point to first-party documentation or authoritative public records when available. The fact that a feature is documented does not prove a specific deployment uses it; lack of an observed feature is not evidence of absence. Prior conversations supplied the comparison shortlist and the design-gap hypothesis; the selected sources below were checked for this package. Product names belong to their owners. This pack does **not** incorporate competitor source code or claim ownership of their designs.
