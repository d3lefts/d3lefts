# Commonwealth OS — Product Design Package v1.0

**Prepared:** 15 November 2026  
**Nature:** Product architecture and research specifications; not a deployed voting system, legal approval or validated scientific result.

## What this package contains

- `Commonwealth_OS_Design_Dossier.pdf`: consolidated, formatted design specification.
- `docs/01–14`: editable, source-attributed detailed product design documents.
- `prototype/living_voter_dashboard_prototype.html`: earlier interactive *fictional* Living Voter demo; open locally in a browser. It does **not** collect identity or submit votes.
- `requirements/`: requirements register, acceptance criteria and journey traceability.
- `schemas/`: illustrative JSON Schemas (law/version, preference, Proof Passport).
- `flows/`: Mermaid source for core workflows and institutional authorization chain.
- `examples/`: clearly fictional data; no real people's votes or legislative opinions.
- `MANIFEST.json`: SHA-256 digests for every included file (except the manifest itself).

## Main strategic thesis

**Decision-state continuity:** institutional charter/rule → authoritative law or proposal version → human participation and lawful authority → verified outcome → authorized effect → updated personal/public evidence. The innovation is a hypothesis under independent review; antecedents are acknowledged (notably Decidim, CONSUL, Loomio, Polis, ElectionGuard and OpenZeppelin).

## Five-stage first pilot (fixed order)

**UAH → other Huntsville universities → Huntsville City Schools high schools → Huntsville HOAs → Huntsville City Council.** Target city consideration by 2030; no institution has yet authorized this work.

## Critical distinctions

- Preference ≠ ballot. A resident's three-way response never acts as an official vote.
- “No preference” is an expressed response; “Unknown” means no warranted position.
- A many-to-one questionnaire answer is not a proof that every related ordinance is supported or opposed.
- The 96-question study design is a *proposed cap*, not demonstrated complete coverage.
- School pilots never profile minors' federal political beliefs.
- Private civic profiles are not reusable for hidden political targeting, representative automation studies or model training.
- Only an institution-approved authority mode may permit binding organizational votes; municipal binding voting requires separate law and certification.
